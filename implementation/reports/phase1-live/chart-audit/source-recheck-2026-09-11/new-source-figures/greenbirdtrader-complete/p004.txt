    Liquidity                                                             Clusters of stop-loss orders. Above an obvious high = buy-side
                                                                                    liquidity (stops of shorts). Below an obvious low = sell-side liquidity
                                                                         (stops of longs). A sweep “grabs” that liquidity.

    Failed breakout                                                        Price goes above a range high, cannot stay there, and closes/falls
                                                               back inside the range. That failure is his short.

    Failed breakdown                                                      Price goes below a range low, cannot stay there, and closes/rallies
                                                               back inside the range. That failure is his long.

   Reclaim                                                                After going outside a level, price trades back through it and holds on
                                                                    the original side.

   PDH / PDL                                                         Previous Day High / Previous Day Low. Yesterday’s session high and
                                                                          low. Extra confluence if today’s sweep also takes PDH or PDL.

   Midnight opening price / TDO / True Day Open                       Charts label this line “True Day Open.” He also says “midnight
                                                                 opening price, mark it off on your chart daily.” Same line. A 5-minute
                                                                        close back through TDO is a named confirmation (e.g. sweep Asia
                                                                           high, then 5m close below TDO = short).

   Golden pocket                                                       His 50%–61.8% Fibonacci retracement of a measured move (he
                                                                                   explicitly said: measure the NYAM move high-to-low, mark 50%, use
                                                            50–61.8% as the golden pocket / discount zone). Very common fib
                                                                    zone; he uses it as a *location*, not a standalone signal.

   Premium / Discount                                                       In an up-move, above 50% of the range is premium (expensive);
                                                               below 50% is discount (cheap). He wants shorts from premium and
                                                                      longs from discount when bias agrees.

   Bias                                                            Higher-timeframe lean: is the day/session more likely up or down?
                                                                                  Built from structure (e.g. PDL swept and reclaimed overnight →
                                                                                bullish into NYAM).

   Confluence                                                    Stacked reasons. “Everything in trading is a matter of confluence.
                                                                      You’re always scoring and rating a setup. More confluence = higher
                                                                               rating.”

  A+ vs B+                                        A+ includes a sweep of the range. He told a follower a setup was not
                                                 A+ *specifically because there was no sweep*. B+ setups are “not
                                                                easy to trade.”

    Invalidation                                                The price that proves the idea wrong. Usually beyond the sweep
                                                              extreme (above the swept high for a short; below the swept low for a
                                                                              long). Stop goes there.

    Partial / runner                                              Take some contracts off at a target; leave some on (“runners”) for
                                                                    the bigger move. After first target, stop goes to breakeven.

   Base hit                                                             Small, clean win (he cites 20–30 point scalps on non-A+ days)
                                                                       instead of swinging for a 100+ point “banger.”

   Copy-traded / fleet                                      One execution copied across many funded accounts. His posted
                                                                               dollar P&L is often combined across the fleet, not one account.

   Tradesyncer                                               The copy-trading “cockpit” he uses (flatten-all / kill switch when
                                                                      done).

   SMT                                                       Smart Money Technique / intermarket divergence. He said he is “not
                                                                        big on that SMT train” and did not find an edge.

   Order flow (DOM, footprint)                                He is skeptical for retail: he has “yet to see a proven documented
                                                                        strategy for a retail trader that’s profitable using order flow.” He
                                                                      trades time + price + location on the chart.

  NWOG                                            New Week Opening Gap (also “Weekly Opening Gap”). The gap
                                                                    created when Sunday Globex opens vs Friday’s close. He treats
                                                                                 unfilled NWOG as a magnet / primary target. “Markets have a
                                                                tendency to rebalance inefficiencies.” One Monday short: sweep
                                                                   midnight → fail → close the whole trade when price hits NWOG.
                                             NWOG prints Sunday ~6:00 PM ET.

   London high / low                                               Another session edge. On a textbook short he stacked: sweep of
                                                     PDH + Asia high + London high, then failed breakout, then bearish
                                                                          structure shift, target = NWOG.

   DLL                                                                  Daily Loss Limit on funded accounts. Hard stop for that account for
                                                                    the rest of the day. He has blown 20 Apex accounts into DLL in one
                                                              morning and still calls DLL a feature, not a bug: it stops the spiral.

   Bodies vs wicks                                              “The bodies tell the story. The wicks do the damage.” He does not
                                                                    get shaken out by stop-hunt wicks if the candle bodies still support
                                                                    the idea. A wickless bottom he called “super sus” (suspicious —
                                                                                     likely absorption / unfinished business, not a clean sweep).

   Scratch                                              A trade exited near breakeven on purpose. Pre-FOMC example: 5
                                                                      trades = 3 scalps + 2 scratches.
