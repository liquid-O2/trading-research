3.3 The long (failed breakdown)

Exact mirror.

1. Wait for the range to exist (after 10 AM for NYAM).

2. Price sweeps the range lows.

3. Price fails to stay below them.

4. Price reclaims the range.

5. Extra confluence example he posted: the sweep also took previous day’s low.

6. Enter long on the failed breakdown, not because “it dipped.”

7. First target: range highs.

8. Partials, stop to breakeven, runners.

3.4 What is *not* a signal

• A sweep alone. “A sweep alone isn’t my signal. I’m watching what happens AFTER it. Does price hold above the Asia
highs, or fail back inside?”

• “Price is high” or “price is low.”

• Predicting the next direction of NQ.

• Indicators, Discord calls, SMT, retail order flow.

   Grade                                                 What he has actually required

                                                                                tight invalidation. He has called the stacked version “structure +
                                                                                    liquidity + retracement + failure.”

   Bonus confluence (raises the grade)                               Simultaneous sweep of PDH or PDL; close beyond midnight open /

                                                                        already-bearish or already-bullish day.

4. Second model: Golden Pocket continuation / reaction

Same philosophy (location → wait for failure → execute), different measuring stick.

How he defines the zone

  “Measure the NYAM move from high to low, mark off the 50% and there is your discount zone. I use the 50%–61.8% fib
  retracement as my golden pocket zone, pretty common.”

So:

1. Identify a completed impulse (e.g. NYAM high-to-low on a bearish day, or a larger HTF swing).

2. Draw Fibonacci from the start of that impulse to the end.

3. The 50–61.8% retracement is the pocket.

4. On a bearish day that pocket is a short location (retracement into premium/mid).
