7. Entry, stop, target, management — the mechanical rules

Pulled only from things he has written or shown on tickets.

Entry

• After failure + reclaim, not during the sweep.

• Preferred confirmation: 5-minute close back inside / back beyond TDO or PDL.

• Then a small wait (“waited a few points”) rather than chasing the close tick.

• He strikes when the A+ picture is there. “When you see your setup, you gotta act. No hesitation.”

• He does not need the market to make a brand-new high for a short if the failure already happened at the level (he
challenged a comment with “How? It never made a new high?” — meaning the *hold* above matters more than a literal
new high label).

Stop / invalidation

• Beyond the sweep extreme or beyond the zone.

• Examples from posts: stop right above PDL on a PDL-sweep short; “clearly defined invalidation above the zone” on a
golden-pocket short.

• Screenshot tickets (illustrative, not a fixed formula):

- Long after NYAM low sweep: stop ~32.5 points below, first targets stacked above, R:R shown ~6.7.

- Short after 9–10 high sweep: stop ~34.5 points above, targets down toward PDL, R:R shown ~8.7.

- Golden-pocket / PDL short: stop ~49.75 points above PDL, R:R shown ~4.2.

• After first objective pays: stop to breakeven. “Risk removed.” “From that point on I had already guaranteed myself a
green trade no matter what happened next.”

Targets

• First objective = opposite side of the range you used.

- Short the 9–10 high failure → first target is the 9–10 low.

- Long the 9–10 low failure → first target is the 9–10 high.

- Asia high failure → target Asia low.

• Then runners toward next liquidity (session low/high, PDL, measured extension).

• Partials every 25 points (stated on the Sep 8 NYAM long).

• He will take partials *instead of* waiting for the entire theoretical move. “I took partials rather than waiting for the
entire move.”

• Limit orders on screenshots are often parked in a ladder (~25–40 point spacing), matching the 25-point partial rule.

Position sizing and “pressing”

• A+ day: full conviction, can look for 100–200 point swings, runners on.

• No A+ / messy day: stay small, 20–30 point scalps, no reason to size up. “Green is green.”

• Winners: he learned to add to winners (average up, not down). “I don’t average down. I average up.”

• After the plan is paid: flatten / lock accounts. Kill switch in Tradesyncer. “There comes a point where enough is
enough.”

• Combined-fleet P&L numbers are marketing-noisy. For *rules*, ignore the dollar figure; keep the structure.
