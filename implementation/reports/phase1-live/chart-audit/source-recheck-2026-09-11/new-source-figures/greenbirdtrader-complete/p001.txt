0. Canonical definitions

Every term is tagged:

• HIS WORDS — he typed it.

• HIS CHART — read off a labeled screenshot (True Day Open, AS.L, LO.L, NWOG, session boxes).

• INFERRED — not written by him; taken from where his boxes sit on the time axis. Not a tweeted law.

All clocks are US Eastern. His TradingView is ET.

Sessions and times

True Day Open (TDO) / midnight opening price — HIS WORDS + HIS CHART

He corrected a commenter: “No, midnight opening price.” Also: “Midnight opening price, mark it off on your chart daily”
and “Right at Midnight TDO.” The chart label is True Day Open.

Definition: first print at 00:00 ET. One line. Confirmation he named: 5-minute close back through it after a sweep.

NYAM range — HIS WORDS

9:00–10:00 AM ET. “I wait until after 10AM. The 9 to 10AM range is established. No guessing beforehand.”

Cash open is 9:30. He also uses 9:30 manipulation (sweep below the open, reclaim, long toward discount, stops at those
lows) as a *separate* trigger. Do not take the 9–10 failed-break idea at 9:45 — he called that early.

Previous-hour range — HIS WORDS + HIS CHART

The last completed 60 minutes. “Previous-hour highs get swept.” On 2-minute screenshots the grey/green box is that
hour.

Asia range — HIS WORDS for meaning, HIS CHART for the clock

He never tweeted “Asia is X to Y.” He did say: “Asia session has a high and a low” and “Asia highs and lows are the
edges of the table.”

Painted pink box labeled Asia, low tagged AS.L. On the 15-minute mobile chart (3 Sep) the axis is 6:00 PM — 9:00 PM —
midnight (date change) — 3:00 AM — 6:00 AM. The pink box starts near 8:00 PM ET and ends at or just after midnight /
TDO. After midnight the box is finished; the trade is sweep-and-fail of that finished box, often with a close back through
TDO.

Copy-his-chart definition: Asia High / Asia Low = high and low of roughly 20:00–00:00 ET.
