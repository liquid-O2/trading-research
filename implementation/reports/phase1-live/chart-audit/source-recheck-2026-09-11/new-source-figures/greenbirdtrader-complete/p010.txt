9. Psychology and operating rules (he repeats these as much as the setup)

These are part of the model, not decoration.

1. One model. “Stop collecting strategies. Master one.” “I don’t wake up looking for trades. I wake up looking for *my*
trade. If it appears, I strike. If it doesn’t, I wait.”

2. Process over prediction. Screenshots > opinions. “The screenshots tell the entire story.”

3. Survive to the A+. Three stops in the morning do not invalidate the fourth setup. “Take the papercuts. Protect the
capital.”

4. Boredom is the enemy. The dangerous day is the green-but-ugly day when tilt wants more size.

5. Done means done. Flatten all. Lock accounts. Don’t look until the next session.

6. Base hits scale. “You do not need to trade all day. You do not need to catch every move.” A few clean trades + copy
size = the big dollar screenshots.

7. Document losers too. “Every trade documented. Good, bad, or ugly.”

8. Calm under pressure is the brand because his other job (aviation / EMS) is the metaphor: slow is smooth, smooth is
fast.

9. No heroics on news. Volatility is a gift only if the level and failure already exist.

10. Teach recognition, not calls. “The goal is for you to recognize the next one yourself.”
