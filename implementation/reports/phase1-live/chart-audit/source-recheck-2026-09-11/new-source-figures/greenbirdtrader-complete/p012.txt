15. How to study his screenshots (low-contrast charts)

His charts are the syllabus. When you open one, read in this order:

1. What box is shaded? (9–10 NY, Asia, previous hour)

2. Which side was wicked through? That is the sweep.

3. Did the body close back inside? That is the failure.

4. Where is the red stop box? That is invalidation.

5. Where is the first green target? Almost always the other side of the box or PDL/PDH.

6. Are there stacked limits every ~25 points? That is management.

7. Is there a fib 50–61.8 band? Then it is the golden-pocket variant, not a different strategy.

8. Is PDH/PDL/midnight drawn through the sweep? Extra confluence, same trade.

If you cannot read a washed-out screenshot, those eight questions still decode it.




16. What the screenshots add that the captions hide

Read off the actual chart images, not the P&L cards.

Chart anatomy (repeated across months)

• Product: MNQU (Micro Nasdaq), TradingView, often 1m or 5m.

• Session paint: green/grey “Green Bird” box on the 9:00–10:00 (or prior hour) window; a separate pink “Asia” box
overnight.

• Horizontal lines labeled in the UI: True Day Open, PDH, PDL, AS.L (Asia low), sometimes THURSDAY session separators.

• Position ticket: red stop box beyond the sweep, green target box toward the opposite edge or PDL/NWOG, R:R often
printed 4R–8R.

• Stops seen on tickets: ~17 pts (tight Asia short above TDO), ~32–35 pts (NYAM), ~50 pts (PDL sweep short). Not a
formula — structure.

• Partial ladder: sell/buy limits stacked ~25–40 points apart.

• Mobile chart (Sep 9 “cleaner than this”): 5-minute MNQ. Price swept a morning high near True Day Open, failed, sold
down through the NY box toward PDL. Same model on a phone.

Hidden rules visible only when you look at the picture

1. TDO is drawn as a dotted/solid price line through the session, not just mentioned. Failures that close back through
TDO on the 5m are the ones he screenshots.

2. Asia box is a full overnight rectangle with AS.L marked at the bottom. Shorts after sweeping the top of that box aim at
AS.L.

3. He will take the short even if the wick never makes a dramatic “new high” label — he argued with a commenter on
this. The failure relative to TDO / range matters more than a new-high sticker.
