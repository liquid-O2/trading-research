Premium / discount reminder

5. Context lines he wants on the chart every day

Mark these before the session. He treats them as “watch the magic” levels, not automatic entries.

1. Previous Day High (PDH)

2. Previous Day Low (PDL)

3. Midnight opening price / TDO — “Mark it off on your chart daily.”

4. Asia high and Asia low (for overnight / next morning context)

5. 9:00–10:00 AM ET high and low once that hour has closed

6. Optional: previous hour high/low if you are trading later hours

7. Optional: event boxes he has drawn live (CPI high / CPI low)

8. Fib 50% and 61.8% of the session or impulse you are using

A reclaim of PDL overnight is, in his words, “one of the cleanest Green Bird entry models” and can create the next day’s
bias (get long overnight, buy pullbacks in NYAM).
