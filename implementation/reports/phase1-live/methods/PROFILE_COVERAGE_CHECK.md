# Profile coverage and source interpretation check

Checked September 12, 2026, against the live wiki, FORMULAS, implementation, retained profile caches and the original figures. This is a coverage audit, not a new strategy experiment.

**The requested profile concepts are already in the distilled documentation. Their complete implementation is not.** Calling all of this missing source information was incorrect. A passing arithmetic fixture or a plotted native histogram does not establish that the complete set of source profiles has been recreated.

## What the original figures establish

The [Jumbo raw archive](/workspace/sources/x-raw-2026-09-11/JJumboFX_Raw_X_Archive_v2.pdf) p.4, June 12 post `2065444190557761675`, visibly contains several separate session profiles, each with its own volume shape and reference lines. Those are not one merged composite. The picture alone does not establish that every left-hand profile is overnight or that every prior profile uses the same RTH/ETH window. On p.40, July 16 reply `2077828415923581206` explicitly identifies RTH session volume and delta profiles; the associated p.41 figure shows both. The July 10 example on p.42 separately discusses the RTH open relative to previous-day value/range.

![Jumbo source figure with separate session profiles](/workspace/implementation/reports/phase1-live/methods/charts/profile-audit-jumbo-multiple.png)

[Mastering AMT and volume profile](/workspace/sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf) p.14 explicitly defines the illustrated overnight profile as **18:00 on the previous day through 09:30 New York**, and discusses its LVN/shelf and older POC alignment. Pages 15–16 distinguish the ETH and RTH profile references, including H/L, VAH/VAL, POC and the separately defined midpoint MPOC. **This clock is already distilled in FORMULAS O011.** O073 and its wiki page cite the source but do not carry the specific clock or an explicit binding to O011 into their procedure. The gap is a cross-reference/profile binding, not an unpublished setting or a fact missing from the entire documentation. It must remain scoped to this Sires example, rather than becoming a universal clock for every author or every ETH label.

![Sires source page specifying the overnight profile window](/workspace/implementation/reports/phase1-live/methods/charts/profile-audit-sires-overnight.png)

The value-area contract already preserves the distinct 70%, Sires 40% intraday and Saint 68% settings. One global 70% profile would therefore fail the existing documentation. A source-exact VA also needs the price rows, bin grid, expansion and tie convention; a value percentage alone does not determine its boundaries.

## Mechanical findings

The [recorded diagnostics](/workspace/implementation/reports/phase1-live/methods/charts/profile-coverage-audit.json) distinguish software fixtures from the one raw-data check:

- **O077 loses price structure.** Its own two-price example, 100 with buy 7/sell 4 and 101 with buy 2/sell 5, returns total 18 and delta 0. The required price rows with delta +3 and −3 are absent. The sign convention in this object is correct; the missing output is the issue.
- **O063 loses snapshot contents.** A supplied snapshot containing bins, VAH/VAL and an ID returns just POC, its time and the mutation flag. This falls short of the already written output contract.
- **Legacy VA implementations differ.** The same synthetic histogram at 100/100.25/100.50 with volumes 4/10/4 gives VA [100.25, 100.50] in `family_open.value_area` and [100, 100.25] in `mbp1_objects.vp_rth`. Both have POC 100.25. These opposite tie choices cannot both be assumed to match an unspecified platform configuration.
- **A retained legacy delta helper reverses the native sides.** [family_value.py](/workspace/implementation/src/trading_research/research/phase1_live/family_value.py:78) treats A as buying, B as selling and unknown sides as selling. The canonical native convention is B buying, A selling, N unknown. For June 12, 2026, 09:30–16:00 New York, instrument 42004058, 224,400 acquired trades contain buy volume 148,593, sell volume 146,402 and unknown volume 46. The known-side delta maximum/minimum prices are 29689/29673; the old scalar cache stores them in the opposite order. The known signed contribution is +2191, while full delta remains uncertain in [2145, 2237]. This complete-session diagnostic is not a reconstruction of the earlier intraday source screenshot.
- **Legacy full-RTH output is not a developing snapshot.** [mbp1_objects.py](/workspace/implementation/src/trading_research/research/phase1_live/mbp1_objects.py:520) calls `vp_rth` through RTH end, then retains scalar levels. It cannot establish the profile visible during a morning decision.

These findings do not alter the previous empty historical method cohorts or establish a trading result. No code or cache repair is claimed by this audit. The earlier five-date native-profile arithmetic checks remain useful input checks; they did not validate every author's profile configuration or reconstruct the full collection of profile panels.

## Corrections to the previous interpretation and phase boundary

**Green Bird's November 20 entry:** the [original p.43 image](/workspace/implementation/reports/phase1-live/methods/charts/M02-source-p43.png) shows a short-position line at **25301.75 in the high-sweep area**. The later annotation says MSS/FVG for entry risk, but the displayed position must not be reconstructed as requiring a later MSS and completed FVG before entry. The user's correction is incorporated here: preserve the entry at the sweep for this case; treat later structure as subsequent context. The screenshot does not supply a complete execution timestamp/history, and its displayed MNQ position is not an actual NQ fill record. The earlier `source-fit-v1` delayed-entry experiment remains a rejected explanation of this trade; its lack of a fill is not evidence that the author waited for MSS.

Timeframe belongs to each source case. This particular figure uses two-minute bars; other Green Bird figures use other timeframes. There is no global two-minute requirement. Further HL2 `(H+L)/2`, HLC3 `(H+L+C)/3`, OHLC4 `(O+H+L+C)/4`, timeframe and entry-timing searches are deferred to the user's later research phase. No further parameter search was performed in this audit.

**Stoic:** the Data Engine workflow is a research process, not a standalone entry strategy. Its catalog entry provides traceability; it should not be counted as a separate trade setup. Applying a consistent observation/trade journal to our own methods is the useful part. Macro work remains deprioritized.

For complete profile coverage, the existing contracts require a separate dated identity for each displayed prior RTH, prior ETH, overnight, current RTH or selected-range profile, with its own window, instrument, native price histogram, signed rows, source VA/POC settings and decision-time availability. Older profiles must stay fixed while the current profile develops. The next implementation work should follow that checklist; source rereads should resolve specific omissions or conflicts, rather than restart the distillation.
