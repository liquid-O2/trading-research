## Corrections before freezing

1. **M03 VWAP endpoint:** remove the proposed additional close-at/above-VWAP condition. FORMULAS M03 explicitly requires a later retest whose low/high straddles the frozen pre-retest VWAP and expressly disallows an invented defended-retake condition. The completed breakout candle must precede the retest observation. Asia/London clocks, 5-minute breakout bars and HLC3/18:00 reset remain comparison assumptions, not verified author settings. The opportunity denominator is breakouts above both completed highs; the endpoint measures later touch, not profitable entry, defense or fill.
2. **M01 extension geometry:** the proposed H+1.33W/H+1.66W and lower mirrors are supported for the explicitly identified beyond-edge convention (wiki `extensions-1-33-1-66.md`). Do not silently change these to H+0.33W/H+0.66W. The comparison must declare outer 06:00–09:00 parent range. It does not reconstruct the modern source's potentially distinct inner-span parent, EV range or P-zone.
3. **M02 MSS/FVG:** `bar3.low > bar1.high` is a wick-to-wick price gap, not a body gap. A close through the preceding two-bar high/low is a deterministic comparison break, not an inferred author pivot. Parent population is completed reclaim opportunities, separately counted from initial sweeps; retain parent IDs. The November 20 sweep entry remains earlier than its later MSS annotations. A later refinement can neither admit nor retime that source entry.
4. **M06 names and missing source conjuncts:** the proposed failed-auction comparison is a prior-RTH-boundary return to POC. Actual Saint failed auction requires distinct older-value and original-balance identities, rejected exploration and later reacceptance. Similarly, one positive-delta upper excursion does not establish the source's two distinct prior failed-buying episodes. Report the simplified observable endpoint by its literal name; keep those source predicates unknown. Source POC traversal follows original-balance reacceptance; a generic POC crossing comparison must disclose that absent antecedent.
5. **M05 VWAP band snapshots:** the prior-bar band is frozen at each initial touch. A later moving band cannot retroactively replace it for response scoring. An observed reentry into that fixed band is not absorption, ladder confirmation, gamma permission or an actual Sires selection.

## Buildable omissions to resolve explicitly

**M01 internal rotation:** missing proprietary EV construction alone cannot justify unavailability of every internal-location observation. FORMULAS permits an identified internal *or* EV band. A separately named outer-range midpoint-contact observation is defensible: complete 06:00–09:00 range; midpoint known at 09:00; first later contact after a prior complete bar lies strictly on one side; freeze that approach side; observe first later contact with either outer edge by expiry. Keep approach-side edge versus opposite edge as distinct outcomes, resolve same-bar double contact as ambiguous, and retain no-edge completed horizons separately. This measures midpoint-to-edge paths, not source rotation context or entry. Do not replace a source EV operand with midpoint geometry. If the lead declines this comparison, the reason should explain scope/meaning, not implementation difficulty.

**M08 opening state:** an A-period-above-prior-value observation is buildable using an explicitly named research profile construction. Use the previous complete same-contract RTH trade-volume profile, frozen fraction/row/tie policy, and the complete 09:30–10:00 A-period low. Count every eligible A period before classifying `A_low > prior_VAH`; do not select only opening-above outcomes and then present their frequency over all opens. A generic 70% comparison is an assumption, not a verified Keani setting (whose value engine is unknown); Saint's 68% and Sires's 40% must not be imported as Keani source facts. This observation does not establish developing-value migration, same-imbalance-band defense, DOM support or an entry. It can be reported separately even when the full setup is unavailable.

Neither observation licenses inventing discretionary selections. Other missing qualitative branch gates are not automatically buildable merely by choosing arbitrary thresholds. In particular, the source microbalance wiki expressly rejects substituting a fixed clock box or universal run detector for the selected source balance. M07 requires independent prior-reaction and minor-HVN selection, not a prior high plus the first profile node. Those limitations are precise and legitimate.

## Population and causal controls

- Initial opportunity records must exist at the trigger even when later confirmation never occurs. Endpoint-qualified records form a nested selected-signal population; attempts/orders/fills remain absent without actual ledgers.
- No endpoint success is required to form a denominator. A complete observed horizon with no endpoint is a comparison failure, while missing future coverage is unknown/censored. POC traversal's proposed three-state endpoint must explicitly define observed no-two-close cases; do not silently discard them.
- A 5-minute aggregate is known at its actual availability/close, not its opening timestamp. A crossing within that bar and its closing reclaim can be causally ordered only with supporting event membership; otherwise preserve the stricter later-bar comparison or ambiguity rule.
- Same-contract prior-session references must come from the chronologically preceding eligible session, not an arbitrary last observed date across a data gap. Previous week/month requires trustworthy complete-period coverage; a missing holiday calendar is unknown coverage, not zero candidates.
- Freeze cash-open reference from the first qualifying event with its actual key. Do not use the 09:30 bar close as the 09:30 open. Parent event identity and native precision survive all joins.
- M09 zones become available only when the qualifying second event is observed. Freeze the initial cluster bounds or version later changes prospectively; never widen an old zone using later prints. Distinct return opportunities require the separately frozen departure/rearm condition. Endpoint displacement uses the frozen zone and aggressor convention. Executed trades alone do not reveal standing queue replenishment, private selection, order ID or fill.
- Unknown source prerequisites stay unknown even when the comparison endpoint passes. All preliminary supported comparisons remain `research_comparison` and non-faithful. Their p/f/u totals and rates cannot be presented as source-method win rates.
- For future-perturbation checks, freeze earlier candidate IDs, creation clocks, parameters and incorporated prefix fields. Outcomes of still-open opportunities may legitimately change as more events arrive; mature endpoint records must not change when only events after expiry are perturbed.
