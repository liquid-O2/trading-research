### 1.1 The strategy as the author actually states and trades it

**The object.** One time-based range per session read. NY: the 06:00–09:00 ET box ("6-9"), frozen at 09:00, with HIGH, LOW, EQ (midpoint), 25%/75% quadrants, the range OPEN (the 06:00 price) and range CLOSE, and projections beyond the edges in units of the range width W: ±0.33/±0.66 (the "mean reversal" band, drawn from December 2025), ±0.5, ±1, ±1.33/±1.66 (the "retracement/reversal" band), ±2, ±2.33/±2.66, ±2.5, ±3. Every chart labels "+0.5" at HIGH + 0.5·W and "−0.5" at LOW − 0.5·W (2025-01-28: H 21,410, L 21,258, W 152.5, −0.5 at 21,182, +0.5 at 21,486; 2025-10-14: H 24,663, L 24,497, +0.5 at 24,746, +1.33 at 24,884; 2025-10-01: H 24,805, L 24,720, +0.5 at 24,846). London: the 02:00–03:00 box traded 03:00–06:00 with identical internals (charts of 2025-10-06/07/08 and 2026-06-05). Other clocks the manual lists (20:00–20:30 Asia, 00:00–00:30, 03:00–03:30, 09:30–10:00, 10:00–10:30, 12:00–12:30, 15:00–15:30) are applications, not products; the author also runs gold from the 18:00 Sunday open with RTH range and 18:00 P-zones, and names GC 08:20 / CL 09:00 as the open analogs.

**The two cases (the author's "core sequences", 2025-10-03 diagram).**
- *Double break / Judas / mean-reversion day*: one edge is swept (blue circle), the sweep extends to the exhaustion area (the −0.33/−0.66 band, −0.5, a P-zone, an overnight or London/Asia high or low), then price reverses through the mid toward the untouched edge (red circle) and beyond. Reversal locations on these days are the ±0.33/0.66 band, ±0.5 and, after 10:00, ±1.33/1.66; the midpoint is passed through, not entered.
- *Single break / trend day*: one edge is broken, the opposite edge stays untouched, price returns to the range OPEN or the EQ/quadrant and continues; the entries are the mid, the open and the quadrants ("single break behaviour through A period, range mid provided the entry area", 2026-07-27, +124; "discard mean reversion and range double breaks when these things align", 2026-07-28, +138). The author does also take EQ trades on other days ("same framework when having a big 6-9 range > long/short the EQ", 2026-09-02; absorption at the mid on 2026-06-08).

**The classifier (decided before the open).** Inputs: range size in % of price (the SDRange+² table prints it, e.g. 0.42%); balanced versus already-purged overnight; RTH open location versus the prior RTH value area and range (pRTHVAH/pRTHVAL/pRTHH/pRTHL/POC, the MGLevels panel); which Asia/London/midnight edges still exist; sister-index relative strength; the news calendar (CPI/NFP/FOMC and 10:00 releases delay "cycle 2"). The author's own table (2026-06-08, over 3,249 days, 9–12 window): range size 0–0.3%: double break 55.5%, single-high 23.7%, single-low 20.3%; 0.3–0.5%: 48.1/25.6/25.7; 0.5–0.8%: 34.8/34.9/29.5; 0.8–1.2%: 34.0/31.2/33.0; 1.2%+: 17.8/35.5/38.5 (no break 8.3%); all: 44.8/28.0/26.1. Open location first-hour table (2026-07-28): open below VAL inside the range (n=396): high only 29.5%, low only 37.6%, both 29.8%, one side 67.2%; below VAL and below PDL (n=517): 23.6/46.2/27.3/69.8. Manual: extended overnight range → EQ/quadrant entries, targets limited to the range edges, out by 10:00; overnight already stop-hunted (relative strength between indices) → expansion from the inner levels, 09:40–09:50 becomes an add window, targets at the projections; range-bound days into news → scalp, reduced R:R; expansive days → higher projections, compound, re-enter.

**Clocks.** The 09:00–12:00 window is the "3-hour candle": cycle 1 from 09:30 to the 09:40–09:50 window (the Judas), cycle 2 from there to 12:00 (the real move). The author's 15-year study (4,537 days): highest reversal count between 09:40 and 09:50 (secondary peaks at 09:00–09:06 and 09:30), average reversal time 09:47:36 on the original range and 09:51:05 on the extended range; 86.46% of days that reach the ±0.5 extended range reverse from it; the manual's second chart shows reversal percentages above 65–86% at the 0.1/0.2/0.3/0.5 mean-reversal projections. The author enters from 09:00 (2025-10-13 sell at the HIGH at 09:03; 2026-07-27 sell at the EQ at 09:03; 2026-01-09 long at 09:32; 2026-02-24 long at 09:33 from the −0.66 band; 2026-08-28 long at 09:30–09:32) and the manual's "after the open" is superseded by practice. Extension entries come after 10:00 (10:35 on 2025-09-09, 10:15 on 2025-11-10, 10:45–11:00 on 2025-11-18). London action 03:00–06:00.

**Objectives (the author's own table, 2026-01-02).** After a LOW sweep, retrace to EQ / to range OPEN / to range HIGH: 09:00–10:00 segment 92.8% / 86.8% / 66.2% (2,043 sweeps); 10:00–11:00 85.5/76.3/51.5; 11:00–12:00 75.2/62.3/35.4. The ladder is EQ, range open, opposite edge, then +0.5 and the extension band; exits are scaled along it (2025-01-28 exit at 21,457.75 above the HIGH; 2025-10-03 exits at the 25% line; 2026-01-09 target the D-1 High; 2026-07-10 sell limit at +1.33). "Retrace to midpoint stays at 60.4%" on single-break days. Unfinished business (leftover London/Asia/RTH highs and lows, the 1.33–1.66 band into 16:00) is a target, not an entry.

**Confirmation at location.** 2/3/5-minute three-candle orderblock (candle 2 sweeps candle 1, candle 3 closes beyond candle 2; stop at the OB midpoint (aggressive) or the OB low (conservative)); rejection block (the close of the next candle beyond the sweep candle confirms; stop at the RB midpoint); absorption candle at the level (Absorption Zone+: body ≤ 0.6 of range, volume ≥ 1.5× the 14-period average, imbalance inside the candle); BigTrades prints ≥ 100 lots in NY / ≥ 75 in London ("anything below 100 is noise"); footprint highlighting the top 35% of transactions; the RTH volume and delta profile (absorption at the mid HVN, taper at the extreme). One of these alone is not a trade; the stack is level + time + signature.

**Levels drawn every day.** The box with quadrants, open and close; the projections; London and Asia highs/lows from liquidity sessions 02:00–07:05 and 18:00–00:05 (deleted when purged); D-1/D-2/D-3 highs and lows; prior RTH high/low/VAH/VAL/POC; the 15-minute opening range with H/75%/mid/25%/L (secondary; "OR mid retracement" entries on 2026-02-24, 2026-07-16, 2026-07-21); overnight high/low; the SessionStat envelope (60-session mean and median high/low excursions of the 09:00–12:00 or RTH or 4-hour London window from the session open, with 0.5 expansions and "minimum average" levels); EVRange (an AM expected-move envelope around the open with a +50% line; proprietary); P-zones (proprietary: percentile distance bands from the session open learned over 500 sessions, volume- and volatility-filtered, tiers T1/T2 and T3/T4, "delete invalidated zones"; anchors 09:00, 10:00 or 09:50, 02:00, 15:30 and 18:00 for gold; used as both entry and target).

**Sizing and frequency.** Clips of 5–10 MNQ or 1–3 NQ; one thesis per session; typical days show 1 to 4 round trips (2025-10-13: two fills; 2026-01-09: two longs; 2026-06-12: two round trips; 2026-08-28: four), scalping-mode days show 13–19 fills at the range lines (2026-07-21, 2026-09-01), "one and done" days exist (2026-07-16, 2026-07-27, 2026-01-02). Scaling in at the lines and out at the next line; trailing sell stops; front-running the target at a mid rejection; flat by 10:00 on A+ days; lunch and "daily target" grinding are the named leaks; after a failed idea the author flips.

**Failure rules (manual §9).** No rejection signature at the projection in the window, continuous momentum through the level, extended bodies instead of wicks, reversal well before or after the window (10:00 news), three attempts at a level without follow-through; then exit and observe, switch model, or cut allocation by at least 50%.

### 1.3 Defects, ordered by impact on the Phase 1.5 results

1. **J8 (other_session)**: the London branch demands a sweep of the box edge before any entry and accepts only the 3-minute OB. The author's London entries on 2025-10-07 and 2025-10-08 came at the 25% line with no edge sweep; 2/3/5-minute OB or RB all qualify. This is why the branch produces 12,918 failures and 162 passes. Correct: entries at the quadrant/EQ/edge/−0.5/extension with a contact (touch or sweep) and any of the four signatures; record whether the edge was swept as an operand, do not require it.
2. **J1 (judas_reversal objective)**: the objective is EQ, mislabelled `plus_0_5`; the author's ladder is EQ (92.8%), range open (86.8%), opposite edge (66.2%), then +0.5 and the extension; his exits scale along it. Correct: record the whole ladder in the objective stage; P&L in B0.2 at the first objective (EQ) is one legitimate reading, but the exit study must run the ladder with scale-outs and the trailing stop, and the family report must print outcomes per ladder rung. Also make `judas_outbound`'s `plus_0_5` (high + 0.5·W) and this label consistent.
3. **J2 (confirmation clock)**: `_scan_judas_reversal` searches the signature only from 09:40; the author enters from 09:00 (09:03 on 2025-10-13 and 2026-07-27, 09:32 on 2026-01-09, 09:33 on 2026-02-24, 09:30–09:32 on 2026-08-28). 4. **J11/J10/J12 (single_purged)**: the window is 09:40–09:50 only, the label admits only opens below value, and the objective is the range edge. Source: expansion from the inner levels during the AM (entries from 09:00/09:30), both directions by the purge and the open location, targets at the projections (−1, −1.33/−1.66), 09:40–09:50 as the add window. Correct all three.

### 2.1 The strategy as the author actually states and trades it

**The object.** "Session highs. Session lows. Breakouts. Fakeouts. I know my range. Then I watch how price behaves at its edges. Break out and hold? I'm looking for continuation. Sweep a level and fail back inside? I'm looking for the reversal. For longs, I want a pullback into discount." (2026-09-15). **The entries.**
1. *Sweep and fail* (the "ONE MODEL"): a drawn edge is taken by a wick, price fails to hold beyond it and comes back inside; entry on the failure. Two named triggers: the 5-minute close back through the level ("I wait for the 5 min close back below the PDL after sweeping above it… once closed below, low risk entry on any retracement with stops above PDL", p.3; 09-14 London long: "After the open, price closes back above the London low. That's my entry"), or a limit at the level once the failure is visible (09-15 short filled at 29,441.50 on the 10:05 retest of the 09:00–10:00 high 29,450; 09-14 short 29,081.50 on the 10:20 retest of the swept Asia high; 09-10 sell limit at the 10:00–11:00 high; 08-31 short 29,510.50 on the 09:33 spike). The 09:00–10:00 box is not traded before 10:00; the 9:30 manipulation is a separate open reaction ("9:30am manipulation below, reclaim, enter for longs targeting retracement into discount, stops at lows", p.3).
2. *Continuation after a held breakout*: break and close beyond the range, hold, pullback into the golden pocket or to VWAP, enter with the move (2026-02-24: "Broke & closed above London + Asia highs → retraced into VWAP → long entry. 30-point stop. Continuation play"; "I only look at VWAP when I'm considering a Continuation trade"; 09-10 long 29,222 on the retest of the broken 09:00–10:00 high 29,200; 09-11 long 29,382 in the pocket of the 08:30 CPI leg 29,213 → 29,500, stop 29,347 at the NY low under the pocket, sell limits 29,494.50 and 29,521.75 at the range high / PDH).
3. *Overnight structure sets the bias*: a PDL sweep-and-reclaim overnight ("Bias from overnight PDL sweep and reclaim. Long overnight. NYAM: buy pullbacks", 07-14; 09-11 first long), an Asia-high sweep after midnight (09-03 about 01:15, 09-08 about 02:00) or during NY (09-14 10:20).

**Bias and context.** A higher-timeframe lean ("HTF bearish", "path of least resistance"), the overnight sweep-and-reclaim, and premium/discount within the day's range ("For longs, I want a pullback into discount"). The bias is qualitative; no rule is published. He sizes down into events (09-15 ahead of FOMC) and after fast markets ("When the market speeds up, I slow down").

**What the paper publishes.** Zone = a cluster of large aggressive prints ("sixty, eighty, a hundred contracts hitting in seconds") at one price area; touch = a later return; base rates: 42% of touches hold, 175 touches a session; fade every touch = −0.285R after costs; a small model on ~20 pre-touch features in four families (memory: held earlier this session / prior-day defence; construction: print count, size, zone width; location: value-area edges, session extremes, distance from open; flow/state: delta into the touch, approach speed, balance/imbalance) grades touches at AUC 0.63 (placebo 0.51), hold rate 25% → 63% across deciles, memory + location carry the signal (flow alone AUC 0.54); execution: the median winner dips 18 ticks past the touch, a resting limit 12 ticks inside the zone with a 32-tick stop and 96-tick target, 30-minute cancel, one position, 1-tick cost + 1-tick stop slippage: +0.143R per trade, PF 1.19, 30% win rate, ~6.8 trades a day, +78R over 79 held-out sessions; independent engine +$3,803 for the year with a losing Q4; −4R daily stop adds ~14 points of evaluation pass rate. The selection model itself (features' exact definitions, the model) is not published; the mechanical entry rebuilt from scratch is negative (OFM p.18).

**Adapter versus paper.** `form_b02_zones` 345–392: prints ≥ 40 contracts clustered within 5 s and 2 ticks with total ≥ 60 (`SOURCE_CLUSTER_MIN_SIZE`), departure 4 ticks, touches after departure, hold = no print 8 ticks through the zone within 30 minutes, bracket 12/32/96/30 min. The B0.2 summary itself marks the result out of bounds: 457 touches a session (paper 175) and a 4.6% hold rate (paper 42%). Both the zone definition (too many, too small) and the hold label (8 ticks through is not the paper's unprinted cutoff) are therefore not the paper's. **Defect R1**: calibrate the zone construction and the hold label to the two published anchors (175 touches a session, 42% hold) before anything is built on the touches; report the touch density per session and the hold rate as the plausibility checks of every REFILL result card. **R2**: the selection layer (memory + location) is where the paper's edge lives; it is our own model to build in Phase 2/3, with the paper's numbers (AUC 0.63, +0.143R, 6.8 trades a day, 30% win rate) as the plausibility band.

- Every family's population count is one to two orders of magnitude above the author's trade count, and the current author-example replay detects 2 of 56 examples. The Phase 1.5 results built on these populations measure our scans, not the authors' strategies; nothing downstream of B0.2 is trusted until the replay passes on the proper entries.
