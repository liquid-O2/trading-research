Audit date: 2026-09-06 UTC. The archive supports substantial Context/Location research, including event-level futures flow and several options-chain families. It does **not** establish synchronized complete chains, historical receipt timestamps for every provider, dealer holdings, full market depth, or executable profitability. Raw sources and market data were preserved.

## Verified capabilities and design consequences

**DA-01 — Futures execution data.** QuantPad NQ MBP-1 begins in 2020; the standalone NQ trade sample starts 2021-09-01 at 18:00 UTC, despite broader catalog scope language. MBP-1 Trade actions are a possible earlier trade source. In the reconciled NQ hour, 374,830 MBP rows contain 23,509 trades, 45,670 contracts and signed volume −2,202; there were no mismatches or unknown-side contracts. The ES hour has 28,679 MBP rows and 3,525 matching trades, 16,226 contracts and signed volume +1,238; 862 contracts have unknown side and one auction quote is crossed. This supports a reconstruction experiment, not whole-archive certification. Expand reconciliation by year/session/roll and event flags before producing features.

**DA-02 — ES availability.** ES MBP-1 is a discontinuous 25-month cohort: January–October and December 2020, then July 2023–August 2024. Fair NQ-versus-ES execution comparisons must use common eligible dates first, with separate broader NQ results. Lack of ES BBO on a day is not a valid zero-spread assumption and is not proof NQ is economically superior. NQ MBP-1 has about 11.536 billion rows/82.214 GB; ES about 2.951 billion/19.867 GB according to the catalog. Stream partitions; do not load raw tape into RAM as a table.

**DA-03 — Timing and identity.** QuantPad trade/MBP samples omit receipt timestamp and exchange sequence. Preserve source file/row identity, event time and original storage ordering; simultaneous events from different streams are only partially ordered. Use measured/conservative lag scenarios until a live receipt sample exists. Native Databento futures-options trades include receipt, sequence, action and side. A sequence number alone is not a globally unique event: sampled statistics share a sequence across instruments/stat types. Timestamp, publisher/channel, instrument, type, action and payload participate in deduplication; distinct identical-looking trade prints must not be discarded merely for sharing time/price/size.

**DA-03a — MBP-1 fields and flags.** The all-167-footer check confirms that NQ/ES MBP exports retain action, reported side, price/size, best prices/sizes, instrument, timestamp and flags; separate provider receipt, sequence and order counts are absent. The newly sampled HG/NKD/SI native MBP files retain those additional fields. NQ `2021-02-08.parquet` row group 88 has a selected 4,096-row prefix with `F_MAYBE_BAD_BOOK` set, through values 4 and 132. Native prefixes contain snapshot/receipt-quality and publisher-specific flags. These require explicit quality/initialization handling. The provider already normalizes MBP; never impose an MBO-only F_LAST trade filter. In the selected 86,016 records, 7,833 of 7,878 trades lack that bit. Raw data remains intact; replay/feature certification is still pending. [Binding source semantics](MARKET_DATA_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md).

**DA-04 — Quote integrity.** The bounded sample contains 18 crossed and 8,826 locked quotes and 38,144 zero-side quotes across heterogeneous datasets. These are counts in a deliberately selected sample, **not estimated population rates**. Separate auctions, closed markets, one-sided quotes and corrupt rows. A crossed opening auction is not evidence all data are bad. Future-paired Theta quotes occur in SPX 2020-01-10 (0.734 seconds ahead), SPXW 2020-01-02 (0.982 seconds) and SPY 2020-01-02 (0.951 seconds). NDXP 2023-05-02 contains a sampled quote age of 14,565.492 seconds. Rebuild an as-of quote only if prior quote data permit; otherwise classify trade sign as unknown. One-minute snapshots cannot reconstruct intervening NBBO for every print. [Theta's documented pairing](https://docs.thetadata.us/operations/option_history_trade_quote.html) requires an earlier/no-later quote and therefore does not explain away these records.

**DA-05 — Chain coverage is not full quoted-chain coverage.** Contract listings, EOD and OI are much broader than intraday quotes. The intended broad quote scope is ≤60 DTE and roughly ten strikes on each side per expiry; additional near coverage is ≤14 DTE with wider strike counts (NDX/NDXP 70, SPX/SPXW 90, QQQ 42, SPY 45). Trade quotes generally cover ≤7 DTE within corresponding strike limits. Older coverage can be substantially broader than this description. One-month NDX/SPX files can legitimately be absent from the near-expiry acquisition because no monthly expiry is within 14 days. Determine eligibility from contracts before labeling missing dates outages. Options lists are not automatically certified point-in-time listing universes.

At 15:00 UTC on 2026-09-02, valid-quote OI coverage in 15–30/31–60 DTE is about 11.0%/2.9% for NDX, 6.6%/2.6% for SPX, 6.3%/2.8% for SPXW, and 7.7%/6.6% for SPY. On 2023-05-02, sampled NDX/SPX buckets are around 85–92% while SPXW remains sparse in farther expiries. The 0DTE coverage sample is not uniformly high either. No sampled family has intraday quote coverage past 60 DTE. These fractions are relative to *reported known OI in that date's listed archive*, not unknowable true total exposure. Appendix snapshots retain every numerator/denominator. Quote duplicates arise from overlapping acquisitions; choose by provider/version/freshness with a conflict check, never sum duplicate contracts.

**DA-06 — Surface feasibility.** Build observed-near, observed-broad and estimated-wing representations with masks. Longer dated unquoted OI can support concentration summaries, but a real-time gamma magnitude for it requires an estimated volatility surface and carries model uncertainty. Do not pretend yesterday's IV is a live observation. NDX/NDXP, QQQ, SPX/SPXW and SPY each retain separate exercise/settlement/universe identity. Monthly and weekly index series cannot be merged by strike/date alone. The NDX-derived-level hypothesis receives a fair matched-coverage test, with an explicit data-upgrade dependency for a fully observed comparison.

**DA-07 — OI timing.** A date label does not establish morning availability. The selected 2020-01-02 files include OI rows timestamped after the 15:00 UTC snapshot: NDX 32, NDXP 642, QQQ 268, SPXW 92, SPY 378. They were excluded in the coverage audit. Other sampled OI files contain next-UTC-day timestamps or afternoon timestamps. Documented OI is a previous-end-of-day observation generally reported around 06:30 ET; actual receipt/publication governs use. Missing OI is not zero. Keep `position_date`, `published_at`, `received_at`, `known_at`, `revision_id` distinct. Tomorrow's reported OI is an aggregate supervised label only; expiring contracts require settlement/expiry-aware censoring and do not yield an intraday holdings path. [Theta OI documentation](https://docs.thetadata.us/operations/option_history_open_interest.html).

**DA-08 — Futures options.** Eight native DBN families provide ES/NQ option definitions, trades, statistics and minute OHLCV; each has 81 DBN files, January 2020 through 2026-09-01. They do not provide an options BBO/depth stream. Settlement/statistics can be carried forward only after publication, not from `ts_ref`. Sample definitions have null-sentinel `contract_multiplier=2147483647` and `min_price_increment=9223372036854775807`, but unit-of-measure quantities decode to 50 ES and 20 NQ. Verify the authoritative contract registry, including variable option ticks. NQ raw symbol `NQH0 C0000` has a strike field of 10,000: never parse strikes solely from display names. Parent universes contain user-defined spreads as well as outright options; one sampled negative statistic price can belong to a spread and must not be fed to an outright-option IV solver. Definition class/legs are prerequisites. Header `partial` contains many symbols on monthly files; this is a mapping-coverage signal to reconcile with instrument lifetimes, not a count of corrupt files or missing trading days.

**DA-09 — Sentinels and clock skew.** Statistics use typed int64/uint64 missing sentinels for quantity, price and reference time even when Arrow reports no nulls. Null conversion precedes arithmetic/joins. Some sampled QQQ statistics receipt times precede event time by about 0.5 ms; distinguish clock skew from causal receipt, preserve both, and carry measured clock uncertainty. Never move a decision earlier to exploit a clock inconsistency. Test large sentinels, signed spread prices, same-sequence multiple records and stale definition snapshots explicitly.

**DA-10 — Broader information universe.** The catalog appendix enumerates all acquired assets and granularities, including other futures, ETF/equity minute/daily series, VX/volatility-related data and VIX options. Availability-specific experts can use all supported families; selected execution remains NQ or ES. There is no assumption of tick-level ETF aggressor flow, a live intraday NDX/SPX cash price, full intraday VIX index or complete constituent history merely because related daily data exist. Cash indices in the free archive are daily; ETF minute prices provide a separate proxy. Cross-index event prediction is distinct from converting index/ETF strike coordinates into futures prices.

**DA-11 — Macro and earnings.** The sampled FRED historical series have `realtime_start=realtime_end=2026-09-04` even for old observations: revised/current vintages. Historical macro surprise or revision-sensitive value features are deferred until true vintages and publication times exist. The current earnings archive contains estimates/reported EPS without complete announcement-time vintages; do not make future surprises available at the opening bell. Fixed calendar/time-to-known-event indicators remain eligible only with documented historical scheduling availability. FRED explicitly distinguishes observation date from [real-time availability periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html).

**DA-12 — Features we can and cannot observe.** Traded CVD, trade-size-cohort CVD OHLC, real trade-at-price volume/delta profiles and visible best-quote OFI are feasible in relevant futures cohorts after quality gates. Size cohorts are not participant identities. MBP-1 cannot identify off-touch replenishment, queue priority, hidden iceberg inventory, broker ownership or stops. Options aggressor sign can be uncertain; contract flow, premium flow, delta-equivalent flow and gamma-weighted flow need separate units. Minute signed volume cannot reconstruct within-minute CVD extrema. No input in this archive directly observes aggregate dealer holdings.

## Resource envelope and bounded next audits

The inspected pod's effective limits are approximately 77.3 GiB RAM, 17.85 CPU quota and an NVIDIA RTX A4000 with 16 GiB GPU memory. Host-reported 128 cores/503 GiB are not pod entitlement. The large shared `/workspace` free-space display is not a personal storage quota; the temporary overlay has only about 24 GB. Confirm billing/storage allocation before materially expanding data. No paid acquisition was made.

Use a memory-capped stream (initial research cap 48 GiB process RAM), column projection, instrument/day partitions and incremental aggregates. Cache sparse chain tensors and event objects, not many duplicated raw tapes. A first transformation should time one liquid and one stressed day; derive throughput, CPU-hours and bytes/output-row before full-history approval. A provisional 1–3 TB additional feature/replay budget is a planning envelope, not measured need. A4000 can support small tabular/sequence/set experiments; large cross-chain temporal models may require a larger GPU or distributed research job, but only after simpler candidates and data pipelines pass.

Before promoting the data layer: expand stratified stream reconciliation; scan critical keys/sentinels/duplicate/gap metadata across all intended partitions; certify listing and receipt availability; quantify common NQ/ES/chain cohorts; decode outright-versus-spread DBN instruments; verify historical exchange session/settlement calendars; and collect a future read-only live/shadow receipt sample after the appropriate authorization. Missing capabilities disable only dependent experts. They do not force a small information universe or block the initial futures-only and supported-chain benchmarks.

## Third-review bounded VIX-option check

The [VIX feasibility report](review/third-review/VIX_FEASIBILITY.md) adds a completed read-only audit of 261,970 quote rows on 2020-01-02, 2023-05-02 and 2026-09-03, with nine fixed decision cuts and 18 expiry/cut combinations. Illustrative parity forwards and OTM Black IVs were computable in each case under declared rate/clock/availability assumptions. Several full pair sets were inconsistent under those assumptions; this is a quality finding, not certified arbitrage or an official VVIX reconstruction. Three accompanying OI files showed expiries beyond the directory's nominal 60-DTE label, so quote/OI scope must be audited separately. No broader archive or predictive-quality claim follows from these three dates. VIX minute quotes/OI do not supply signed VIX option trades, and distinct UX options on VX futures remain a separate acquisition dependency.
