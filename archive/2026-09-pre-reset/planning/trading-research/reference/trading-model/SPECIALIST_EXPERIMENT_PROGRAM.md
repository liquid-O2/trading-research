# Testing and improving every part of the system

This program binds the existing **153 ten-part component contracts** to **191 named refinement experiments**, each with **eight explicit phases**. The resulting **344 units and 2,752 phase records** are research specifications, not completed trading tests or a claim that 344 separate models should be deployed. Several experts may share feature kernels or a learned representation while keeping separate targets, predictions, ablations and diagnostic ownership.

Start with [how we judge model quality](MODEL_QUALITY_AND_STOPPING_RULES.md), then the [family experiment index](experiments/README.md). The binding [153 full-system definition refinements](SYSTEM_REFINEMENT.md), [153 upgrade paths with 191 individually authored child instructions](UPGRADE_PATHS.md), and [implementation constructions](UPGRADE_CONSTRUCTIONS.md) extend every unit's definition, cases, comparisons and interactions. The [volatility/IV/skew/VIX specification](VOLATILITY_RESEARCH_SPEC.md) is one family detail; [E0](E0_REFERENCE_EXPERIMENT.md) and [runtime scheduling](components/RUNTIME_SCHEDULER.md) remain shared contracts.

Preserve source-faithful computable → corrected/earlier-upgraded → further-candidate stages. Every named source alternative inside a unit must be represented and compared or have an explicit evidence-backed dependency/disposition. A parent summary or one representative child does not discharge the other mechanisms. Shared kernels reuse genuinely identical reference evidence while keeping independent target/clock/coverage and integration scorecards. No empirical upgrade is assumed successful in advance.

## Eight phases for each parent and each named refinement

| Phase | Question and deliverable | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|
| P0 definition/data/target | Exact algorithm, units, input fields, causal clocks, coverage, label and observation population; frozen reference and variant manifest | A request list or field name does not establish acquired data or identifiable truth. |
| P1 reference/causality/faults | Local numerical/sequence/edge cases, source-specific assertions, prefix deletion, revisions and restart | Correct arithmetic, causal availability and stated ambiguity precede accuracy. |
| P2 isolated quality | Source/simple/statistical/nonlinear challengers on the same targets and dates; local metrics and learning curves | Information gain, definition improvement and model-class improvement are separate experiments. |
| P3 calibration/support | OOF dependencies, held-out calibration, effective sample counts and conditional uncertainty by session/regime/age/coverage | Exact measurements do not get invented probabilistic confidence; latent states are evaluated only where observable. |
| P4 increment/interactions | No-component/simple-component, input-family, definition and matched-placebo ablations; prespecified 2×2 synergies | Standalone superiority is not compulsory for an exact service or a demonstrated joint contributor; hard position/account/integrity constraints remain in all eligible policy comparisons. |
| P5 decisions | Same-candidate scorer tests, same-scorer generator tests, actual timing and fill consequences, one-component interventions | A more accurate label forecast may still arrive too late or displace a better trade. |
| P6 integrated confirmation | Complete one-mini sequential costs/risk/account/date ledger; frozen outer comparison and objective gap | Zero-trade days, non-fills, occupied time and changing risk budgets remain in the result. |
| P7 future/operations | Fixed future inference/endpoint, captured receipts, parity, fallback, drift, restart and versioned update policy | A detailed plan or retrospective backtest is not future evidence. |

Each record in the family files names **local fixtures, distinct targets, model alternatives, metrics, ablations and decision use**. The eight generic phase names do not replace those local requirements. At implementation, each compound fixture is split into named assertions with expected output and provenance; simply creating an empty test named after a source/component fails P1. Parameter/asset/horizon variants become concrete trial IDs with their own manifests and results.

## Specialist design and model comparisons

Measurements retain their exact meaning: CVD is not replaced by a learned estimate merely to increase model count. Separate predictive heads ask what the measurement forecasts. Continuous variance, jump probability, upside/downside tails, ordered reach, node persistence, runner duration, fill probability and net action value require different targets and often different model families. We test fixed rules and shrunken frequencies, regularized linear/GAM/count/survival models, tree-based distributional models and compact sequence/set/graph models where the information structure warrants them.

For sequence questions compare finite-state prefixes and duration/hazard models with compact GRU/TCN or similar temporal challengers using identical causal observations. HMM/HSMM and prefix-only CRF variants need their actual online information constraints; full-sequence smoothing cannot serve as a real-time feature. Hawkes-style models compete with simple rolling marked intensities. Large state-space/recurrent/transformer or foundation-model candidates mentioned in earlier conversations remain optional registered challengers; architecture reputation and model size do not establish fitness, data sufficiency, permissible licensing or supported uncertainty. Verify implementation/library constraints when selecting a concrete package.

For each information family compare: separate independently trained heads; a shared backbone with separate heads; a transparent target-specific mixture; and a simpler input-matched joint model. Hold information, chronological splits and tuning/compute budgets comparable. Inspect residual correlations and ablations alongside proper scores; different architectures can still repeat the same evidence. Reusing a useful deterministic kernel across specialists does not reduce their independent evaluability.

## Search space and evidence management

Do not launch the full Cartesian product of every feature, asset, horizon, window and model. First certify a canonical reference, then perform bounded definition/information experiments, then model comparisons on promising supported targets, then selected interactions. Preserve source-faithful and simpler complete benchmarks throughout. Parameters explicitly supplied by sources remain named benchmarks; discovered thresholds/windows are fitted inside folds and frozen before confirmation.

Each concrete trial manifest contains unit/variant ID, exact input columns and hashes, information cut/scheduler, target and maturity, asset/chain/expiry/moneyness cohort, data/label/model versions, date partitions, fitted transformations, model/search budget, seed, primary metric, useful-effect and non-inferiority limits, interval/endpoint method, resource cap, baseline and complete policy hash. Results record every attempt, including failure, timeout and no useful gain. A recent known historical cohort is not relabeled as untouched future.

Each scorecard has four separately reportable states: **calculation/causality**, **isolated quality**, **incremental decision value**, and **integrated/future support**. A service may pass the first with the others inapplicable or pending. A predictive model may have excellent standalone calibration and no incremental use. A joint contributor may pass a registered interaction despite a weak isolated score. No status is inferred from the number of specification fields or tests listed here.

## Delivery and sequencing

E0 runs the minimum exact data/label/order/account chain before building all complex experts. E1 develops range/auction/P-zone/Location information; E2 options/flow/node and cross-chain information; E3 the rich physical/implied volatility family; E4 profile/cohort/cross-market and integrated selection experiments, with dependency order set by the DAG. Independent subexperiments need not wait for every family to succeed. E5 deepens Response after the requested Context/Location program; its mechanisms and tests are preserved now.

Every family produces a reproducible evidence bundle and best-supported-version decision using [quality and stopping rules](MODEL_QUALITY_AND_STOPPING_RULES.md). The [validation plan](VALIDATION_PLAN.md) governs promotion, and the [backlog](IMPLEMENTATION_BACKLOG.md) assigns implementation milestones. All production development, large training, trading and paid data work remain future tasks; the current work is the strengthened plan and bounded review evidence.
