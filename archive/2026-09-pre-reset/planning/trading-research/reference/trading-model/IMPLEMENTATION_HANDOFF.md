## Evidence ledger and completion rules

At B00 create a durable implementation ledger outside the generated planning manifests. Import every scope ID and record independently: definition reviewed, code/reference artifact, semantic/causal verification, data/cohort eligibility, each applicable experiment phase, evidence locations and disposition. Keep an append-only history; checkpoint current work, exact versions, commands/results, blockers and next eligible steps for resumption. Scope regeneration never overwrites actual status.

Use separate engineering states (`unstarted`, `in_progress`, `implemented`, `verified`), evaluation states (`unrun`, `running`, `evaluated`) and dispositions (`undecided`, `selected`, `rejected`, `inconclusive`, `dependency_blocked`, `not_applicable`) with explicit evidence and applicability. A rejected model may be fully implemented and tested; an operational invariant is accepted on fidelity rather than alpha. `not_applicable` requires a contract-based reason; it cannot replace an unwanted comparison. Every deliberate scope change carries its prior requirement, reason and dependency consequences.

Before any completion claim, compare exact scope IDs and required phases against the ledger, reconcile source-clause assertions and datasets' field/cohort eligibility, and inspect missing/blocked/inconclusive records. Report separately:

- **Engineering completion:** requested eligible modules, reference fixtures, orchestration, reports and reproducible bounded research runs are implemented and verified.
- **Research disposition:** every eligible hypothesis/comparison has required evidence supporting selected/rejected/inconclusive status; unsupported ones retain specific dependency records.
- **Economic evidence:** the complete policy's measured results and uncertainty, including all eligible days and constraints. Coding completion does not establish the $2,000 objective.
- **Outstanding future work:** prospective time, live receipt data or other unresolved dependencies, without relabeling them completed. Future observation cannot be produced in one sitting by faster compute.

All phases remain planned at this handoff. No model is selected simply because its name appears in the plan, and no family disappears because E0 or a neighboring specialist performed poorly. Use registered useful-effect, power and resource rules to limit inconclusive research; record closure and next evidence explicitly.
