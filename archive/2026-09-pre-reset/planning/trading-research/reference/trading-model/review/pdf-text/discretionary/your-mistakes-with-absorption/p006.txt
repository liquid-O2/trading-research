06 Mistake 2: not following the auction



  the same signature is a coin flip at POC and a real signal at a real extreme


  Knowing the order flow tools, the footprint, DOM, and Big Trades bubbles, is not enough on its

  own. They are all showing you roughly the same underlying information: real orders. What they

  cannot tell you is whether the price they are printing at is a place where absorption actually

  means something.


  The example: a daily volume profile with its Delta profile, VAH, POC and VAL already drawn.

  Buyers push into a top, get hit to the wall, sellers come in and the opposition looks like it is

   failing, exactly the absorption reversal signature. Price should go down. It doesn't. It comes

  back up, because the location was close to POC and balance, the area price is statistically most

   likely to chop inside rather than reject from.


  The fix is not a different read of the print. It's asking the location question before taking the

  signal at all: is this at an extreme, a shelf, a ledge, a low volume node, a minor volume node, or

   is it in the middle of balance where the signature means nothing.



  THE ABSORPTION SIGNATURE AT POC: RIGHT SHAPE, WRONG LOCATION





  Labeled "THIS IS NOT ABS" in his own notes, not because the order flow print is fake, but because the
   location makes it meaningless.
