10 Mistake 4: not waiting for your side to win



  the fastest read is Delta, not price


  The fourth mistake is really the same underlying check as the first three, waiting for

  confirmation rather than the print, but read through a different tool: Delta, rather than price

  action or CVD.


  The example he opens with is a deliberate non-example. A buyer comes in, sellers come in, it

  looks like a valid entry. But the location is POC, the single most decisive area for who should be

   in control, the area price is most likely to chop through rather than resolve from. Correct

  response: filter it out entirely, regardless of how clean the print looks.


  The real signal shows up at value area high: buyers push in, get hit to the wall, sellers come in

   for the reward. Where does that wall actually originate? Traced back, it lines up with a high Delta

  spike of buyers, the moment the opposition was visibly losing control, right before sellers

  arrived passively to absorb the rest of that aggression.
