Naked POCs and composites





Two ideas turn the profile from a single-session tool into a genuine map: naked POCs and composite profiles.

The logic is the same at every timescale. A shelf on a 5 minute profile is a scalp level, the same shelf on a composite
weekly profile is a swing level. Zoom the profile to match the trade you are hunting.





  HOW TO USE THEM

   Mark naked POCs as targets and composite HVNs as your heavyweight levels. When a naked POC lines up with
   a composite shelf, that is a level worth building a whole session around.
