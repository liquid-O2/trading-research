 Market Conditions
 (section d.)
   In this section, I will touch on the importance of market conditions and how
   they dictate the outcome of the model. Just like any other model, market
   conditions play a significant role in the trading session due to their somewhat
   predictable nature. While we cannot fully anticipate market conditions, we can
   predict their behavior and adjust our expectations and risk management
   accordingly.
   Economic Calendar
   Monitoring the economic calendar is crucial to understand where we are in the week
   with regard to limiting our participation on range-bound or increase exposure in volatile
   days. This allows us to adjust our expectations for risk, profit-taking, and overall
    participation.
