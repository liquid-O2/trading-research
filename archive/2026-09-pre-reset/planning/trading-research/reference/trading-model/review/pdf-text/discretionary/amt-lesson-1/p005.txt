The value area: POC, VAH, VAL


Inside every balance there are three prices that matter more than the rest. They are the extremes of the value area, and
they are where the auction makes its decisions.

POC               Point of Control. The price where the most trading happened, the busiest shelf in the profile. The auction
                    gravitates back to it.

VAH              Value Area High. The top boundary of the zone holding roughly 70% of the volume. The ceiling of agreement.

VAL              Value Area Low. The bottom boundary of that same 70% zone. The floor of agreement.
