PD RTH Range+

The primary purpose of this indicator is to help traders visualize the price range
established during regular trading hours, which often serves as important Key levels for
next day’s RTH session tracking and displaying the Previous day’s Regular Trading
Hours (RTH) range High and Low and just like the SDRange+, it only highlights the RTH
session when toggled on, helping visualize better the sessions of interest.

Refer to ICT One Set Up for Life for the view on RTH sessions.
I tweaked this framework to my preferences and added my own view to it as well as creating the indicator for it
