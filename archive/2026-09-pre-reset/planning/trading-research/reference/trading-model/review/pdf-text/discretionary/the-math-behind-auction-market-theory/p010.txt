    States: a usable alphabet



        five labels that replace vague adjectives


       Instead of describing an auction with vague adjectives, jetbundle uses five labels, each

       one a heuristic for judgment, not a signal for automation.

       B                     Balance                                      two-sided, quiet mid

       A                  Absorption                               effort without displacement

       D                   Discovery                                  effort with displacement

        E                  Exhaustion                              effort, then liquidity fails

     W                  Withdrawal                                          cancels dominate
