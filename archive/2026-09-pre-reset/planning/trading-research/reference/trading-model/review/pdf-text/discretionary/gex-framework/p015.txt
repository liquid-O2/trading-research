Reading it live in GEXRADAR


Use 0DTE, the options expiring today, because you are intraday, and pull QQQ since it is the derivative of NQ. The
terminal reads the gamma regime, the key levels and the hedging pressure for you.





A long gamma read in GEXRADAR: the regime is mean-reverting and dealers suppress moves, so expect chop and rotations. The
gamma regime, the key levels, the flip and the hedging-pressure gauge are all on one screen.

In a short gamma read the same panel flips: the regime shows dealers amplifying instead of suppressing, the
hedging-pressure gauge leans hard one way, and you switch from fading rotations to trading the trend and the
breakout through the level.
