11 The checklist



  every rule in this document, printable


  READING THE EXTREME


        I've checked whether price arrived aggressively (defend it) or slowly (expect it broken).


      I'm not assuming rejection or acceptance from the extreme alone.



  USING A PREVIOUS BALANCE


        I have a breakout AND a retest that held, not just the breakout.


         If the retest failed to hold, I've flipped my read to the other side rather than forcing the original one.



  TRAPPED POSITIONING


        I can point to an aggressive push on Delta that visibly faded, not just assume exhaustion.


        I understand that trapped positioning fuels the move against it, not just fails to stop it.



  WHEN THE HIGHER TIMEFRAME GIVES NOTHING


      I'm willing to take no trade through a genuine chop, rather than force one.


        I've dropped to a lower timeframe (his default: 15 minute) for confirmation before acting on a higher
      timeframe bias.
