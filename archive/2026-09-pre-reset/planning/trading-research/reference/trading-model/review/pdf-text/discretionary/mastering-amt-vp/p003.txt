03 What AMT actually is



  a philosophy, not a strategy


  Auction Market Theory does not tell you to buy or sell, and that is the first thing to unlearn

  coming from indicator-based trading. AMT tells you how buyers and sellers are actually

  interacting: where one side has strength, where the other is trapped, where aggression is

  missing entirely. Volume Profile and TPO are not order flow either, they are a visualization of

  AMT, a way of seeing where that interaction already happened. Order flow itself, the part that

   tells you whether to give a level leniency right now, comes from the DOM, the footprint, the

  orders actually arriving. AMT tells you where to look; order flow tells you whether to believe

  what you're looking at.





  The three rules, on the same board they get taught on: understand the auction, respect the 80/20 split,
  and stop trading VAH/VAL as if they were fixed levels.
