11 The B-shaped profile



  the same idea, mirrored to the downside


  A B-shaped profile is the exact same mechanism as the P-shape, run in the other direction: one

  side, this time sellers, drives price lower first, and both sides then come into agreement at that

  lower price, building fair value there. The profile shows a wide bulge of volume at the bottom

  and a thin stem above it.


  Read it the same way as the P-shape, mirrored: don't fight the drive that built it, read the range

  once fair value is established, and with a B-shaped profile the underlying trend is lower, the

  same way a P-shape says the underlying trend is higher.



  THE B-SHAPED PROFILE: THE MIRROR OF THE P-SHAPE, TREND READ AS LOWER
