What VWAP actually is


VWAP stands for volume weighted average price: the average the market has actually paid, weighted by volume.
Institutions use it to identify premium and discount zones, which is exactly why you should watch it.





VWAP with its deviation bands. The median line is the volume weighted average, the bands are the extremes around it.

In AMT logic the VWAP median is the POC of the session so far: where
the business has averaged out.



   THE BANDS

   The lines above and below are standard deviation bands. Run plus and minus 2 and 2.5, you can add 3 but price rarely
   touches it. They are the extremes of premium and discount, the same idea as the edges of value.

Premium and discount by deviation





The deviations turn VWAP into a premium and discount model.





   PLUS AND MINUS 1        The working premium and discount zone. Ordinary business happens inside here.





   PLUS AND MINUS 2        The tops of the extremes. This is where the odds genuinely stack, an institution
                                    trying to get filled is getting its best price out here.





   THE HIGHER THE           The higher the chance of reversal soon, because that is the ideal spot for
   PREMIUM                      institutions to get in. Same in reverse for discount.





   THE TWO RULES

   One: the trades worth taking live beyond the 1 band and ideally at the 2, deal with at least the 1. Two: only
   trade VWAP extremes WITH absorption, never on the touch alone.





   THE FALLING KNIFE WARNING

     If there is no confirmation, or the draw beyond the band is clearly still higher or lower, do not trade against it.
    Deviation is probability, not a wall.

CVD, the aggression meter





CVD is cumulative volume delta: the running net difference between aggressive buyers and sellers, market buys
against market sells, accumulated over time.





   CVD RISING                                     CVD FALLING

   More aggressive buying is entering the market. The            More aggressive selling. The sellers are crossing the
    buyers are the ones paying up.                               spread to get out or get short.





Price tells you what happened. CVD tells you who paid for it.

The three step CVD plan





The three step plan for putting CVD to work.




      Step 1, hunt the divergence
         Price rising while CVD falls means sellers are active despite the move. The move is being carried by
         passive orders, a potential reversal signal.





      Step 2, grade every breakout
        Real breakouts print rising CVD. Price breaking out on flat or falling CVD is likely a fakeout, low conviction
        dressed up as momentum.





      Step 3, spot the absorption zones
         Price stalling while CVD keeps climbing or falling means large players are quietly taking the opposite side.
        That standoff usually resolves violently.





   WHERE THIS FITS

  VWAP gives the location, the deviation extreme. CVD plus the ladder gives the confirmation. Location plus
   confirmation is the whole method in one sentence.

Anchored VWAP




The session VWAP resets every day. The advanced move is to anchor it manually to the exact event the big players
are averaging from.



An anchored VWAP starts from a moment that mattered, not from
midnight.





   ANCHOR TO THE SWING     Drop the VWAP from a major swing high or low. It becomes the average price of
                              everyone who has traded since that turn, and it acts as dynamic support or
                                 resistance from that point on.





   ANCHOR TO THE EVENT      CPI, FOMC, the earnings gap, the session open. Anchoring from the catalyst shows
                            you the true average of the move the news created, which is the level institutions
                                defend.





   MULTIPLE TIMEFRAMES    A weekly and a monthly anchored VWAP give you swing levels. When session,
                             weekly and an anchored VWAP all converge, that is a heavyweight level worth
                                  building a trade around.





  WHY IT BEATS THE PLAIN VWAP

   The session VWAP answers where is the average today. The anchored VWAP answers where is the average
   since the thing that actually moved the market. Institutions think in the second one, so you should have it on
   the chart.
