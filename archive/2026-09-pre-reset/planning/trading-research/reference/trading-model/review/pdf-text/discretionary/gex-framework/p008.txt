Why this matters for you


Once you see hedging as a force, price behaviour that looked random starts to make sense. Dealer gamma is why:

       Price reverses with no news
       You have all seen price rip into a level and reverse with zero news behind it. That is not random, it is dealer
        hedging defending a strike. Once you know the regime you stop getting trapped by these.


       Breakouts fail at certain strikes
         In long gamma dealers fade strength, so a breakout into heavy positioning gets absorbed and snapped
         back. If you are buying that breakout blind, you are walking straight into their hedging.


       Trend days happen below the gamma flip
       Below the flip dealers are short gamma and have to chase, buying higher and selling lower. That is the fuel
        behind the big one-way days where every pullback just gets bought.


      Range days happen above the gamma flip
       Above the flip dealers are long gamma and suppress every move, so price chops and mean reverts. Trying to
        run breakouts up here just bleeds the account.


      0DTE can control the whole session
       0DTE is now a huge chunk of the flow, and because it expires today it carries the entire session. That is
         exactly why you read QQQ at 0DTE, not a contract expiring next month.



     It always comes back to this: how much hedging pressure are dealers under, and in which direction. Answer that and
   you know the kind of day to expect, more reliably than staring at the VIX.
