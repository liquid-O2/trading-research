04 Mistake 1: not waiting for a reward system



  the 3 tick rule, and how to actually read it


  The single most common mistake, ahead of the other three combined. Absorption prints, and

  the entry goes in immediately, on the print itself. His correction: wait for confirmation after the

  absorption occurs, not the moment it occurs.


  The confirmation has a specific shape. Price needs to move in your direction within three ticks

  of the absorption for it to count as replenished. Miss that window and the absorption is treated

  as unconfirmed, whatever it looked like on the print.


  On the chart: buyers come in at a top, price comes down, sellers confirm the move lower. But if

  those sellers are not being replenished, if the time and sales or speed of tape shows sellers

  refreshing and buyers stepping in instantly to defend, that is buyers defending, not sellers in

  control. Entering there means entering where the side you are fading is actively winning.
