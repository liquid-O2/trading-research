06 Trapped buyers on the higher timeframe



  an aggressive push that fades, and why that fuels the move down


  On the higher timeframe auction shown, price pushed up and failed to hold. The Delta told the

  story before price fully confirmed it: buyers were a lot stronger going in, then visibly faded. That

  fade is the tell. Buyers came in aggressively, got exhausted, and lost interest in pushing any

  higher.


  The consequence follows directly: those buyers are now trapped, and a trapped position

  eventually has to be sold to get out of. That forced selling doesn't fight the move down, it fuels

    it, which is exactly why sellers taking control from here tends to be a more amplified move than

  sellers taking control from a level with no trapped buyers behind it. This alone was enough to

  start looking for shorts.
