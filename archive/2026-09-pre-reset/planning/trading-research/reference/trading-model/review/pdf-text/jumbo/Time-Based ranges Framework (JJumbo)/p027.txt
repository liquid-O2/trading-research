 Entry Models (section e.)
  “Personal favourites not fixed to the model ”

    In this section, I will go through my usual entry models to engage in the market
    with the model presented.
   The entry models will be tied to your win rate and management, everyone has a
     different view on win rate, some prefer a high win rate and low Risk/Reward,
   some prefer a low win rate and high Risk/Reward.
    This is entirely up to you, but the way a personally operate, i usually go for high
    Risk/Reward trades, sacrificing my win rate.
    As a rule of thumb, win rate doesn’t define your profitability as a trader and it’s
    very much dependant on the trader’s style.


    Orderblocks - Rejection blocks
    These are my personal favourite functions for my entry models, especially on reversals,
    they usually provide a good invalidation with tight stop losses allowing me to engage in
