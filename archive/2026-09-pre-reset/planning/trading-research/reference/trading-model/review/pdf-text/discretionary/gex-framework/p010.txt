GEX as a regime filter


GEX is shorthand for how the market is positioned in gamma and what that implies for hedging. Read as a regime, it
tells you the day type before the open.


  SHORT / NEG GAMMA                      LONG / POS GAMMA
   Trend day                                             Chop day

      Higher R, target the expansion                                  Smaller R, scalp rotations

      Continuation and breakouts                                       Failed auctions and fades

     Fewer trades, let it run                                         Skip the breakout chases
     TREND       CHOP

   GEX does not tell you exactly where to enter. It tells you how the day will move, so you pick the right kind of setup and
    target. Context first, then confirmation, then execution.
