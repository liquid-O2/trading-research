Framework Overview

The framework is based entirely on time-based ranges that appear through
out the day during electronic trading hours and regular trading hours (all
times i refer to during the entirety of this document will be in Eastern
Standard Time, ET).

Markets often exhibit rhythmic behavior tied to sessions, where liquidity and
volatility spike predictably for order accumulations and breakouts

The idea behind this and how i view ranges is that every range has
measurable exhaustion points where price absorbes orders accumulated
during the range window and reverses or breaks after hitting trapped stops
to then distribute to post-breakouts targets often corresponding to the range
extensions. It usually happens in the form of “False Breakout” or judas swing
as ICT describes it (which is one of the models later presented) or just “Range
breakout”.

Our job is to anticipate these moves in the exhaustion areas and position
ourselves during specific time windows during the trading session where
statistically these moves form.

This logic can be applied to multiple ranges present throughout the day
helping you identify these moves and the signatures in price at said levels.
This system is fairly simple and a rules-based system focusing on time and
price signatures can reduce overthinking, making it actionable if backtested
and followed consistently.
