07 Reading the shape of a day



  three shapes, one profile


  He calls them dealing ranges. Once a session's volume settles into a shape, that shape has a

   letter, and the letter is shorthand for which side actually won the argument that day.


  A P day has its bulk of volume sitting toward the top of the balance, a fat head with a thin tail

  hanging below it, and it resolves with price breaking down out of the bottom. A B day is the

  mirror of it, volume concentrated toward the bottom, resolving higher.





  P day on the left, B day on the right. The letter is where the volume built before the break; the break is
  which side actually spent more time defending it.
