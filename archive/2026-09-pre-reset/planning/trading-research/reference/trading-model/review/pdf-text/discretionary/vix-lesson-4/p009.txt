VIX plus the auction





This is where the lesson connects to everything before it. AMT logic tells you where value is. VIX tells you whether
the auction will rotate inside that value or explore outside it.




      Balance on the profile, VIX dropping
         Play the rotations. Fade the edges of value back toward the POC, price is more likely to rotate inside the
         value area and respect VAH and VAL.





      Balance on the profile, VIX rising
        Prepare for the range break. Expansion is loading, do not lean on the walls the way you would on a falling-
        VIX day.





       Size the breakout by the VIX
      When VIX is dropping hard, size smaller on breakout attempts, they fail into rotation more often. Real
        breaks need real volatility behind them.





   THE ONE LINE

   The profile is the map, the VIX is the weather. You need both before you drive.
