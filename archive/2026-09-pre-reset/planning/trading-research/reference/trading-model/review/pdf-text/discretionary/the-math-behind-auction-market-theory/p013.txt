13 Fix two: real extremes need two transitions



  the difference between a shelf, a ledge, and a tail


  The second fix is about which levels actually qualify as extremes. Value area high and value area

  low are arbitrary, they shift as volume builds and behave like a lagging indicator if you trade

  them as fixed. Low volume nodes, ledges and shelves are the real extremes, but only once you

  understand transitions.


  A low volume node needs a second transition after it, a return to balance, to actually count as

  one rather than just a tail, a taper of volume trailing off with nothing behind it. Same rule for a

  shelf versus a ledge: a shelf is volume gradually tapering into the next transition, a ledge is

  volume cutting off instantly with no taper at all. Mark either one without a second transition

  behind it and you're not looking at a real extreme, you're looking at noise that happens to look

   like one on the chart.


  A SHELF DRAWN LIVE: VOLUME TAPERING TOWARD THE NEXT TRANSITION





  Not an instant cutoff, which is what would make this a ledge instead. The taper itself is what earns it the
  name.
