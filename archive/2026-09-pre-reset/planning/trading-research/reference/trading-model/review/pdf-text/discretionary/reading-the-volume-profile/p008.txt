08 The double distribution



  his favorite structure for building context, with a full trade example


  A double distribution is where both sides initially agree on a price and establish fair value, then

  one side takes control and drives price away into a fresh imbalance, before both sides come

  back into agreement at a new price and build a second area of fair value. The result is two

  distinct shelves on the profile, one above, one below, joined by the leg that connects them.


  This is one of his favorite structures for building context specifically because it gives you a plan

  before price ever gets there. With two shelves marked, if price opens and comes down to retest

  the lower shelf and buyers hold it, the read is a long back toward the upper extreme, entry at

  the retest, target at the far shelf. If price opens lower instead and sellers break through with a

  retest that holds, the same logic runs in reverse: short the retest, target the next extreme down.
