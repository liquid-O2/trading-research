What really moves NQ and ES


The real engine behind ES and NQ is options flow. Options flow is the stream of options trades that creates hedging
pressure in the futures market, pressure dealers must respond to in real time. Most of that flow feeds straight back
into the futures you trade.

NQ and ES are derivatives.

Derivative comes from the word derive: they come from something else. Think of universities. There is no single
university, but they all teach from the same curriculum handed down by the board. Price works the same way. The
QQQ options complex is the origin point that NQ derives from, and SPY is the origin for ES. So when you read QQQ
options, you are reading the source that moves NQ.

 QQQ               NQ                ES
   the options origin                                   derives from QQQ                                   derives from SPY


This is why reading the futures chart alone leaves you blind to half the picture. The order that moved your candle
often started in the options market.
