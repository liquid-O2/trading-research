11 Which one is the real setup



  the same test from the video


  Two real NQ charts, both showing a break of balance followed by a return to a grey zone. Only

  one is an actual Failed Auction setup. Cover the answer below and decide first.





  Chart 1 on the left, chart 2 on the right. Same visual grammar, and only one of them is the setup from the
   last chapter.




     THE ANSWER

      Chart 1 is correct. Balance, break, a tag of the prior balance's white box, an instant rejection, and

     an entry back toward VAH as target. Chart 2 fails the definition even though it looks similar: price
     does break balance and does retest a grey zone, but it never tags a prior balance and rejects

      there, it simply breaks down, keeps going, and the retest is a trend continuation entry, not a

       failed auction. Same visual grammar, opposite trade, which is exactly why the setup needs its full

       definition and not just "price came back to something."
