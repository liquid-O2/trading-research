 TRADING VIEW:
 Adopting the use of presets for your orders, will allow you to be quick on executions
 during high volatility especially at market open.
  It will set your target and stop loss instantly without wasting time pulling R/R tools or
 manually setting stop loss and target.
 There three different entries: Market Order, Limit Order, Stop Order each of them will be
 beneficial on different scenarios.
     Market Order
 Instant execution at current or best price available (this is what i mostly use during high
  volatility)




                                      Set your desired Stop Loss and target price
                                    as well as your desired risk and save preset.

                                Once activated Buy/Sell buttons and instant
                                      execution at the top-left of your chart, you will
                                  have instant execution when pressed buy or
                                                     sell.




     Limit Order
  Generally i don’t use Limit Orders unless i’m copying multiple accounts on my copier for
  precise execution avoiding to de-sync my accounts when entering a position and
  trading low volatility conditions.

                                      Set your desired Stop Loss, target price
                               and desired risk, i have my “ask/bid” set to 0
                                            for a more precise execution.
