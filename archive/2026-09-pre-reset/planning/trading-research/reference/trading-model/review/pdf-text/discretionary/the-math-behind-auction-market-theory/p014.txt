14 Fix three: know what cycle price is in



  pairing higher timeframe auction with a lower timeframe trigger


  The third fix ties the first two together. Before taking any level, know whether price is in a

  balanced state or in price discovery. In balance, expect consolidating, two-sided moves, and

  take the reversal trades at the extremes. In discovery, price has already broken balance and is

  searching for a new one, and taking a reversal short into a discovery move higher just because

  "that's where sellers always show up" ignores what the auction is actually doing.


  The dome, footprint, book map and heat map are all showing you close to the same information,

  and that's the lower timeframe entry trigger. But 90% of what shows up on the dome is noise

  unless it's paired with the higher timeframe auction first: know the cycle, know the level, then

  use the lower timeframe order flow to time the entry, never the other way around.
