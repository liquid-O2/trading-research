05 Extremes



  the outer edges of the auction, and the one question they force


  Extremes are the same idea as value area, from the other direction: the outer edges of the

  auction, the taper in volume at each end of the bell curve. When price reaches an extreme, there

   is exactly one question worth asking: does the market want to keep accepting prices out here,

  or does it want to reject them and return to fair value.


  That question is more useful than it sounds, because it replaces a guess with an observation.

  You are not predicting what price will do at the extreme. You are watching what it actually does

  once it gets there, and reading that.
