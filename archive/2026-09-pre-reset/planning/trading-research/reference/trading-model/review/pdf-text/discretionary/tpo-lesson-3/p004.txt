POC, VAH and VAL in time


The same three levels from the volume profile exist here, built from time instead of volume.

POC            The price level with the highest amount of time spent. It acts as a draw for price, the level where buyers and
                     sellers previously reached their fairest agreement.


VAH             Top of the value zone. Above it, price is trading at a premium.


VAL             Bottom of the value zone. Below it, price is trading at a discount.
