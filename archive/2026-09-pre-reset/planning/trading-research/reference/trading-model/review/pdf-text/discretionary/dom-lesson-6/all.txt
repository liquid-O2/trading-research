Absorption, the trap





Lesson 5 gave you the ladder. This lesson gives you the two behaviours on it that actually make entries: absorption,
and pulling and stacking.





Absorption is a trap. Retail attacks a level, institutions feed on the
attack.





  HOW THE TRAP WORKS

    Lots of retail traders buy at a key resistance, an old high, expecting the breakout. Big institutions use exactly those buy
    orders to sell into. The big side has more power, price drops quickly, and the retail buyers are left stuck in losing
    positions.





   THE SIGNATURE

   Big market orders hitting your level that do not push price through it. The aggression arrives, the level eats it,
    price reverses. That is absorption, and it is one of the highest quality triggers on the ladder.

Trading absorption, step by step





The step plan, exactly as it runs at the screen.



      Mark your levels first
         Shelves, ledges, value edges, old highs and lows. Absorption only means something at a level worth
         defending.





       Reset the DOM before price arrives
         Clear the counts so what you are reading is only the fight at the level, not an hour of noise.





      Read the fake break
       The DOM shows heavy opposing orders, it looks like your level is about to break. If price rejects fast and
       moves your way instead, that was institutions absorbing the retail orders. That is the entry.





      Watch the market orders, not the limits
       The middle column is the truth, the aggressive prints. Limit orders are intentions, market orders are
         actions.





     Remember it can spread
        Absorption is not always one price. 300 buy orders can get eaten across 3 ticks. Watch the zone, not just
        the single tick.

Pulling and stacking





The passive side of the ladder plays its own game, and it has two moves.





   STACKING                  Traders keep adding limit buys on the bid, or limit sells on the ask. Bids stacking
                                while bid delta rises looks like strong buy support. Asks stacking while ask delta
                                    rises looks like strong sell pressure.





   PULLING                  Those stacked orders get removed before price ever trades there. Bids pulling
                                while bid delta is positive means buyers just lost their support, price can drop.
                             Asks pulling while ask delta is negative means sellers lost their pressure, price can
                                        rise.





Stacking shows intent. Pulling shows fluff. Delta confirms who is
actually aggressive.

The read at the level





Absorption on the ladder: aggressive sellers hit the bid, the level (highlighted) refuses to break and reloads, price
reverses up.




       Aggression into the level, no follow-through
       The absorption tell. Someone bigger is on the other side of the attack.





      Stacked size that stays
        Real defence. Stacked size that vanishes as price approaches was never real.





       Delta agrees with the read
         Positive delta with no upward progress means trapped buyers. That is fuel for the other direction.

Absorption vs exhaustion





Absorption has a close cousin that traders confuse it with constantly. Knowing the difference is the whole edge,
because they point opposite ways.





   ABSORPTION                                      EXHAUSTION

   A passive giant soaks up aggression at a level and             The aggressive side simply runs out. Volume dries up,
    holds it. Heavy volume trades, price does not move,             the pushes get smaller, no big passive player is
    the aggressive side gets trapped. It signals a reversal,          needed, the move just dies of its own accord. It signals
   someone wanted every contract at that price.                 a pause or a fade, not necessarily a hard reversal.





Stopping volume is the extreme version: a single burst of huge volume that halts a trend dead. It is absorption at its
most violent, the last aggressive push meeting a wall, and it often marks the turn.





  HOW TO TELL THEM APART

   Watch the volume as price stalls. Big volume with no movement is absorption, someone is there. Shrinking
   volume with no movement is exhaustion, nobody is left. Absorption reverses harder, exhaustion drifts.
