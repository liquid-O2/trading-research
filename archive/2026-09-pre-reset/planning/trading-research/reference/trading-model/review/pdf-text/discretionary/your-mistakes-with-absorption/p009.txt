09 Reading the sponge on the chart



  effort, a passive wall, no reward, then the reversal aggression that confirms it


  Walked in order: aggressive effort from buyers pushing into a level. A wall of passive sellers

  absorbing it, not fighting it directly, just soaking it up. No reward for that buying effort,

  confirmed by price failing to hold the push. Then, and only then, seller aggression arrives and

  gives you a second energy source behind the move, not just an absence of buyers but active

   selling.


  His own instruction from here: enter on the test of the reward system, not on the wall itself.

  Once price is going lower with aggression on your side and the opposition is failing, wait for one

  more retest with a decent amount of refresh orders in time and sales, visible on the DOM or the

  footprint too, before taking the trade.
