04 The misconception that breaks it



  effort versus reward, and nothing else


  Here is where most people go wrong with any aggression tool, and it happens in the first hour of

  using one. You see sellers being absorbed at a low and you read it as a buy signal. That is not

  what it is.


  In his own words: "This doesn't tell you when to buy or sell, it just shows you orders, it just

  shows you information." A bubble is a record that size traded aggressively at that price. It is not

  a direction.


  What makes it usable is the pairing. Aggression is effort. The candle that follows is the reward,

  or the absence of one. Effort that gets paid is control. Effort that gets nothing is exhaustion. That

  single distinction is the entire read, and it is why two identical looking clusters of bubbles can

  mean opposite things.


  The trap is that both sides usually show effort at the same time. Buyers absorbed at the top of a

  range, sellers absorbed at the bottom of it. When that is the picture, you do not know who is in

  control, because nobody is yet. His instruction there is to wait for the break rather than to pick a

  side inside the range.





        If you can only take one thing from this document, take this one. Aggression measures effort.

      Price measures whether the effort was worth anything. A tool that shows you the first without

     you checking the second will lose you money faster than having no tool at all.
