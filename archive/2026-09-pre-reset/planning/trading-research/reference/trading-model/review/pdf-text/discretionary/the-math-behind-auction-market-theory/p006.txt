   Imbalance is not a directional oracle



      replenishment is the only question that matters


      A resting order book imbalance, more size sitting on one side than the other, feels like it

       should tell you direction. It doesn't, at least not on its own. Treated as a static snapshot,

           it's what jetbundle calls a directional oracle that isn't actually oracular: a discretionary

        trader reacting to a lopsided book in isolation is trading noise.


      The only question that actually matters once price approaches a level: is that size

        replenishing, or is it vanishing? An imbalance that keeps refilling as it gets consumed is a

       completely different situation from one that's disappearing into fills. That's the real

         signal, and it's a dynamic one, not a screenshot.
