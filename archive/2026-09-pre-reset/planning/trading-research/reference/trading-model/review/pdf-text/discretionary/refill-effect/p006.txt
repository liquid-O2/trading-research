Testing it honestly means turning the idea into numbers. Every zone becomes a row of
data. Every return of price to a zone, every touch, becomes an event with a known
outcome: the level held, or it broke. And every fact we could have known beforethat
outcome becomes a feature: a checklist item a trader could read off the chart in real
time.

That checklist, the feature set, is what this paper is really about. Not an indicator, not a
magic entry: a set of observable facts about a level that, together, tell you whether the
defenders are likely still there.

The next sections measure it end to end: the data and the baseline, where the profit
actually lives, the out-of-sample record, the overfitting battery, and what it means inside
a funded-account evaluation.
