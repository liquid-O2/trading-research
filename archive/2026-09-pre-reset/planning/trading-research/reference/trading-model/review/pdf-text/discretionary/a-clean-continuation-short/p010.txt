10 The second trade: origin of the move,
 retaken



  a failed squeeze, a refill zone, then sellers show up again


  A second good trade from the same session came from retaking shorts at an origin of the move.

  Sellers showed their hand first, willing to price lower, which is what a genuine origin of the move

  entry needs: initiative, not just a guess. Taken there directly, it would have been a high risk

  entry, since the squeeze lower hadn't actually confirmed yet.


  Price came back up instead, into a refill zone where sellers had already shown control,

  effectively top-ticking there. From that point, sellers came back in with even more aggression

  than the first attempt, confirming that control was back with them despite the earlier failed

  squeeze. Price moved back toward intraday daily value, buyers hit the wall on the way and got

  absorbed, and that's the re-entry point, stop placed right there. Target: back to where sellers

  previously had no control, or more conservatively, the current intraday POC.
