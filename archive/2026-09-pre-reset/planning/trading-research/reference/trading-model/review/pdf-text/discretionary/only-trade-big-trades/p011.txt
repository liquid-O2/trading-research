11 The failed auction long



  balance, out of balance, rejected at prior value


  Put together, the higher timeframe sequence is a failed auction. Price balanced. Price left

  balance. Price then hit an area that was previously a minor volume node and previously balance,

  and rejected there instead of accepting into it. His own summary is that this is "a really, really

  clean failed auction long."


  The reason he takes this specific trade, out of everything on the chart, is that both halves agree.

  The lower timeframe says buyers just took control off the origin of the move. The higher

  timeframe says price is rejecting away from a level it should reject from, with thin volume

  underneath and a yearly composite behind it. Neither read is doing the work alone.


  That alignment is also what gives you what he calls leniency. If you know the higher timeframe

  favours buyers, then you know who to give the benefit of the doubt to when the order flow gets

  messy, and you know which absorption to expect to hold. Without it, every wick is a coin flip.
