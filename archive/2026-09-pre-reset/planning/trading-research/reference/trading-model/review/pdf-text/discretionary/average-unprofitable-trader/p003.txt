  THE CASE03
 Meet the average unprofitable trader

  Most people watching a case study expect a disaster story: someone reckless, someone
  lazy, someone who never learned the concepts. Keani is not that. He's average, and
  average is the problem.

  Keani, who goes by Alive, has been trading Nasdaq futures since September 2025, and
  his path was better than most. A friend steered him away from ICT and into order flow; he
  joined the Data server, learned AMT from the basics up, moved on to MotiveWave. Daily-
  chart reference points, the 30-minute TPO, poor highs and poor lows, value. On paper,
  that's a framework.

  But he had a job, which meant he had eval money, and that's where the spiral started.
  Eval after eval, each one bought on the feeling that this time he knew enough. He went
  from blowing evals to passing some. And then the sentence that tells you everything:



  "Whenever I did pass the evals, I just felt like I got lucky doing it... that
  I actually didn't deserve it." 01:03


  He passed and still didn't trust it, and he knew he couldn't prove it either way. That
  feeling, "I got lucky and I can't prove otherwise," is the actual subject of this PDF.
  Everything that follows is about replacing it with data.
