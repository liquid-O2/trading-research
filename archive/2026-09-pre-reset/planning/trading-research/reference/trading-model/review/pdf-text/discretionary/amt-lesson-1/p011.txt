Reading the open




The first half hour tells you more about the coming session than any indicator, because the open is where the
overnight decision gets revealed. Four open types, in order of conviction.





   OPEN DRIVE                  Price drives hard in one direction straight off the open and never trades back
                               through it. The strongest statement the market can make. Do not fade it, look for
                                 continuation entries on shallow pullbacks.





   OPEN TEST DRIVE          The market opens, tests a key reference first, prior low, prior value edge, finds no
                               business there, then drives the other way. Second strongest, and it hands you the
                                    level your risk lives behind.





   OPEN REJECTION          Opens, auctions one way, gets rejected and trades back through the open.
   REVERSE                 Moderate conviction, expect two sided trade and treat the rejection extreme as
                               the day's reference.





   OPEN AUCTION               Price rotates around the open with no conviction either way. Nobody is in control,
                               expect a balance day and treat the early extremes as fade material.





  WHY THIS PAIRS WITH DAY TYPES

   The open type is the earliest read on the day type. An open drive points at a trend day. An open auction points
    at a normal day. You are building the same picture from two angles before most traders have taken their first
    loss.
