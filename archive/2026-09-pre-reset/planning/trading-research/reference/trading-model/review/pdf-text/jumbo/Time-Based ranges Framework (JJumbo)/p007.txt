Time-Based Ranges

In this section i will go through a series of time-based ranges that i personally
discovered and backtested and consider significant, by no means this what you
should only trade but feel free to experiment with other times with the help of
my tool


Overnight Session:

   08:00pm - 08:30pm (Asia opening range)
   00:00am - 00:30am (Midnight opening range)
   03:00am - 03:30am (London opening range)
   06:00am - 09:00am (ONS session - main New York model)

RTH Session AM:

   09:30am - 10:00am (Equities opening range)
   10:00am - 10:30am


RTH Session Lunch/PM:

   12:00pm - 12:30pm (Lunch opening range)
   03:00pm - 03:30pm (MOC macro trading session)
