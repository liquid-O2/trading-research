 why a breakout deserves suspicion, not conviction


  That 80/20 split is not a targeting tool by itself, it's the reason a breakout deserves suspicion

  before it deserves a trade. A move outside the box is the less likely outcome by a wide margin,

  which is exactly why the Failed Auction setup, a few pages from here, is built around what

  happens after the break, not the break itself. Trading every breakout as if it's automatically the

  start of something is the same mistake as trading VAH blindly, just wearing a different name.


  This is also why day type matters, covered next: a P day or B day tells you the balance already

  resolved once, which changes how much weight a fresh breakout deserves. A D day tells you it

  hasn't, which is a different setup entirely.
