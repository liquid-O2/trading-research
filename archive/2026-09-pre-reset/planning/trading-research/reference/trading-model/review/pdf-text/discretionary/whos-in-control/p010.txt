10 Why alignment is the whole method



  the higher timeframe read is a bias, not a trade, until price agrees


  Across the whole session, the strongest trade wasn't the first place price looked tradeable, it

  was the retest that came after sellers had already proven they could take control once.

  Everywhere else in the range, control was, in his own words, "free game," up for grabs to

  whichever side showed up. Down at the level that had already been broken and retested, sellers

  had already demonstrated the read, and the second retest simply repeated it.


  His own summary closes the method: even when the higher timeframe gives you a reaction, you

  need to confirm it, back it up by checking whether the lower timeframe is actually showing you

  that price wants to go the direction the higher timeframe suggested. A bias without that

  confirmation is a guess wearing a framework. It's all about alignment.
