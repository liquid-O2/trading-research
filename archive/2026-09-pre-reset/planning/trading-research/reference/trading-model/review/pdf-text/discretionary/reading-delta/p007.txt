07 The delta print, on a real order ticket



  reading the highest point on the profile against an actual short


  He calls the highest point on the Delta profile a delta print, and treats it as the single most load-

  bearing piece of information on the chart: whichever side produced it is the side that was

  rewarded. On the trade shown, that highest print is sell aggression, and it lines up with a short

  entered right there, stop and target both placed off real levels rather than a fixed distance.


  What happens next follows directly from the print: buyers who were active earlier get trapped

  as more sellers regain control, visible as what he calls a sell bubble. Price pushes to target.

  From here the same trailing logic from method one applies again: if buyers show up again on a

   retest, the stop can move above that new print rather than sitting static.
