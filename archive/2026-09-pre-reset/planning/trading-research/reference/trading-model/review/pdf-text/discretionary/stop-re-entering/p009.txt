09Step three: delta as a filter

 Trapped buyers, read straight off the delta





  The boxed zones hold outsized delta prints at the highs: a crowd of buyers acted there, and price paid them nothing. Those
  are trapped buyers. The short triggers when price breaks below the print zone, confirming the trap, with the CVD rolling over
  behind it.

  Delta is a filter, not a trigger. A huge delta print at the highs tells you a crowd committed
  at one price. The next few bars tell you whether they were rewarded or trapped, and
  that's the only part you trade. If the print sits at the highs and price can't leave, those
  buyers are inventory waiting to be liquidated. Below the print, sellers are in control and
  you have a location-backed short with the losing side fueling it.

  The tape-level version of the same read: you want the opposite side to come in and
  thicken, and you want the losing side to thin out. Doubles and triples where your side
  acts, singles where the losers used to be. When both happen at your level, the filter is
  passed. When neither happens, there is no trade, no matter how strong the urge is after
  the last stop-out.
