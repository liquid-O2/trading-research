08Limitations, stated plainly

  The out-of-sample window is 79 sessions, one regime slice, and the most recent quarter
  lost money. Limit fills assume a fill when price trades at or through the level; real queues
  are less kind. Costs are modelled, not lived.

  The per-trade edge is thin enough that discipline, not the signal, decides whether it
  survives contact with a human. And every number here is historical simulation: it
  describes what was, not what will be. Nothing in this paper is financial advice, and no
  result guarantees any future outcome.
