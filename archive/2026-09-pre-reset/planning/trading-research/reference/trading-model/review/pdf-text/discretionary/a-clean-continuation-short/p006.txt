06 Why the exit wasn't POC



  consistency rules, and reading a gamma long environment for chop risk


  The exit on this trade came off a transition in established value, not off POC, even though POC

  would also have worked as a valid take profit. The reason is deliberately not a pure order flow

  reason: consistency rules on a funded account, and the regime context for the week.


  For the prior week and a half, the market had mostly been sitting in a gamma long environment,

  which raises the odds of chop rather than a clean move all the way to the lower extreme. Taking

  the safer, closer exit reflected that risk rather than assuming this session would behave like a

  trend day. The two sessions immediately before this one had been in a negative gamma

  environment instead, which lines up with why price dropped and dumped into this balance in

  the first place.
