Delta divergence





The most powerful confirmation on the footprint is delta divergence, and it comes in two flavours you need to name
on sight.

Bearish delta divergence: price grinds to a fresh high while cumulative delta rolls over. The buying is passive, the
aggressive side is quietly selling.
