 How to use this

  This PDF is the in-depth companion I promised at the start of the case-study video. It
  follows one real trader, Keani, through a live diagnosis: his account, his numbers, his
  answers to my questions, and the blueprint we built to fix what those answers exposed.

 A lot of people will skip straight to the checklist page and print it. Don't. A checklist you
  don't understand is just another thing to ignore at 9:31. Read the case first, because
  every rule in here was written against a specific mistake you'll probably recognise in
  yourself. Then walk the flow map once with your own last twenty trades in mind. Only
  then print the checklists.
