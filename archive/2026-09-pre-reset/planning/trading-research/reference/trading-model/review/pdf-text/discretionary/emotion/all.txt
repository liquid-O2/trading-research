Process and routine





Performance is decided before the open, not during the session.





       Preparation defines performance
        Premarket analysis, journaling and a clear routine are non-negotiable. The trader with a plan is trading, the
       one without is reacting.





       Journal and tag every trade
         Timing, clarity, emotion, entry and exit quality. An unreviewed trade is wasted data, and data is the only
        honest coach you will ever have.





      Sim and review when struggling
        Switch to simulation, drill the setups, review the recordings and find the exact error. Struggling live with
          real money teaches nothing except fear.

Strategy and execution





The strategy side of discipline is subtraction, not addition.





   ONE TRADE, ONE EDGE     No system hopping. Stick to one framework, the auction, the order flow, until
                                mastery. Depth beats breadth by miles in this game.





   LESS IS MORE              Minimal charts, minimal tools. Simplicity breeds clarity, and clarity breeds
                                 execution.





   CONFIDENCE VS            Stay self aware on the hot streaks, that is exactly when recklessness gets invited
   OVERCONFIDENCE              in.





   RISK AND DISCIPLINE        Trading is not gambling when there is a tested system, defined risk and strict
                                     rules. Remove any one of the three and it is.

The emotions themselves





Emotions are information, not instructions.




      Accept them, do not suppress them
         Feel the fear, the greed, the frustration, and act on none of it. The gap between feeling and action is the
         entire skill.





       Losing streaks have one question
       Were you following the plan? If yes, keep taking valid trades, variance is doing its thing. If no, step back
       and review before the account pays for the lesson.





       Simplify every decision you can
        Fewer choices means fewer emotional errors. Overmanaging trades kills the probabilities the system was
          built on.





   THE FEEDBACK LOOP

   Emotional discipline outside trading strengthens trading, and trading discipline strengthens life. It is one loop,
   feed it from both ends.

The pre-market routine



Discipline is not willpower, it is a routine you do not have to think about. Here is the pre-market ritual that makes the
boring things automatic.


     Map the auction before the bell
         Yesterday's value, the naked POCs, the VIX read, the day type call. Ten minutes. Walk in with a plan, not a
        hope.




       Write your A+ setup down
       One sentence. If the trade in front of you does not match it, it is not a trade. The written rule is what beats
        the impulse.




       Set a hard loss limit for the day
      A number of R, or a dollar figure. Hit it and you are done, no negotiation. This one rule saves accounts that
         everything else cannot.




       Trade the plan, journal the deviation
        Every time you break a rule, log it. The pattern in your breaks is the exact thing costing you money.




       Close the laptop after the session
        Detach. The market runs tomorrow. Grinding a screen after your limit is how discipline erodes into
         revenge.





   THE POINT OF THE RITUAL

   You cannot feel your way to consistency, you can only route around your feelings with a process. The routine is
   the process. Run it on the good days and the bad days identically, and the emotion stops driving.
