Trading Set Ups (section a.)
6-9 Model (main New York AM model)

Time range selection: 6:00am - 9:00am

    Judas Model




 This model is what i define as my A+ bread and butter, always terminating my
 trading session before 10am. Quick in and out and easy to spot providing on
 average 80 -120 pts, it usually presents two distinct trade opportunities, the
 judas trades from 9:30 to the reversal window and the actual reversal trade
between the 9:40 and 9:50.

   Trade #1

As stated above, this model presents two trades with two levels of difficulty, the
first trade is where we position ourselves right at the open where we participate
in the judas move (false breakout) towards the projections of the range where
the reversal will take place.
The time window of this trade spans from 9:30 open until the 9:40 -9:50 reversal
window where the real move will happen. I suggest to have positions closed as
we reach the reversal window, the yield of this trade outcome will vary
depending on the size of the 6-9 range (e.g. the distances from open to
projection levels) application of risk to reward needs to be considered)

   Trade #2

The second trade in this model is easier to trade, filters out the volatility of the
open and allows you to let price develop before taking place in any position
avoiding to be knocked out of your trade.
Here is where we participate in the reversal during the 9:40 - 9:50 window, levels
of interest will be the mean reversal levels or -0.5 projection.
By this time we are already in position to ride the actual move after the fake
breakout, price will move faster and cleaner also allowing you to build a larger
position towards the higher projections of the range and compound on the
winners.
