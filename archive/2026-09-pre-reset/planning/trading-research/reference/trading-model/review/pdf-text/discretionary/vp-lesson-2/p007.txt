Trading the structure





Price is likely to react at the sensitive areas, the build-ups and the fall-offs. That makes shelves and ledges your
reaction zones, the places where a trade can exist.




       Trade the ledge, not the middle of the shelf
       The edge of the structure is where the decision happens. Inside the shelf is rotation, chop and noise. At
        the ledge the market either defends the business or abandons it.





       Only with the confirmation correct
      A ledge on its own is a line. Watch the DOM and the footprint at the level: absorption, aggression, the tape
         picking a side. No confirmation, no trade, same rule as every level in this method.





       Stack it with other levels
      A ledge that lines up with another reference, VWAP, a prior value area edge, an old POC, is worth far more
        than either level alone. Confluence is the filter. VWAP gets its own lesson later in the series.





   THE SEQUENCE

    Structure first, confirmation second, execution last. The profile tells you where the trade lives, the tape tells you
   when to take it.
