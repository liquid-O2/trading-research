What the profile is showing you





Lesson 1 gave you the auction: balance, imbalance and the value area. This lesson is about the tool that draws it, the
volume profile, and the two structures inside it that most traders never learn to read.




The profile shows you volume at price, not volume in time. It is a map
of where the business actually happened.





   HVN, HIGH VOLUME NODE                                 LVN, LOW VOLUME NODE

   A price where volume built up. The market spent time        A price where volume fell away. The market rejected it

    shelves and slows down inside them.                            traverse them quickly.

Real extremes vs lagging levels


Here is the part that actually builds an edge. VAH, VAL and POC are useful, but they recalculate as price moves. They
drift with the session, like a lagging indicator. A ledge does not move.

Shelves and ledges are real extremes. They are structure, not
statistics.





Ledges marked with the red lines. These edges were set by real business and they stay where they were set.





The simpler view again: four ledges around the POC. Fixed reference points on a profile that otherwise keeps changing.

Naked POCs and composites





Two ideas turn the profile from a single-session tool into a genuine map: naked POCs and composite profiles.





   NAKED POC                                      COMPOSITE PROFILE

   A prior session's POC that price has not traded back to         Merge several days or weeks into one profile. The
    yet. Untested, it acts like a magnet, the market tends         HVNs and LVNs that survive across all that data are the
    to return and tag it. A list of naked POCs above and               levels the whole market respects, far stronger than any
   below is a ready made target list.                                 single day's.





The logic is the same at every timescale. A shelf on a 5 minute profile is a scalp level, the same shelf on a composite
weekly profile is a swing level. Zoom the profile to match the trade you are hunting.





  HOW TO USE THEM

   Mark naked POCs as targets and composite HVNs as your heavyweight levels. When a naked POC lines up with
   a composite shelf, that is a level worth building a whole session around.

Trading the structure





Price is likely to react at the sensitive areas, the build-ups and the fall-offs. That makes shelves and ledges your
reaction zones, the places where a trade can exist.




       Trade the ledge, not the middle of the shelf
       The edge of the structure is where the decision happens. Inside the shelf is rotation, chop and noise. At
        the ledge the market either defends the business or abandons it.





       Only with the confirmation correct
      A ledge on its own is a line. Watch the DOM and the footprint at the level: absorption, aggression, the tape
         picking a side. No confirmation, no trade, same rule as every level in this method.





       Stack it with other levels
      A ledge that lines up with another reference, VWAP, a prior value area edge, an old POC, is worth far more
        than either level alone. Confluence is the filter. VWAP gets its own lesson later in the series.





   THE SEQUENCE

    Structure first, confirmation second, execution last. The profile tells you where the trade lives, the tape tells you
   when to take it.

The shelf checklist





Five checks before you trade any profile structure. Run them in order, every time.




      Mark the nodes
       HVNs and LVNs on the ranges that matter: yesterday, the overnight, the current balance.





      Draw the ledges
       The exact prices where each build-up starts and each fade-away begins. Those lines are the trade
          locations.





       Label the shelves
       Know which zones are agreed business. Inside them you expect rotation, not follow-through.





      Check for confluence
       Does a ledge line up with VWAP, a value area edge or an old POC? Stacked levels first, lone levels last.





      Wait for the tape
      DOM and footprint at the level. The structure is context, the confirmation is the trigger.
