    PROS DO IT TOO

     Studying Chicago Board of Trade proprietary T-bond traders, Coval and Shumway found traders with
     morning losses about 16% more likely to take above-average afternoon risk than traders with morning
      gains. Even pros in the pit sized up after a red morning; a rule that survives the impulse is protection. Coval
     andShumway,"DoBehavioralBiasesAffectPrices?",JournalofFinance,2005
