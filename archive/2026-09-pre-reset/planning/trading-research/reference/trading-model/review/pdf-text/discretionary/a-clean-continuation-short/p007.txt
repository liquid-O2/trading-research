07 The higher timeframe: yearly composite



  a low participation zone, sliced through, then used as resistance again


  Zooming out to the yearly composite, the level in play sat at an area of low participation, a zone

  price had previously sliced straight through rather than idled inside. Higher timeframe low

  volume nodes behave in one of two ways: price stalls and tries to build balance there, or, if

  there's enough selling pressure, it slices straight through. The Delta on that slice showed sellers

  clearly showing their hand, and price fully dumped through it.


  From there, price arrived at a yearly composite balance area, again with a nearby zone of low

  participation. The same previous balance from earlier in the session got touched again and used

  again as resistance to push price back down, slicing through the same low volume area a

  second time.
