10 Two mentorships, and what neither gave
 him



  order flow from one, strategy from another, habits from neither


  He'd been in two prior mentorships before this one. The first taught him most of his order flow

  basics, reading confirmations, absorption, CVD divergence. The second taught him a scalping

  strategy. Both are, in his own words, real skills he still uses. Neither one taught him what to do

  with a losing trade in the moment it stopped looking clean.


  His description of trading before this mentorship: he'd see a valid order flow signal, take it, and

  then panic the instant it didn't play out immediately, moving his stop, moving his target, second

  guessing a read he genuinely had right. He knew the confluences. He didn't have the conviction

  to sit inside a trade once it stopped feeling comfortable, because nothing was backing that

  conviction with data.




     HIS OWN FRAMING

      Beginner order flow traders lose to what looks like a psychology problem, when it's actually a

       risk management problem. A framework that tells you exactly where to trail, where to take

       partials, where to go breakeven removes the decision a panicking trader makes badly in the

     moment. It isn't a mindset fix, it's a rule that makes the mindset irrelevant.
