  THE BLUEPRINT21
 Worked example: open above value

  Late in the call, Keani laid out his own setup, and it's worth walking through because it
  shows what a framework sounds like once the vagueness is squeezed out.

  His scenario: the current day's TPO opens fully above yesterday's value, A period sitting
  clear of the prior value area high. He waits until around 10:00 for the day type to
  establish from the shape of the profile, then watches the current day's value area high for
  a break and a retest, entering off the volume profile.

  I drew it out, and we validated it through AMT rather than through "does it look good."
  Sequence matters, so follow it in order. Value opening and building higher is the market
  telling you buyers are in control and sellers are getting exhausted. Price will probably
  reject off the POC or the previous day's value area high. Once we're above it and price
  breaks the current value area high, you should see aggressive buying imbalances doing
  the breaking. Then the test that decides everything: price comes back to those
  imbalances. If the buyers who broke the level defend them, that retest is a good long. If
  they fold, you have your answer, and it cost nothing.

  Notice what made the setup good. Every step is the auction confirming the same story
  from a different angle: value shift, rejection, aggressive break, defended retest. No step
   is decoration.

 Now the caveats, because I gave Keani these on the call and they're load-bearing. The
  trade is still subject to time of day, still subject to what the DOM shows at the retest, and
   it still needs a decent higher-timeframe objective, somewhere for the auction to actually
  go. A clean break-and-retest into nothing is a clean entry into a losing trade. Thesis first,
  always. The entry is the last gate, never the first.
