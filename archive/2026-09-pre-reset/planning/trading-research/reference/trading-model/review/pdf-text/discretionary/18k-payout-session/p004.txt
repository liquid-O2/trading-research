04 The thesis before the first trade



  a dealing range, a weekly delta print, and the gamma flip





  The higher timeframe view drawn out before the open: the prior dealing range, three minor volume nodes
  marked as the levels that had already produced reactions, and the weekly delta print underneath price.


  Before a single trade went out, the read was built top down. Price had broken a prior balance,

  come back to rebalance it, and the weekly delta footprint showed sellers trapped at the lows

  and exhausted on the way back up, buyers covering into that low. That is not a chart pattern. It

   is a record of who lost the fight for that price and who is now defending the win.


  The objective was set lower, because of where the balance sat on the yearly composite to the

   right of price: a lot of built-up value above and comparatively little below, which is the kind of

  asymmetry that makes one direction the path of least resistance rather than a guess.




     SHORT GAMMA, STATED AT THE OPEN

     "We have this and we are currently in a short gamma, and know that short gamma environment."

     Chat pointed out one gamma model was reading positive. The honest answer given live: price

     was sitting almost exactly on the flip between the two, close enough that either read was

      defensible, and the trade plan did not change because of it.
