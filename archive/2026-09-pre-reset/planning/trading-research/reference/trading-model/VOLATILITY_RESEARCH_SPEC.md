# Expanded volatility, IV, skew and VIX-option research

This specification expands C09–16/C23–24, O02/04/05/10/16/21 and G02 into independently tested measurements and specialist forecasts. [Experiment phases and registry](SPECIALIST_EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM.md) apply to every child. All model comparisons are proposed. The archive supports different information sets: long bar history, newer native futures tape, partial equity/index option strips, and full-strike VIX-option minute snapshots. Each receives its own coverage cohort and matched intersection comparison.

## Distinguish the quantities before combining models

Realized variance is a measurement of an observed path. Forecast physical variance concerns a future path under the empirical measure P. An option IV is a pricing-model coordinate under risk-neutral valuation Q; it is neither a direct physical forecast nor an intraday VIX observation. A price-excursion distribution, ordered barrier probability, surface-change forecast and VIX-option IV have different target IDs. G02 may combine experts only at compatible targets. A learned P bridge can use Q features to forecast NQ/ES variance, downside/upside excursions, jump likelihood or path events.

Every surface node stores source instrument/expiry/settlement, quote cut and age, forward/discount assumptions, coordinate `k=log(K/F)`, time-to-expiry T, IV fraction σ, total implied variance `w=σ²T`, bid/ask-compatible interval, support and solver flags. Different maturity contracts are not the same instrument. Use paired call/put raw-quote parity where contract style permits it; otherwise use a separately validated valuation model. Inputs to physical forecasting include uncertainty and missingness, not just fitted midpoints.

## Formula and target fixtures

`w(T2)−w(T1)` divided by `T2−T1` is an interval average **implied variance proxy** when w comes from ATM Black IV. The interpretation as expected integrated forward variance needs a variance-contract/replication definition and assumptions. The near and far expiries must have compatible source/settlement semantics. Example: T1=.1, σ1=.2 gives w1=.004; T2=.2, σ2=.3 gives w2=.018. The interval proxy is .14, not `.3−.2=.1`. Constant-tenor interpolation operates on w before dividing by the target T and taking a square root. A missing bracketing tenor yields missing or explicitly modeled extrapolation.

A 25-delta put IV of .30, call IV .24 and ATM .25 gives put-minus-call skew .06 and butterfly .02. Store .06 as a volatility fraction, not 6 in arithmetic. Reverse the skew convention only by changing its definition ID. Shift the source forward while holding each contract quote fixed: fixed-strike, fixed-k and fixed-delta changes must be distinguished. A near-expiry maturity rolling through a scheduled announcement must not smooth away the event unknowingly.

For Greeks, finite-difference checks cover each contract's valuation coordinate. A joint shock changes delta through `gamma*dS + vanna*dσ + charm*dt` to first order, under the declared sign/time convention; compare exact scenario repricing and second-order residuals where material. Time is an independent scenario dimension, not a division by price change. O05 keeps vega and volga/vomma as distinct sensitivities. O16 produces separate delta/gamma/vega/vanna/charm event-weighted CVD and true observed-event OHLC channels. Reprice aggregate exposure under joint shocks without equating option sensitivity with observed dealer hedge orders.

For VIX options, the nontradable index and changing terminal settlement mean an equity-style calendar constraint is not automatically valid across all expiries. O04 must validate each no-arbitrage restriction against the contract's actual payoff/underlying before projecting the surface. Never hide inconsistent quotes by forcing an inappropriate constraint. The bounded feasibility audit uses a declared simplified pricing convention to establish calculability, not official index parity or executable arbitrage.

## How these specialists improve the remaining stack

Keep physical-volatility competitors separate: seasonal range, Parkinson, Garman–Klass, Yang–Zhang, multiscale RV/semivariances, jump measures, HAR and ARCH/GARCH. Add microstructure-noise-aware sampling, robust tails, intraday seasonality and event-conditioned forecasts as independently ablated children. Do not feed the final volatility ensemble into an expert that already produced an input for that same ensemble; use primitive and later refinement ports in the DAG.

C06 consumes compatible physical forecasts to update joint future excursion/time distributions and L04 P-zone candidates. C15.LEVERAGE owns the restricted early C15.SCENARIO port defined in the computation schedule. O10/O12 consume those **primitive** joint surface scenarios to distinguish mechanical node migration from flow/holdings changes; their completed forecasts feed later C/L and X fusion without reverse same-batch feedback. G04/G05 require ordered dynamics or survival heads for reach/runner questions. P02/G06 evaluate whether improved volatility changes feasible stop, destination, fill or full-position value. A better IV fit alone is insufficient evidence that a location or trade improves.

For every child run quote/units/clock fixtures, prefix/restart tests, held-out reconstruction or proper forecast scores, OOF calibration and missingness stress, information/model-class ablations, joint interaction experiments, complete one-mini economic comparison and frozen future parity. Report results by year, session, expiry bucket, event proximity, volatility regime and coverage, using effective date/report counts. Small/rare groups receive shared parameters and explicit uncertainty. The registered experiments describe how to choose a supported version; none promises a globally best or permanently profitable model.
