# Policy, execution and account risk: individual experiment phases

All phase records below are **planned trading-system checks**, not completed test results. Each parent retains its ten-part specification; named refinements isolate particular information, definitions or model/policy alternatives. They do not imply one process or model per row.

[Phase rules and model quality](../SPECIALIST_EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM.md) govern chronology, metrics, interactions, useful effects, failure decisions and future testing. The [complete upgrade paths and specific child bindings](../UPGRADE_PATHS.md) and [implementation constructions](../UPGRADE_CONSTRUCTIONS.md) bind every unit below. Every listed local case needs separate named assertions at implementation; a test bearing the component name alone is insufficient.

| Unit | Kind | Individual target |
|---|---|---|
| [P01](#p01) — Action state and complete sequential policy | measurement-or-integrity | `Decision` and `OrderIntent` with preconditions, state/version, expiry and full future management-policy ID. Target `VALUE(action,h)` includes non-fill and the subsequent policy; abstain has zero trading P&L but logged foregone opportunities. |
| [P01.ACTION_STATE](#p01-action_state) — Finite one-mini action transitions | measurement-or-integrity | Exact feasible action set and sequential position/P&L path. |
| [P02](#p02) — Structural stop and full-position destination choice | policy-or-value | `BracketPlan(entry,stop,target,time_exit,geometry_version)` with planned gross/net risk and payoff distribution. Labels record barrier order, unresolved and gap ambiguity, not nominal RR as win probability. |
| [P02.BRACKETS](#p02-brackets) — Structural invalidation and destination choices | policy-or-value | Joint bracket net/duration/tail distribution. |
| [P03](#p03) — Full-position exits, protected structure and trailing value | policy-or-value | `ManagementDecision` links old/new stop/target, reason and confirmation time. Labels: realized net exit, all-trade MFE/MAE, giveback, duration and tail gap loss. Future maxima are labels only. |
| [P03.EXIT_VALUE](#p03-exit_value) — Full-position hold, protect and exit | policy-or-value | Incremental future net and occupied duration through boundary. |
| [P04](#p04) — Re-entry, idea memory and cumulative risk | policy-or-value | `RetryState(attempt,visit,loss_used,new_evidence,belief_revision)` and eligible/veto reason. Label incremental repeat value and entire parent-idea/day outcome. |
| [P04.REENTRY](#p04-reentry) — Idea lifetime and cumulative risk | policy-or-value | Additional attempt net, cumulative idea loss and opportunity occupancy. |
| [P05](#p05) — Event replay and passive-fill uncertainty | policy-or-value | `SimulatedOrderEvent` with scenario, possible/confirmed-under-assumption fill, price/time/cost and ambiguity cause. Queue position is latent, not ground truth. Labels include non-fill and adverse markouts. |
| [P05.CLOCKS](#p05-clocks) — Venue arrival versus information time | policy-or-value | Scenario marketable fills, shortfall and account net. |
| [P05.PASSIVE](#p05-passive) — Passive fill and adverse-selection uncertainty | policy-or-value | Fill probability/time and net conditional on fill/non-fill. |
| [P06](#p06) — Routing, latency, cost and adverse-selection experts | policy-or-value | Cost/markout distributions, fill probability and stale-intent expiry; P05 supplies scenario-conditioned fills. Target net action value includes probability mass for non-fill. |
| [P06.SHORTFALL](#p06-shortfall) — Cost and latency specialists | policy-or-value | Arrival/implementation shortfall and tail costs for each permitted order type. |
| [P07](#p07) — Broker order state, idempotency and reconciliation | measurement-or-integrity | `OrderEvent`, reconciled position and discrepancies, reconciliation latency and unprotected-exposure intervals. No prediction target for exact state handling. |
| [P07.IDEMPOTENCY](#p07-idempotency) — Order and broker reconciliation | measurement-or-integrity | Correct position/order state at every event and restart. |
| [P08](#p08) — Independent daily USD risk and pre-trade reservations | measurement-or-integrity | Approval/veto, reservation, halt/flatten events, planned versus realized overshoot and tail loss. Objective is bounded planned exposure; gaps can violate realized budget and must be reported. |
| [P08.RESERVATION](#p08-reservation) — Daily loss and atomic risk reserve | measurement-or-integrity | Correct headroom and feasible one-mini actions; separately verified firm floors. |
| [P09](#p09) — Firm product and account lifecycle constraints | measurement-or-integrity | `AccountState(rule_version,floor,headroom,eligibility,breach_reason)`; account survival, time-to-eligibility and actual cash timeline. Hypothetical eligible payout is not received cash. |
| [P09.ACCOUNT_PATHS](#p09-account_paths) — Product lifecycle and rule scenarios | measurement-or-integrity | Correct eligibility, breach, reset and payout-state transitions. |
| [P10](#p10) — Day boundary, outage and emergency flattening | measurement-or-integrity | Boundary plan/events, confirmed-flat timestamp, late/failed flatten, unprotected duration and incident report. A forced exit is costed in daily net. |
| [P10.REMAINDER](#p10-remainder) — Boundary-truncated opportunity and flattening | measurement-or-integrity | Feasible late-session net and confirmed flat by boundary. |
| [P11](#p11) — Trading net, business cash and objective accounting | measurement-or-integrity | Daily/monthly trading and cash ledgers, gross-to-net reconciliation, target gap, risk distribution, equity/floor paths and payout eligibility. One NQ $2,000 equals 100 net points; ES equals 40. |
| [P11.CASH](#p11-cash) — Trading net versus business cash | measurement-or-integrity | Mean net per eligible day and separate withdrawable/received business cash outcomes. |
| [P12](#p12) — Execution-instrument research selection | policy-or-value | Selection record with uncertainty, common and extended-cohort results and reasons. Targets are complete daily policy distributions, not normalized points alone. |
| [P12.FAIR_INSTRUMENT](#p12-fair_instrument) — NQ versus ES research selection | policy-or-value | Daily net/risk/account survival and data-supported execution choice. |
