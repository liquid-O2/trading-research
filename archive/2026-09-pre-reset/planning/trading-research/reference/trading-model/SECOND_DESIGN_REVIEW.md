## Preservation and improvement assessment

The final design preserves both Jumbo single-break mechanisms rather than using one fade/trend label; internal quarters/EQ/open versus edge extensions; prior RTH versus 06–09 anchors; news-delayed and later-session branches; minor profile/delta nodes; source-only SPX/SPY-to-NQ opportunities; ordinary/cohort/option-weighted CVD and within-bar paths; rich volatility alternatives; and distinct Response failure/retest/resqueeze/quiet/protection sequences. Source numerical claims are not calibration priors.

Improvements are concrete experiments: conditional and adaptive formation/zone models; exact traded profiles versus bar allocation; causal size-cohort paths; supervised next-report OI with uncertain intraday scenarios; surfaces and exposure-change attribution; local and joint strike/expiry/asset temporal patterns; and complete action/wait selection after fill/cost/remaining-room estimates. Each compares faithful causal rules, a simple statistical baseline and justified learned alternatives. Granularity supports error attribution; it does not require deployment of every specialist.

The task’s sequencing is maintained. Current implementation-ready focus is Context, Location and supporting data/options/cross-asset/selection/evaluation. The R cards preserve later mechanisms; P supplies a neutral full-position benchmark and operational contracts, with richer management alternatives left for the later experiments. Response absence is not a universal veto. One account and one mini are the sole primary economic unit.

No universal three-board agreement, largest-node-only selection, extreme-only location, gamma-sign day type, mandatory HTF confluence, fixed holding minimum, copied-account scaling, partial exits or post-loss recovery trade is imposed. Unobserved identity/depth and unprovided formulas remain unknown or explicit dependency-limited variants. Improvements of 10–20× remain the user's aspiration; there is no evidence here to quantify achievable improvement.
