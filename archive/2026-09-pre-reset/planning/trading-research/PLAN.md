## Deliverable 1 — Statistics, definitions and timing

For each research family, produce a report backed by reproducible tables containing:

- The actual computable definitions and meaningful source alternatives, their purpose and their observable limitations. Preserve original, corrected and candidate constructions when they represent different information or behavior; document invalid or undisclosed definitions without inventing a reproduction.
- Exact input fields, units, identities, formation windows, known-at times, revision policy and eligible coverage. Establish correctness with relevant independently specified examples and real-data reconciliation.
- Distributions, frequencies, conditional paths, excursions, temporal persistence and failure cases appropriate to the mechanism. Include missing, no-event, no-contact and censored outcomes explicitly.
- Comparisons of source timings/windows, sensible neighboring clocks and justified adaptive candidates. Discover choices on training/development dates and confirm on separate later dates. Report stability across years, sessions and supported regimes, with date/episode/report-aware uncertainty and sparse-support warnings.
- A readable result for each material question: what was measured, what was learned, what was unsupported, and the next comparison that could resolve uncertainty.

Report empirical findings from actual data. A schema inventory, a source-reading ledger or a passing synthetic test is supporting evidence with its own scope; it does not complete this deliverable. All families remain in the research queue even when one family is promising or difficult.

## Deliverable 2 — Independently evaluated Context

Build specialists or distinct prediction heads for the questions in the shared specification: range/path and session behavior, auction state and migration, participation/flow, physical and implied volatility, remaining movement, options/OI/exposure/node behavior, events/regimes and cross-market transmission.

Each meaningful target gets an individual result containing its input cut, population, baseline, tested alternatives, chronological evaluation, appropriate error/proper score, calibration or interval coverage, effective support and incremental information. Keep calculation quality, predictive quality and usefulness to Locations separately visible. Models with different targets remain independently evaluable even when they reuse features, a representation or a fitting tool.

Start with transparent frequencies or a suitable regularized statistical model. Compare a stronger information-matched learner when diagnostics or the data structure justify it. Compare measurement/representation changes separately from model capacity. Do not require every deterministic measurement to become a learned model, and do not stop after a crude baseline solely because it ran.

Use one reusable data/fold/fit/evaluate path. Transformations, discovered clocks, calibration and downstream stacking must respect the chronological training closure. Combine only compatible targets and score the final emitted predictions. A model can be accepted for the stated research use after these checks while its trading economics and future deployment remain untested.
