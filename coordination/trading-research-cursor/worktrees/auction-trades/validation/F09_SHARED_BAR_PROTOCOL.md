## Frozen inputs and outputs

Toy time is integer nanoseconds; prices are exact integer raw-contract ticks, sizes exact integer contracts, counts unique original whole events. Every definition binds raw instrument, source measurement/filter, calendar/alignment, reset epoch, bar kind, threshold and positive storage bounds. Quote points and supplied coarse bars are separate types. OI/cumulative daily options fields or quote size cannot be silently used as raw underlying traded volume.

Shared summaries retain OHLC, V and original count, priced/unpriced support, sum(pV), sum(p²V), known signed and unknown mass, history/order status and relative cumulative-flow extrema including initial zero. Quantity composition adds like units. Ordered CVD composition uses d=A.d+B.d, high=max(A.high,A.d+B.high), low=min(A.low,A.d+B.low). It is associative for compatible ordered blocks, not commutative. Cross-block timestamp ties require source order evidence; ID sorting does not provide it. Unordered price extrema/mass can remain available while ordered endpoints, CVD extrema and activity partitions are unknown. Missing history has no invented finite unobserved-mass bound.

Time intervals are explicit half-open F03 windows. The first actual eligible print defines observed open; missing early prints or covered-empty intervals never create prices. Finality requires interval close and available completeness watermark, while complete price/history coverage remains a separate flag. Actual publication is no earlier than the captured cut or any input. A final-only consumer cannot read provisional HTF values or future OHLC at a drawing coordinate.

Count, volume and range bars close at the first whole event reaching their frozen threshold; range means high minus low in ticks. Retain overshoot, source cardinality and completion delay. A reset closes only a nonempty pending bar as reset-partial, with no cross-reset pooling. Threshold updates require a new explicit definition/reset. A separate allocation plan must sum exactly to its original event, carry original availability, and remain non-interchangeable with independent trade observations.

Corrections create new published versions with the complete affected block/request lineage. Saved input-prefix cursors, including later admissions at an already captured cut, cannot alter old publications. Duplicate identity is idempotent only for identical immutable content. Capacity, malformed request and conflicting admission failures are atomic. Checkpoints retain original inputs and per-publication request/coverage/watermark/prefix evidence; restoration reconstructs arithmetic, threshold/reset partitions, publication order and canonical identities rather than trusting stored output fields.

The common layer may merge fully contained compatible final coarse bars. It rejects opaque bars straddling a requested boundary and cannot recover within-bar CVD order or a constituent body envelope lost during aggregation. Bar identity is independent of display visibility and contains semantic configuration; equal-valued consecutive intervals remain distinct observations. Thin downstream fixtures exercise finality, deduplication, strict versus inclusive contact and delayed confirmation without implementing hidden source predictors.
