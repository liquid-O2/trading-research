# B00.6 finite decision contracts — v2 draft

The direct governing scope is B00.6 and validation T-CAPABILITY, T-HORIZON, T-REWARD, T-STANDING-QUOTE, T-GEOMETRY and T-OI-ASSIMILATION. Twenty-five hand-derived cases are frozen only after root review and registration. No market replay or model fit belongs to this protocol. The original third-review finite arithmetic is read and preserved; it is not executed or edited. The implementation is a reusable exact contract kernel and independent literal fixtures. It does not implement all G06/G07/P01/P11/O07/O08/O21 mechanisms or finish their local phases.

Exact public inputs and outputs use signed-int64 nanosecond timestamps, integer or Fraction ticks and Fraction USD. Strings, floats, booleans masquerading as integers, nonfinite values, mutable nested collections and unexplained unit conversions are rejected where typed numeric state is required. Identity, account, raw instrument, scenario, reward basis and common account boundary are explicit. Pure functions return immutable values; invalid requests do not mutate a ledger.

## Frozen draft public contract

All 25 BC identifiers are retained with explicit fixture variants. The retained v1 draft is provenance only. The three new files are owned by /root/f04_batch_review. Feynman owns existing label/fold modules; their public valid-input ObjectTarget, ExactPoint, ObservationWindow and reference_object_label signatures remain compatible. `FeeCharge(id,amount_usd)` binds a nonempty immutable cash-charge ID to a nonnegative Fraction USD amount. `RewardDomain(account_id,raw_definition_id,scenario_id,reward_basis,common_boundary)` is an immutable shared domain. All identities are nonempty strings; boundary is int64. `NetMark(at,domain,gross_marked_usd,charges,max_fee_ids=256)` carries this domain, requires at<=boundary and an immutable tuple of FeeCharge. Equal fee-ID/equal-amount repeats collapse once; conflicting amounts fail. `incremental_rewards(marks,max_marks=64,max_fee_ids=256)` requires one identical domain, at least two strictly increasing marks, nondecreasing fee history with stable ID/amount binding, and limits both mark count and the unique fee union. It returns `IncrementalRewards(marks,increments,net_change,cumulative_charged_fees,interval_new_fees,max_marks,max_fee_ids)`, with net marks available as a property. Cumulative fees are the final distinct fee total; interval-new fees exclude fees already present at the first mark. No fee is subtracted twice. The BC07 preexisting-fee variant distinguishes these totals explicitly.

`RewardState(at,domain,state_version,position,pending_order)` retains the exact external state version and shared domain; its small position/pending fields do not replace the full external risk/budget state. `RewardSegment(id,initial,terminal,net_increment_usd,fee_ids)` inherits its single domain from equal initial/terminal domains, requires terminal>initial and terminal<=boundary, and accepts only a unique immutable tuple of fee IDs. `compose_rewards(segments,max_segments=64,max_fee_ids=256)` returns `RewardComposition(segments,initial,terminal,net_increment_usd,fee_ids,max_segments,max_fee_ids)`, preserving all segments for resumption. It requires a nonempty bounded tuple, unique segment IDs, exact consecutive RewardState equality, one domain and disjoint fee IDs across all segments. The net increments already include their fees. A zero-reward waiting segment must be supplied explicitly to bridge any gap; its endpoints still must match exactly. No missing gap is inferred.

`compare_complete_rewards(left,right)` consumes two RewardComposition values. It requires the same exact initial state/domain; each final at must equal the common boundary, position must be zero and pending_order false. It returns exact `left.net_increment_usd-right.net_increment_usd`. Domain/initial-state mismatch raises ContractError; a valid partial, nonflat or pending terminal composition raises DependencyUnavailable. Composition itself may retain these partial states for later continuation. Invalid terminal time beyond boundary remains ContractError. BC08–12 fix full states and domain-mismatch variants, plus explicit waiting and ineligible boundary states.

`EvidenceSeed(target_signature,atoms,known_at,likelihood_contract)` binds a unique immutable tuple of ultimate input atoms, an actual availability clock, the exact immutable target signature and the seed's explicit likelihood contract. The signature is the actual Target.signature, never its display ID. `EvidenceFactor(id,target_signature,atoms,payload_version,known_at,likelihood_contract)` uses the same signature; atoms must be nonempty, immutable and unique. A missing likelihood_contract may be represented as None on a factor, but admission refuses it with DependencyUnavailable. Other malformed factor fields raise ContractError. Seed likelihood is a nonempty string; empty seed atoms are valid.

`EvidenceAdmission(factor,outcome,admitted_at)` records only outcomes accepted or redundant_evidence. `EvidenceLedger(seed,seen_factors=(),known_at=None,max_factors=64,max_atoms=256)` defaults known_at to seed.known_at, otherwise validates the exact replay-consistent value. Its target_signature and likelihood_contract derive from its seed. Its accepted_factors and consumed_atoms are derived properties reconstructed from the seed and ordered seen bindings, never caller-supplied state. Unique seen IDs include both accepted and redundant factors. max_factors bounds all unique seen IDs; max_atoms bounds the unique consumed union including seed atoms. Each factor's atom tuple is also bounded by max_atoms. Every retained factor matches the seed's target signature and likelihood contract. Accepted factors consume disjoint new atoms; redundant factors consume no new atoms but retain their immutable ID/content binding. Partial overlaps always raise DependencyUnavailable. Distinct IDs alone never prove independence.

`admit_evidence(ledger,factor,cut=...)` returns `(new_ledger,outcome)`. It first validates cut>=ledger.known_at and cut>=factor.known_at, then exact target agreement and explicit likelihood compatibility. Future/backdated requests raise DependencyUnavailable; changed target raises ContractError. An existing exact factor returns `(ledger,'duplicate')`; conflicting same ID raises ContractError, including IDs first recorded as redundant. A new ID with all atoms consumed yields redundant_evidence, retaining its binding and advancing ledger.known_at to cut. A disjoint admitted factor yields accepted, extends the consumed union and advances known_at to cut. Empty seed/no history known_at is exactly seed.known_at; thereafter it is the last retained admission cut. Exact duplicates do not advance it. All invalid requests leave earlier immutable values unchanged.

The default and absolute ceilings are64 marks,64 segments,64 seen factors,256 consumed atoms and256 unique fee IDs. A caller may choose a smaller positive exact-int limit, never a larger ceiling; booleans and zero/negative/noninteger limits fail. Constructors and record restoration enforce these bounds before returning state. NetMark retains its fee bound; composition/incremental kernels reject any mark with a different configured fee bound. EvidenceLedger rejects mismatched limits on restore. No silently truncated state is returned. BC25 fixes concrete at-limit and over-limit vectors for every bound; all are small synthetic inputs.

All new immutable value types have public `record()` and class `from_record(...)`. Every record is a dict with exact keys `schema`, `type`, `fields`; schema is `decision-contracts-v1`, type is the exact class name. For FeeCharge, RewardDomain, NetMark, RewardState, RewardSegment, EvidenceSeed, EvidenceFactor and EvidenceAdmission, fields contains their exact declared constructor fields. EvidenceLedger records only seed, seen_factors, known_at, max_factors and max_atoms. RewardComposition records only segments, max_segments and max_fee_ids; IncrementalRewards records only marks, max_marks and max_fee_ids. Derived fields are never serialized. Nested value types use the same record envelope. Fraction transport uses reduced two-integer `[numerator,positive_denominator]` pairs, with gcd1 and zero encoded[0,1]. Tuples use JSON arrays. Unknown/extra/missing keys, wrong types (including bool integers), noncanonical rationals and schema/type mismatches raise ContractError. Record transport may be mutable but every reconstruction is newly validated immutable state; no record object is retained by reference. `version` hashes the canonical record. `EvidenceLedger.from_record(record,expected_target_signature,expected_likelihood_contract,max_factors=64,max_atoms=256)` requires exact caller-expected identity/likelihood/limits and replays each recorded admission, rejecting conflicting IDs, outcomes, clocks, overlap, missing/changed likelihood or mismatch in reconstructed known_at. `RewardComposition.from_record(record,max_segments=64,max_fee_ids=256)` requires exact expected limits and reruns composition from retained segments. `IncrementalRewards.from_record(record,max_marks=64,max_fee_ids=256)` likewise recomputes from marks under exact expected limits. No consumed-set, accepted-list, endpoint or total fields are accepted in these source-only records. BC25 resumes both admission and reward composition and compares original semantic versions, duplicate outcome and110 net total with the uninterrupted path.
