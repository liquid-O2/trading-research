## Source and timing contract

Shared capture validates actual supported TradeLedger/TransactionTapeView and exact requested raw instrument, aggregation unit, half-open interval, observed event prefix, decision cut and publication. Filter event_at <= cut without out-of-domain cut+1 arithmetic. Capture all resolved source versions including explicitly ineligible F05 rows, explicit excluded mass, correction impacts and exact WindowCoverage content. Reject unresolved eligibility, aggregation mismatch, conflicting support and malformed/unbounded input before mutation. Source domains qualify local order; a legacy provider-domain-unavailable stream cannot claim cross-feed order.

Coverage, unknown aggressor, missing price and order ambiguity are separate dimensions. Observed signed bounds use plus/minus observed unknown volume. Missing tape gives no finite true-flow bound. A completely observed quiet window is zero activity; no observed duration yields unavailable rates. Unknown-only tape is not quiet. Observed empty cohort is flat at its opening baseline; missing cohort coverage is provisional/unavailable. Nonzero production cumulative opening must come from authenticated prior same-anchor evidence or an explicit versioned mathematical offset, not invented observed flow.

Capture and all outputs are immutable. Revalidation uses the actual view at the original cut. Corrections generate new current provenance and leave historical publication unchanged; moving a corrected trade between anchors impacts both. Restart reconstructs exact source state and repeats the original capture/request, comparing complete output identity. Same numbers with another legitimate source version, coverage version or publication time are distinct receipts. Duplicate exact inputs may retry idempotently; conflicting same identities fail.

## Learned boundary

Pure fit kernels return unpublished state only. Public fitted cohort, residual or surface inference requires actual F11 SemanticArtifactStore/CommitRef, retained transform node, exact ReadRequest and TemporalFoldV1 population. The retained FitEvidence includes actual training ReadSession manifests, complete exact training memberships/groups, matured target-label references and every transitive fitted dependency. The adapter recomputes state from admitted retained training-read rows and compares exact transform payload. A caller fold ID or fabricated calibration table never certifies provenance.

Validate actual temporal fold population and dependency bytes at F11 admission. Before publication validate actual fit completion, schema, units, target/endpoints, original sample/date, cut, assembly, upstream fold routes and OOF exclusion through validate_oof_producer. A future fitted definition, held-out fit-feature read, in-sample learned ancestor, changed target, missing calibration lineage or final-fit substitution fails before numerical inference. Tiny synthetic fitting is allowed only within the later single shared verification. It does not close prior V01 repair obligations, chronological empirical calibration or alpha/economic gates.

Unsized arbitrary generators are rejected. Exact capacity and one-over failures are registered. No silent data truncation, rebucketing or loss of eligible opportunities.
