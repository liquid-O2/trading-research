# Research architecture and phase boundaries

The intended NQ decision process has separate responsibilities: understand context, identify useful locations, evaluate response, choose an entry and manage its risk. Development phases establish and test these responsibilities in order. A source setup can contain some context already; the later context expert adds a forecast/selection layer without erasing the setup's required predicates.

| Responsibility | Current evidence or planned output |
| --- | --- |
| Source setups and objects | Phase 1 complete acquired census; source facts and disclosed inferred reconstruction remain distinct. |
| Better individual rules | Phase 1.5 finite reconstruction/breadth/refinement and unchanged-entry exits; currently specified. |
| Context and forecasts | Phase 2 jointly fitted intraday volatility, native options/OI updates, auction/flow/cross-market and method-specific experts; currently specified. |
| Actionable location quality | Phase 3 planned scope: native areas, arrival, conditional reaction, competing levels and P-zone upgrades. |
| Response and final entry | Phase 4 planned scope: fitted response/entry experts, context/location integration and combined NQ replay. |
| Learned management | Later recommendation, with fixed-entry controls first; no numbered Phase 5 commitment. |

A location may be on asset A, a useful response on B and execution on NQ. Other assets supply permitted custom gamma/vega/vanna/OI/rolling-volume/VWAP/prior-extreme areas and flow; they do not run cloned Jumbo/Green Bird/Sires strategies. Native coordinates and evidence clocks stay attached to every record. Source-linked NQ strategies keep their source prerequisites.

One fitted volatility expert combines GK,YZ,HAR and IV features and issues multiple intraday/remaining-session forecasts. Different major mechanisms and source methods retain separate fitted artifacts and diagnostics. Later published OI may train a historical model only after publication; an intraday OI estimate is uncertain model output, not observed dealer inventory.

Definitions: an **opportunity** is a causally qualifying rule observation, not an executed trade; **current state** uses available observations; a **forecast** predicts a later target; a **conditional plan** recommends branch/reference/confirmation/timing with alternatives, uncertainty, expiry and invalidating evidence. **Coverage** describes what was observable, independently of whether an opportunity succeeded. A complete no-arrival horizon is a negative arrival observation; reaction conditional on that nonexistent arrival is undefined.
