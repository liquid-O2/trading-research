# Jumbo’s Judas and two single-break classes

Family: **day-type**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[Time-Based ranges Framework (JJumbo).pdf, pp.8–14,17–24,36–38](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/Time-Based%20ranges%20Framework%20%28JJumbo%29.pdf#page=8) distinguishes: Judas first leg after 09:30 followed by reversal around 09:40–09:50; single break after an already-extended overnight range, using internal levels and reduced projections; single break after overnight liquidity was already purged, often compressed, using internal continuation and larger extensions. News can delay or spoil the illustrated sequence. AM balance/PM expansion and AM expansion/PM balance are separate hypotheses. [xfcmg2.pdf, pp.7,15–18,23–24,42; posts 2093335135789719861,2075602926215561312,2074569053239226627,2064008375751311653,1974149966609400120](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/xfcmg2.pdf#page=7) supplies width, prior value, open position, untouched/swept edges and OP cartoons. OP is not automatically the next 09:30 open.

## 2026 open-location switch

The source's switch is conditional: [10 July](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2075602926215561312) associates an RTH open inside prior value/range with mean reversion and failed breakouts; [28 July](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2082104746077245851) discards mean reversion and range double breaks when the context aligns. Its [clarification](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2082205480877781167) names the RTH open relative to prior-day value/range and the current 6–9. On [9 July](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2075238880060571966), an outside-both long example adds above-average RVOL and a claimed 76% one-way A-period (09:30–10:00), without return to the OR low. That percentage and the RVOL/event interpretation require independent measurement through the named variants in [SPEC](../SPEC.md#ev-range-and-open-location-switch).

Preserve open-in versus open-out as a measured context switch, with mixed, boundary and unavailable states. Keep in-value and in-range double-break expectancy as separate marginal and joint tables. Use width, overnight balance, current 6–9 position and known-then RVOL as evidence; final A-period direction remains an outcome. [EV range](ev-range-expected-move.md), overnight H/L and EQ supply location context, not an automatic fade.

The [27 August](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2093020177696755878) claim that windows between 05:00 and 09:00 behave similarly motivates a named **5–9 ≈ 6–9** comparison. It does not replace the locked 6–9 benchmark. Small-range double-break % is not evidence of edge; compare width and balanced/imbalanced overnight populations, including single breaks that hold.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Range geometry, overnight purge history and open versus prior value can be measured. ES/YM/RTY synchronized histories support sister-index nonconfirmation on their valid overlap. Event calendars are usable only with availability metadata.

Store source class evidence as separate booleans and times before producing an exclusive label. Every eligible session receives a faithful label, unresolved-definition label, or unmatched label with reason. Do not force all days into three stories. Store width, balance/extension measures, clean edges, open-relative-to-prior-RTH-value and sister SMT as known then. Final path labels are outcomes, not pre-open inputs.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **quantile-width-labeler**: discovery-frozen narrow/normal/wide thresholds.
- **path-first-labeler**: classify first break, return and second break before attaching overnight state.
- **purge-sequence-labeler**: explicit prior-session sweep and clean-edge sequence.
- **clock-grid/formation-bar-labeler**: measure sensitivity without changing the source label. No trained day predictor in Phase 1.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Class counts, unresolved/unmatched coverage, agreement matrix against faithful labels, path-class separation, timing and year stability. Count every failure described in the manual, including no rejection, momentum through projections, late/early reversals and absent volume signature.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
