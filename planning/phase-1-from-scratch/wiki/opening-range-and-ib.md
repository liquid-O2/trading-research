# Opening ranges and initial balance

Family: **day-type**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

**Jumbo 2026 role: 5m/15m reference only.** His [19 May clarification](https://x.com/JJumboFX/status/2056797248604815569) says he sometimes references the 5m or 15m OR; the illustrated main box is a time-based range. OR/IB is secondary in this Jumbo framework. The separately attributed IB/ORB constructions below remain other-source measurements, not Jumbo's main operating box.

[tpo-lesson-3.pdf, pp.8–10](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/tpo-lesson-3.pdf#page=8) defines IB as the first RTH hour, A+B. [IB ORB Live Stats.txt, L10–21,177–254](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses NY09:30–10:30 IB and 15m ORB; size z/percentile and final-extreme formation order inform a descriptive contingency. Equality counts as break and same-minute ties favor high. [IB ORB Statistical Mapper hardcoded.txt, L15–34,167–243](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses different hardcoded ORB/IB populations and the first outside bar close. [Initial Balance Statistical Mapping.txt, L10–64,268–358](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) supplies eight hardcoded shape categories and a retest tolerance tied to 0.1% of price.

[Initial Balance & Extensions with Stats by DOW.txt, L245–558,603–696](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses exchange08:30–09:30, current-inclusive z, next-IB excursions and a backward break scan. [Initial Balance & levels with stats.txt, L516–581](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) instead counts strict breaks in a separate 09:30–16 window. [NQ Stats Initial Balance.txt, L123–129](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) predicts from the first outside close without event tracking. [NY 5m and & 15m Orb Statistics & LTF Candle structure.txt, L449–803,873–1189](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) adds 5m/15m ORBs and hardcoded validation labels. [Weekly Initial Balance.txt, L38–116,161–360](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) displays successive individual Mon/Tue sessions, not a cumulative two-day IB; its weekday rates are shares of hit bars.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Minutes permit full-session counts and formation order with ties unknown. Trades resolve ties. Historical weekly information is permitted without overnight positions.

Keep NY-RTH, literal exchange-clock and source first-bar variants separate. Capture O/H/L/C, final-extreme order known at formation end, close half, width class and later break paths. Preserve all neutral/doji/no-break categories. Weekly intended aggregation stays unresolved.
