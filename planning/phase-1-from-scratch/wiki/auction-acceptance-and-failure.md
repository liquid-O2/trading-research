# Acceptance, failed auctions and distinct 80% claims

Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[amt-lesson-1.pdf, pp.7–9](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/amt-lesson-1.pdf#page=7) describes exit from prior value then re-entry, with an asserted80% opposite-edge traverse and a stricter hold-inside branch. [amt-on-live-markets.pdf, pp.3–11](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/amt-on-live-markets.pdf#page=3) separates depth from acceptance and conditions on POC crossing/rejection. [mastering-amt-vp.pdf, pp.9–12](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf#page=9) gives a narrower failed auction: established balance→departure→older balance POC→instant rejection→current balance edge. Ordinary break/retest does not qualify. [only-trade-big-trades.pdf, pp.10–12](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/only-trade-big-trades.pdf#page=10) uses an older minor node/balance as a related variant.

[mastering-amt-vp.pdf, pp.3–8,18](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf#page=3) separately discusses time spent in balance and an open-outside-prior-value rule requiring two consecutive30m periods inside before a far-edge target. [your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf, pp.12–14](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf#page=12) labels72–80% as unaudited experience. These are distinct claims and denominators, not one “80% rule”. [reading-the-volume-profile.pdf, pp.5–11](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/reading-the-volume-profile.pdf#page=5) contrasts repeated POC failure→same-side edge with aggressive crossing→far edge.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

VP plus time-ordered executions permits causal acceptance signatures. TPO periods support the two-period branch. OHLC approximate variants require explicit limitations.

Keep five identities: balance occupancy; generic exit/re-entry; hold-inside re-entry; open-outside/two-period reacceptance; older-POC rejection back to current balance. Store significant-volume, dwell, retest and crossing observables before outcomes. Include accepted break/retest and fast traverse without new value as alternatives.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **dwell/volume-normalized acceptance**, **trade-ordered crossing**, **POC versus older minor-node anchor**, **40/68/70 value**, **clock/activity-bar confirmation**, **no-POC-filter ablation**.

## Phase 1 outcomes

For each eligible trigger: POC/far-edge/current-balance return, rejection depth/time, accepted departure, fast traversal, nonreturn and censoring. Report union and conditional denominators separately, with failures and yearly support.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.

## Definition questions
