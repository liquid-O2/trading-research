## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Trades and MBP-1 executions support size, side, price and pace. Actor identity, actual held positions and hidden inventory are not observed.

Preserve print-level versus cluster-level objects and source thresholds by instrument/session. Body/wick location is only known at closed bar. A large print is effort evidence; subsequent result is counted, never assumed.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

## Phase 1 outcomes

Print/zone density, later contact, effort-to-displacement, body/wick reaction, repeat tests, no-result, departure and no-return. Include failed large prints and zero-signal sessions.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
