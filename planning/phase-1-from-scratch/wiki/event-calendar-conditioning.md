## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Use only acquired schedules and release-known values. Final/revised macro readings and later earnings records need as-of labels; unknown timestamps cannot become earlier predictors.

Emit event kind, scheduled/actual time when known, pre/post distance, session calendar and previous available rates/volatility. Do not design a macro data platform or inherit a source news-day trade ban.
