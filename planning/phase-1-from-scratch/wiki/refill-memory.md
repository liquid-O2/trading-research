Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[refill-effect.pdf, pp.2,5–10,14,16–18,22](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/refill-effect.pdf#page=2) defines print-cluster zones, departure and return, prior holds/defense, construction size/width, location and arrival flow. It reports199m NQ/MNQ ticks,235RTH sessions,41,152 touches and 42% hold; clustering/rearm/hold horizons are absent. Raw-flow AUC.54, memory/location.63 and placebo.51 are source claims. A contaminated memory feature and weak latest-quarter results are disclosed. [origin-of-the-move.pdf, pp.15–18](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/origin-of-the-move.pdf#page=15) repeats related findings and distinguishes causal touch grading from an unsuccessful entry finder. [18k-payout-session.pdf, p.11](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/18k-payout-session.pdf#page=11) compares steady versus shrinking/slowing refills. [anatomy-of-a-losing-start.pdf, pp.3–7](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/anatomy-of-a-losing-start.pdf#page=3) and [trapped-buyers-one-retest.pdf, pp.3–9](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/trapped-buyers-one-retest.pdf#page=3) preserve same-zone identity and earlier failed tests.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

NQ trades/MBP-1 support price-zone memory and BBO response. NQ/MNQ are separate instruments; their prints cannot be silently pooled. Available inventory must establish any MNQ sample before using it.

Assign stable zone and touch identities. Prior touch counts stop at arrival time. Store created, departed, returned, invalidated and nonreturning objects. A later hold or first-touch outcome cannot enter its own memory. Source AUCs do not authorize fitting a grader in Phase 1.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **size/pace/width-normalized clustering**, **age-decayed memory**, **prior-day versus same-day history**, **first/second/third touch strata**, **location/no-location and memory/no-memory ablations**.
- **tick-ordered arrival**, **time/volume/dollar windows**, **matched-density controls**. No learned touch grader.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Hold/break at each defined horizon, depth before hold, prior-touch conditional tables, return/nonreturn, confirmation delay and yearly/regime stability. Report all failed touches, not eventual winners only.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
