# Origin-of-move, control changes and role flips

Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[origin-of-the-move.pdf, pp.2–14](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/origin-of-the-move.pdf#page=2) describes catalyst/first aggression, earlier failed attempts, release, wall, return through catalyst, lower refill, later reclaim and re-squeeze. Passive failure can precede fresh aggression; the stages are not interchangeable. [only-trade-big-trades.pdf, pp.7–16](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/only-trade-big-trades.pdf#page=7) distinguishes failed-start/retest OFM, immediate squeeze and balance fade without required fresh opposite aggression. Nonreturning breaks are explicit. [18k-payout-session.pdf, pp.5–8](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/18k-payout-session.pdf#page=5), [a-clean-continuation-short.pdf, pp.10–11](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/a-clean-continuation-short.pdf#page=10) and [whos-in-control.pdf, pp.3–9](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/whos-in-control.pdf#page=3) show cross/retest/control renewal and failed alternatives. [origin-of-the-move.pdf, p.18](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/origin-of-the-move.pdf#page=18) discloses later-day leakage in an earlier profitable entry finder and negative causal out-of-sample results.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Trade flow, confirmed structures and BBO proxies can describe the sequence. Actual trapped positions/forced liquidation are not observed.

Store catalyst origin time separately from its later confirmation, each cross/retest/renewal and eventual outcome. Keep immediate squeeze, failed-start OFM, passive reclaim and balance fade as distinct source variants. Do not reuse an old entry engine or backdate later confirmation.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **causal stage labeler**, **trade-efficiency/control threshold**, **passive-versus-aggressive renewal**, **memory/location ablations**, **time/trade/activity-bar scale**.
- Preserve a raw sequence record so alternative labelers share the same path outcomes.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Creation/confirmation, return/nonreturn, role-flip hold/breach, second reclaim, excursion/delay and yearly stability. Count failures and incomplete sequences; no entry, stop, R-multiple or P&L.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.

## Definition questions
