## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Every required CVD construction can feed the same event definition once its input is valid. Confirmed swings impose a delay; continuous excursions need no hindsight pivot.

Store matched extrema and their known_at, normalized price change, CVD change and own-baseline distance in separate units. Keep regular extreme divergence, effort/no-result and rolling baseline state distinct.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Event counts, disagreement across CVD variants, confirmation delay, conditional rail/zone reaction, continuation/reversal excursion and yearly stability. CVD state is a descriptor, not a Phase 1 trade signal.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
