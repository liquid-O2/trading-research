# Overnight rails, gaps and reference-touch tables

Family: **day-type**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[mastering-amt-vp.pdf, pp.14–18](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf#page=14) uses overnight18:00–09:30 and claims94% RTH touch of either ONH or ONL, and 73% midrange MPOC touch when RTH opens inside prior ETH balance. MPOC here is midpoint, not VPOC. [mastering-amt-vp.pdf, pp.19–25](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf#page=19) reports ES 2021–24,1040 days split260 per quarterly contract group. It includes prior close/H/L/IB/VA relationships; single/joint ON and prior-reference touches; gap/half-gap fills; IB nesting/breaks; close outside after one-sided break; and RTH volume/range distributions. Some conditional denominators are unstated. “PST” RTH06:30–13:00 must not become a fixed UTC clock. Printed probabilities and Gaussian-looking drawings are unverified claims.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

ES/NQ minutes offer long reference samples. Value/VPOC requires profile data on its own coverage. Futures rollover and contract identity must be explicit.

Recreate every appendix table family with its source instrument and then a named NQ transfer variant. Preserve either/both/individual edges, gap up/down, open inside/outside and conditional versus joint n. Store volume/range histograms empirically; do not inherit ES point thresholds for NQ.
