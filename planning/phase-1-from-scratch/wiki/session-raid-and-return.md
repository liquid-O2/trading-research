## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Minutes support breach/re-entry with same-bar uncertainty. Trades give first passage. No source code execution is needed or planned to establish definitions.

Keep raw breach, first re-entry, maximum pre-re-entry excursion, midpoint return, no-return and no-break. Retain source-as-coded precedence as a diagnostic branch; never present inferred same-bar order as observed.
