# Absorption as an observable sequence

Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[Time-Based ranges Framework (JJumbo).pdf, pp.31,35](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/Time-Based%20ranges%20Framework%20%28JJumbo%29.pdf#page=31) shows Absorption Zone+ body threshold0.6 “as % of range”, volume1.5×14-period average. Unit and inclusion details are missing. [xfcmg2.pdf, pp.26–29,44–46](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/xfcmg2.pdf#page=26) shows absorption at internal rails and imbalance inside absorption candles.

[dom-lesson-6.pdf, pp.3–7](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/dom-lesson-6.pdf#page=3) defines heavy aggression without follow-through; declining-aggression exhaustion and a single stopping-volume burst are distinct. [stop-re-entering.pdf, pp.3,9–16](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/stop-re-entering.pdf#page=3) gives four stages: aggression/hold; maintained/growing replenishment; aggressor fading/delta flip; opposite aggression with price reward. Three ticks of replenishment and 2–4 upticks of price reward are different statements. [your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf, pp.3–5,8–14](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf#page=3) also says replenishment within three ticks and shows a3-tick-move caption. Its27%/72–80% figures are unaudited experience. [average-unprofitable-trader.pdf, pp.23–26](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/average-unprofitable-trader.pdf#page=23) and [ny-am-session.pdf, pp.3–10](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/ny-am-session.pdf#page=3) retain pacing, earlier failures and later confirmation.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

MBP-1 supports signed executions, displacement, spread, best-price persistence and displayed renewal. It cannot prove full-depth stacking or exact iceberg reserve. OHLC-volume Absorption Zone+ is a separate baseline.

Persist each stage onset/confirmation/expiry separately. Stage4 must not be available at stage 1. Keep continuous effort, result and quote-response fields before thresholds. Preserve source declining-flow, replenishment-failure and huge-delta/no-extension meanings under separate names.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **trade-efficiency absorption**, **BBO-resilience confirmation**, **volume/session-normalized effort**, **pacing ratios**, **stage-duration**.
- **OHLC-body-volume versus trade-zone**, **time/trade/volume/dollar/range bars**, **fixed/developing/no-location ablation**, **threshold-frozen sequential labeler**.
- No learned response or reversal model is trained in Phase 1.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Stage transition and failure counts, confirmation delay, hold/break, post-stage excursion, repeated tests and year stability. Count unconfirmed stalls and false confirmations, not only completed reversals.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
