# Inside/outside bars, round rails and candle patterns

Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[8020 System.txt, L1–489](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) is a round-hundred±20 rail/40-point-band system, unrelated to AMT80%. It defines no-wick repair; three-candle forks with≤0.5 body/range,3tick outer match and 4tick deeper center; two directional bodies≥0.6range and 40 ticks with≤2tick junction; body gaps≥4 ticks; confluence within8 ticks and A+ clocks09:30–11/15–16. [Inside and Outside bar stats.txt, L52–80](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses inclusive inside, strict outside, omits dojis and includes an immature final signal. [Reversal Breakout System Outside Inside Bar with Stats.txt, L85–221](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses strict inside bars, mother H/L but inside-candle color, and first subsequent chart close outside without expiry; it contains no outside-bar algorithm.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

OHLC reconstructs closed-candle objects. Trade data resolves same-bar touches. Round levels are numerical geometry, not liquidity or reversal proof.

Preserve exact pattern tolerances, doji/equality conventions, source color field and near-edge fill. Keep overlapping mother references and unresolved patterns as explicit states.
