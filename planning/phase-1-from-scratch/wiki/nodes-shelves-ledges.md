Family: **auction-order-flow**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[vp-lesson-2.pdf, pp.3–9](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/vp-lesson-2.pdf#page=3) distinguishes HVN buildup, LVN traversal/reaction, shelf zones, ledge boundaries and naked prior-session POCs. Several local ledges can exist within one profile. [the-math-behind-auction-market-theory.pdf, pp.12–14](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/the-math-behind-auction-market-theory.pdf#page=12) requires two supported balances around a true LVN; an unsupported thin tail is distinct. Shelves taper; ledges cut off abruptly. [reading-the-volume-profile.pdf, pp.5–11](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/reading-the-volume-profile.pdf#page=5) describes double-distribution shelf holds and accepted breaches. [10k-first-month.pdf, pp.5–8,12–13](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/10k-first-month.pdf#page=5) and [a-clean-continuation-short.pdf, pp.4–9](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/a-clean-continuation-short.pdf#page=4) show minor/yearly nodes and warn through an example that a post-trade discovered confluence was not known earlier. [your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf, pp.6–7,10–13](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/your-mistakes-with-absorption.pdf#page=6) compares fixed edges/minor nodes against developing VA and middle balance.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

Trade VP supports signed/unsigned row geometry. OHLC-synthetic VP is an explicit lower-fidelity comparator. MBP-1 suffices; no hidden-book premise.

Retain node bounds, support on each side, peak/trough/gradient, confirmation time, profile anchor and test history. Freeze identified shelves/ledges; distinguish them from a developing POC. Naked status ends at the first defined revisit but the object remains in history.
