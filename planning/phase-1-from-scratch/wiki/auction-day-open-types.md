# Auction day and opening behavior labels

Family: **day-type**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[amt-lesson-1.pdf, pp.10–14](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/amt-lesson-1.pdf#page=10) defines trend, normal, normal variation, neutral and non-trend days. First30m opens are drive, test-drive, rejection-reverse and auction. Developing shape can change later. [mastering-amt-vp.pdf, pp.19–23](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/mastering-amt-vp.pdf#page=19) has a different four-class appendix; its class percentages are not exhaustive. [average-unprofitable-trader.pdf, pp.21–24](../../../sources/documents/discretionary/average-unprofitable-trader.pdf#page=21) uses first 30m TPO entirely above prior VAH and the developing profile around10:00 as an observed sequence.

## Computability and faithful reconstruction

OHLC, trade VP and TPO provide different coverage. Final shape requires the completed window; early snapshots retain known_at.

Keep AMT and Jumbo taxonomies separate. Store primitive early range, IB extension, close location, profile migration, recrosses and tests. Retain unmatched/ambiguous labels rather than forcing exhaustive source categories.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **threshold-frozen AMT labeler**, **continuous shape descriptors**, **snapshot-at-30/60/120m**, and fixed-clock/trade/activity-bar sensitivity.
- Compare label agreement and descriptive path separation; do not fit an opening/day predictor.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Eligible label coverage, disagreement matrix, class counts, later path and year stability. Show overlap between taxonomies without merging their meanings.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.

## Definition questions

Q07 defines wide/narrow, shallow pullback, hold/test/recross and class precedence. Final outcomes cannot define earlier features.
