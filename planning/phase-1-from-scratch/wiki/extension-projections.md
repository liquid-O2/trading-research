Family: **Jumbo**. Measure faithful objects; experiment with named upgrades in Phase 1.

## Evidence and source rules

[Time-Based ranges Framework (JJumbo).pdf, pp.8–11,17–21,32–35](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/Time-Based%20ranges%20Framework%20%28JJumbo%29.pdf#page=8) shows range-based projections, including 0.5 beyond an edge and 1.33–1.66 areas. [xfcmg2.pdf, pp.9,25,31,33,48; posts 2082134071702860091,2062826544381215027,1993371273934606757,1990858695702442275,1884378289189364205](../../../sources/documents/jumbo/xfcmg2.pdf#page=9) distinguishes extensions from P-zones.

[6 to 9 Session and Levels.txt, L299–317](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses edge±1.33W/1.66W. [CISD with fib or range extensions and stats.txt, L1–430](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) has distinct fib and range anchors, with close-within-0.5ATR hits. [Open Source Fractal - Customized.txt, L94–104,557–581](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Open%20Source%20Fractal%20-%20Customized.txt) calls CISD-anchor multiples −1/−2/−2.5/−4/−4.5 “deviations”; they are not statistical SD.

IB/ORB price-percent projections are distinct from width multiples: [Initial Balance Statistical Mapping.txt, L44–52,395–429](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) and [NY 5m and & 15m Orb Statistics & LTF Candle structure.txt, L140–147,275–304](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip). [Range Projections Statistical Levels.txt, L44–105,273–301](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses weekday price-percent quantiles at session open. [Session Range Projections with stats.txt, L317–351,1049–1085](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) uses both wick width and an envelope of individual candle bodies. [magic_hours, L664–723](../../../sources/documents/indicators/Pinescript-indicators--main.zip) bins first-break extensions and later opposite runners; its file ends incomplete. ## Computability and faithful reconstruction

OHLC supports reach with ambiguous intrabar order; trades support first passage. Price-percent, ticks, width multiples and empirical SD must retain distinct units.

Persist anchor price, coordinate origin, multiplier, side, source variant and frozen width. Reconstruct each published coordinate literally; unresolved Jumbo label conventions stay flagged. Extensions are the extension family, never ATL or P-zones. A rail touch is not a reversal.

## Upgrades to measure

These are proposed experiments, not attributed source rules. The user requests stronger objects and independent comparisons. [Develop Trading Model.md, L13–31](../../../sources/documents/conversations/Develop%20Trading%20Model.md)

- **common-coordinate** geometry comparison without renaming source variants.
- **prior-excursion-quantiles**, **GK/RV-scaled**, **tick-scaled**, **source-width-SD versus excursion-SD**.
- **trade-touch versus close-tolerance**, **running-max versus current-bar extension**, **aggregate-body versus body-envelope**, and fixed-clock/bar variants.

## Phase 1 outcomes

Touch, first passage, pre-return overshoot, re-entry, internal/open/opposite-edge return, no-return and timeout. Compare equal horizon and starting distance; report projection coverage separately from post-touch rejection.

Use [the common measurement contract](measurement-contract.md) for availability, denominators, discovery and confirmation. A source percentage is a claim to recompute. It is never a pass threshold.
