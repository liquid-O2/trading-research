# Source mechanisms and Phase 1 dispositions

This is an evidence crosswalk, not an implementation-unit catalog. Each mechanism record is the linked wiki page. Its original definition has raw path/page/line citations; its faithful reconstruction, MBP-1/inventory limits, named upgrades and counted outcomes are separate sections. Multiple source variants of the same mechanism share one page and one outcome contract. Tickets are derived later from these records.

**Disposition:** measure now means a source-defined object can be measured with acquired data after its listed definition blockers resolve. Experiment now means a named upgrade is compared on the same outcomes. A blocked-definition row is not scientific completion. Deferred mechanisms retain a reason and emitted prerequisites.

- Learned Context classifiers, adaptive selectors, HMM fitting, learned touch grading and the learned reversal/P-zone model: Phase 2/3. Phase 1 emits the inputs and descriptive outcomes in [the one-page handoff](wiki/deferred-context-location.md).
- Execution, sizing, stops/targets, P&L, prop-account replication and psychology: later execution/risk work. Source anecdotes do not validate the research target.
- Exact all-depth stacking, hidden reserve and trader identity/intent: not identifiable from MBP-1. Measure named best-book proxies; do not require MBP-10/MBO.
- Undisclosed Jumbo P-zone, screenshot-node and SessionStat branches: definition-blocked, with experiments specified. Unsupported VOLI/intraday VX or unproved acquisition remains data-blocked, not fabricated.
- ZIP README and bundle metadata: provenance only, no trading mechanism. Source assistant plans and secondary findings are not approved algorithms.
