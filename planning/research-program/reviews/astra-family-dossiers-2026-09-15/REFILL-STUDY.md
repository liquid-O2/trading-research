## 1. THE AUTHOR'S METHOD AS STATED

- R1 R — Sires × TeamVOT study, July2026: cluster large aggressive prints at an area to form a zone; price must leave and later return. Each later touch is an event. Large print alone is not a trade. The mechanism hypothesized is passive defenders replacing consumed orders inside the zone. REF pp.1,4–7. Identical defender identity/inventory is explanatory, not directly observed by aggressor prints.
- R2 R/D — Freeze pre-touch memory (earlier same-session and prior-day defenses), construction (number/size/span), location (value edges, session extremes, distance from open), flow/state (arrival delta/speed/balance). Roughly20 features, exact transformations, zone clustering and hold-label definition unpublished. Prose examples60/80/100 contracts “in seconds” do not supply one mandatory threshold; the chart plots prints≥40. REF pp.5–9.
- R3 R/E — Train in the past, grade future touches, retain the full cohort and compare selected versus all. Reported199M NQ/MNQ aggressor ticks,235 RTH sessions,41,152 touches, Dec2024–Nov2025. Unselected holds42%, fade every touch−.285R after costs. Hold rate worst→best decile25%→63%; AUC.63, shuffled.51, flow-only.54; memory/location dominate. These are hold/classification metrics, not deployed trade win rates. REF pp.3,8–9,23.
- R4 R/E — Tight scalp brackets were discarded when tick replay exposed dependence on optimistic same-bar ordering/fills. An eventual winner’s MEDIAN dip is18 ticks past touch. Rest inside the zone rather than buy the first touch at market; the proposed mechanism is to be one of the replacing passive orders. REF pp.10–11.
- R5 R — Published deployed configuration: limit12 ticks inside selected zone, stop32 ticks, target96 ticks, cancel after30 minutes, one position at a time, one tick round-trip modeled cost plus one tick stop slippage. No discretionary target or trailing rule is specified for that research configuration. Original classifier, exact zone-side normalization and full selected-order ledger are not supplied. REF p.12.
