## 1. THE AUTHOR'S METHOD AS STATED

- S01 R/D — One operating loop, several executions: define a thesis and its death condition, trade while alive, recreate after death. C1 pp.3–4 allows structure break, value building somewhere new, or major news to kill it. A long/short/neutral thesis records validity band, reason, confidence1–5 and objectives. C1 pp.5–7 compares ES/NQ/YM first use of prior balance/refill objectives; a correlated market consuming its objective matters. This is a discretionary market interpretation, not stop-distance sign or microbalance position.
- S02 R/D — Map the current auction, not any stale box: prior/current/composite balances, naked POC, shelves, ledges, HVN/LVN, actual extremes and untouched destinations; choose profile horizon for the trade (VP2 p.6: five-minute scalp versus weekly swing). HVNs attract/stall; LVNs may reject or traverse quickly. Avoid balance-center chop for the strict absorption reversal; a POC can still be a destination or another setup’s context. AMT1 pp.3–13; VP2 pp.3–8; ABS pp.5–8; CONT pp.4–8.
- S03 R/D — Keep auction routes separate. Balance fade: edge→confirmed rejection→POC/reassess far side. Accepted breakout: participation and acceptance outside→broken-boundary retest→next value. Reacceptance: return/accept inside→opposite edge. Narrow MAMT failed auction: established balance→break→tag an older separate balance POC→instant rejection→return to the original boundary (VAH for return from above, VAL from below); follow the labeled original balance, not a directionless “prior POC” formula. AMT1 p.7 uses failed-auction language more broadly. MAMT pp.9–12.
- S04 R/D — TPO uses 30-minute periods, A9:30/B10:00; time POC is maximum time occupancy, not volume POC. Single prints are internal one-period rows, excess is a terminal tail of at least two single-period prices; poor extremes lack completed excess and are especially useful on NQ. IB is the first hour. ON is18:00–09:30 ET. MAMT’s MPOC statistic refers to overnight midpoint, not VPOC. TPO pp.3–9; MAMT pp.14–26. C3 p.7 separately recommends a40% intraday VA, while introductory lessons use70%; preserve the profile variant.
- S05 R/D — Read VIX, implied-range completion, event expansion/crush, curve/VVIX and gamma before choosing ambition/branch. VIX/√252 estimates daily percentage volatility, not a calibrated high-low range. Native QQQ0DTE complex/flip/walls informs NQ; reread after a large impulse. BIG pp.14–18 uses aggressive OFM in short gamma and balance fade in long gamma. GEX p.18’s long-gamma fade asks for own aggression, whereas BIG p.15 explicitly does not: retain source variants. No unanimous gamma label should be fabricated for K18 p.4’s provider/author disagreement. VIX4 pp.3–9; GEX pp.4–20.
- S06 R/D — Premark the location and read participation there: reset DOM, distinguish executed hits from displayed additions/cancellations, watch pace, spreading/thinning and effort versus price reward. Avoid chasing a thin book or widened spread. DOM6’s300 buys across3ticks and DOM7’s20/30 displayed with hundreds executed or10,000 reserve/500 shown are illustrations, not NQ universal thresholds. An iceberg hypothesis needs executed depletion→replenishment→other participation within the illustrated two-tick area; cancellation before execution does not prove spoofing intent. DOM5–7 pp.3–7.
- S07 R/D — Strict absorption reversal: fixed real extreme→opposing aggression gets no result against passive defense→new controller obtains reward within the three-tick neighborhood→fresh retest/renewed defense, with CVD supporting the read. A developing VA/POC is not the fixed extreme. ABS pp.5–13. STOP pp.6–15 separately orders defense→replenishment→opposing prints thin→absorber turns aggressive/lift-off; reward2–4ticks, entry1–2ticks from confirmation, repeat all checks on re-entry, daily stop−4R. Three-tick proximity is not a count of three depth events.
- S08 R/D — Footprint: bid-left/ask-right diagonal comparisons, typically3–4×; FP8 p.6 says3+ adjacent rows, p.7 says2–3. At an allowed location, candle direction opposing delta can indicate absorption; confirm the POC moving WITHIN that candle and the selected DOM/delta read. A changed POC between final candles is insufficient. FP8 pp.3–7; FP9 pp.3–7. Neither CVD median units/reset nor Speed of Tape(10) settings are disclosed.
- S09 R/D — VWAP is a volume-weighted mean, not POC. Select session or justified event/weekly/monthly anchor; wait at least1σ, preferably2–2.5σ, then absorption/rejection plus DOM/CVD/context. Do not fade merely because a band is touched, particularly if a stronger draw remains beyond it. Target mean or actual thesis objective. VWAP pp.3–8; chart settings show1σ/2σ enabled and3σ disabled, so the drawn example is not a2.5σ parameter receipt.
- S10 R/D — Big Trades marks effort, never an entry alone. NQ40-range example filters30–60contracts per print, adjusted for session volume; hollow white buys versus pink/purple sells. Body prints have result, wick prints lack it. Same-price350% imbalance wording does not settle3.5× versus350% MORE. Both sides absorbed inside balance: wait for control. BIG pp.3–6; OFM p.4. These are distinct from FP8’s diagonal ratios and Refill’s≥40-contract research detector.
- S11 R/D — Aggressive OFM: repeated unrewarded aggression builds catalyst→first squeeze releases and fails→return/reclaim of catalyst/refill→renewed initiative takes intervening wicks→drive’s retest defended→entry, stop beyond the aggression/intermediate wick; target the predetermined HTF objective, scalp working zone1–3R, then chosen trail. Failure point alone is not the preferred trigger. Earlier refill entry is explicitly optional/higher risk. Replay short uses a stop-limit below the wick so initiative must trigger it. BIG pp.7–14,18; OFM pp.5–13; CONT p.10.
- S12 R/D — Passive OFM: failed squeeze without aggression at the failure, tape dies, demonstrated long triggers above buyers with stop below aggression;1–3R scalp zone. OFM p.14. Clean squeeze: fast release without the prior failed attempt and, in CONT p.11, **no retest**. Its direct continuation is not the same as the preferred aggressive-OFM retest. OFM p.5’s plain-squeeze schematic is a related but separately illustrated entry.
- S13 R/D — Long-gamma balance fade (BIG): repeated unrewarded aggression at the extreme→leave→retest still unpaid→fade toward the opposite side’s prior control. No squeeze or own-side reward is mandatory in this branch. A rare later burst inside balance can justify holding farther. BIG pp.14–16,18; “80 percent” and A++ frequency descriptions vary (once/twice a week, twice a month, every couple of days), with no common measured denominator.
- S14 R/E/D — Continuation/re-entry at an established defended refill/minor-node band requires fresh same-side defense and thesis alignment; a strong buying close above a short thesis extreme instead permits a retest long toward VWAP. Microbalance continuation freezes the small price balance, breaks in the existing thesis direction, risks behind that structure, and targets the existing HTF objective. KG1 retest uses a supplied source level plus aggression; its engine is unpublished. ANAT p.7; K18 pp.7,11,14; K2345 pp.4–7; CONT pp.4–10; NYAM pp.8–9.
- S15 R/E/D — Structure sets invalidation→size fits account risk→HTF objective supplies reward, not a desired R:R generating structure. C3 p.3 states consistent0.5–2% risk per trade, while individual funded examples have their own smaller dollar caps. C2 p.4 calls for40–80 trades of winner MAE/overall MFE; p.7 asks100+ trades for expectancy, not the same sample requirement. C1 records30 sessions. Monte Carlo/fractional Kelly are process suggestions without a complete sizing algorithm. C2 pp.4–7; ANAT pp.8–10.
- S16 R/E/D — Choose an account policy: funded static R:R/one partial versus personal trailing/runner choices. C2 p.5 offers alternatives:100stop/200target→at+100 move BE or logical+50; after6–10points trail about3 behind; or50% off at1R and BE remainder. Do not force all three into one mandatory sequence despite p.8’s compressed closing slogan. Protect completed structure/high-delta control, not the newest wick; K18 requires a prior low to break AND close with aggression before the intervening high is protected. Add only once earlier risk is secured. RD pp.4–8; C3 p.4; K18 pp.8–14.
- S17 R/E/D — A stopped trade need not kill the broader band; reset location→reward→delta for a fresh attempt inside it. No inherited confirmation or reset daily loss allowance. Avoid revenge, FOMO, premature BE, attachment to a dead thesis and euphoria; stop at the selected account limit. EMO pp.3–8: ten-minute preparation, yesterday’s value/naked POC/VIX/day type, one-sentence A+ definition, log deviations, close laptop at limit; distinguish compliant losses from bad process. Full-session replay/journal, confidence/regime/all trades, costs, MAE/MFE and one-variable revisions support improvement. C1 pp.6–7; C2 pp.4–7; STOP pp.6,14–15; ANAT pp.7,10.
