## 1. THE AUTHOR'S METHOD AS STATED

R=author instruction; E=illustrated case; D=discretion not fully specified. These are branches of a contextual practice, not instructions to trade every level every day.

1. **R/D Context.** Establish directional opportunity from time-based levels and the current auction; a sweep alone is not the signal. Wait for failure and a destination. September 8 reply 2097205824611377354 says failure then objective; September 10 permits small non-A+ longs, so “no sweep = not A+” is a quality condition, not a prohibition of all un swept trading (GB 18, 21–23, 37–40).
2. **R/E References.** Completed NYAM 09:00–10:00 highs/lows are available after 10; completed previous-hour extremes, prior-day/week/month levels, Asia/London extremes, midnight true-day open (TDO), and Friday-close/Sunday-open gap (NWOG) are separately selected. November 27 defines weekly range from the previous completed weekly candle. The Asia box is drawn approximately 20–00; London 02–05 is visible in April 23 and September 14 charts, not a universally typed rule. Do not use a finished box before completion (GB 20–23, 27, 30–33, 38–40, 45; P14 photo 1).
3. **R/D Formation.** High sweep and inability to remain above→short; low sweep and reclaim→long. PDL/PDH or higher-period confluence improves a particular context. TDO confluence is required in the named Asia/TDO example, not every Asia failure. An opposing-liquidity path supplies the objective (GB 19, 23–27, 30–33; P 14).
4. **R/E Confirmation and entry.** September 1 PDL sweep requires a completed 5 m close below; reply says wait a few points and use a retracement for low-risk entry. September 8 Asia/TDO reply requires 5 m close below TDO. These do not publish a universal close duration for every branch. November 20 figure shows short 2 MNQZ 2025@25301.75 at the high sweep; subsequent MSS/FVG annotation is not a prerequisite for that displayed entry; exact fill time is absent (GB 23–25, 19, 31, 43).
5. **R/E Golden-pocket failure.** Select the actual impulse. A down-leg retracement band is `[L+.50(H−L),L+.618(H−L)]`; September 1 combines its PDL sweep with 5 m failure. August 12 combines bullish HTF fib context, a failed breakdown/reclaim and the 10 am hourly low. A band touch alone is not this failure setup; “discount” in the bearish reply is the author's own wording, not a reliable substitute for direction-aware geometry (GB 23–25, 35, 50–51).
6. **R/E Structural stop.** September 1 says above PDL; another reply says the case's swing high. November 27 says above PWH. September 1 cash-open long risks the lows. The actual protected/recent swing can differ from the entire excursion extreme; stops are not universally one tick beyond its farthest wick. September 8 draw stop 29748.25 lies below the earlier wick>29770; September 3 stop 29255 lies below~29265. No general initial-stop selector is published (GB 25, 27, 32, 40; GB 16/26 crops).
