## 1. THE AUTHOR'S METHOD AS STATED

- K1 R/D — Keani’s own setup is embedded in Sires’s coaching case. Prepare daily references, 30-minute TPO, prior value and poor highs/lows; define a discretionary box and a conditional thesis before timing an entry. Stay within the chosen model, not arbitrary trendlines/footprint noise outside it. AVG pp.3,7–10,19,27–30.
- K2 R — Open fully above yesterday’s value: the entire A period remains above prior VAH. Wait around 10:00 to inspect the developing day type/profile. A is 09:30–10:00 in companion TPO p.3, but “around” does not specify a latest entry cutoff. AVG p.21; p.22 schematic has no A label proving this condition by itself.
- K3 R/D — Within that bullish auction value builds higher; observe rejection at developing POC OR prior VAH, then a break of current developing VAH with aggressive buying imbalances. The p.22 caption says POC AND previous VAH, differing from p.21 prose; preserve the discrepancy and use the explicit OR reading as the declared branch. It is not a break of A-high or a test of developing VAL. AVG pp.21–22.
- K4 R — Price returns to THOSE imbalance prices; buyers defend them, with DOM and time of day supporting the trade. Enter long toward a worthwhile preselected HTF objective. No actual fill price, exact imbalance ratio/stack count, generic stop distance, target-R or 60-minute retest expiry is printed for this setup. AVG pp.21–22.
- K5 R/D — Risk must survive 2–3 unnoticed losses from the trader’s own behavior; size by demonstrated quality, keep the objective logical, and do not let a cash-profit wish choose the target. The static $200 example is criticized. Three-confluence $150/four-confluence $300 and 20-trade 70%/5R comparisons are hypothetical illustrations, not validated Keani sizing constants. AVG pp.11–16,27–29.
- K6 R/E — Qualitative thesis before quantitative testing. Separate absorption fades from trapped-seller continuations; collect roughly 10–20 observations per feature, test one change at a time (20–30 trials is also discussed), screen-record full sessions and freeze the pre-entry read. The own-sample claim that 27% of absorption entries fail without 3-tick reward is a coaching statistic with no n, not this opening setup’s win rate. AVG pp.19–26.
- K7 R/D — DOM sequence is location→pacing→digits: effort must produce reward; examples describe sellers going from 2/3-digit to single-digit prints and buyers reaching 4/5 digits. Value-to-value return “60–80%” is an estimate, not a guaranteed target. Review journaling, assumptions, obsolete books and comparison-driven model hopping. No mechanical universal volume cutoff or order type is supplied. AVG pp.7–10,24–30.
