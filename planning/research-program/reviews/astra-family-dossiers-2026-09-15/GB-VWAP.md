R=stated; E=example; D=discretionary/unpublished. This is one explicitly described continuation example, not his ordinary time-level reversal.

Author says he consults VWAP for continuation and normally trades reversals at time-based levels. Reset, included sessions, price estimator, wick/touch tolerance, number of retests and exact fill clock are not published. Nothing in the case sets a 60-minute retest deadline or requires another close above VWAP after touching (GB 33).
4. **E/D Stop and result:** a 30-point stop is reported. The February 24 caption says 150 points; the February 25 reply 2026623532096971050 says “30 point stop, 100 point win.” Retain both statements and their provenance; neither is a universal target, required R multiple or proven fill ledger (GB 33–34). General target and stop-selection algorithms are unpublished.
5. **R/D Management, sizing, session:** the case says take the continuation; no case-specific contracts, partial schedule or exact session cutoff. Broader GB practice includes smaller size for lower quality, partials/BE/runners and stopping after enough; these are attachments, not mechanical VWAP entry conditions (GB 22/25/35–40; September 14 post 2099513366326730859). February 8's 09–11 EST preference does not introduce a 60-minute deadline (GB 33).

Therefore do not call 24787 the 30-point stop or 25016.75 a realized target. Numbers were read from magnified figure/axis/ticket crops, not page preview.
7. **E Other charts delimit this method:** September 14 London/Asia failures are reversals; September 11 CPI golden-pocket continuation has no VWAP requirement. September 2/10 scalps are not this trigger. The other 48 unique GB figures and seven photos contain independent evidence of those branches and account cards, not additional explicit VWAP case prints (GB 16–32/34–60; P11/P14 photos, full register in manifest).
