# Shared engines and mathematical definitions

All definitions below are research choices unless marked user-required. Parameter changes require a new variant hash. Decimal constants are exact decimal values, not binary-float comparison tolerances. Prices are stored in integer ticks; continuous statistics remain float64 until an order/reference is converted with the specified rounding rule.

## E00. Time, units, nulls and decisions

Use half-open formation and entry windows `[start,end)`. A bar covering `[09:30,09:31)` becomes available at 09:31. A decision at an entry-window end is excluded; an outcome exactly at a named horizon is included only if its execution timestamp is <= that horizon. Features and labels occupy different tables. Never join an end-of-day label into a morning feature row.

Use `d=+1` for a long/reaction upward, `d=-1` for short/reaction downward; `a` denotes aggressor direction with the same sign convention. `tick` and `multiplier` come from raw contract metadata. NQ's standard tick is 0.25 point and multiplier is $20/point; verify contract metadata rather than copying these units to another instrument. [CME NQ specifications](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/nasdaq/e-mini-nasdaq-100.contractSpecs.html).

Typed result states are `eligible`, `not_eligible`, `unavailable_input`, `warmup`, `censored`, `ambiguous_order`, `no_event` and `event`. `no_event` requires a complete eligible observation window. SQL nulls, NaN and unknown input cannot be coerced to false. A conjunction with a required unknown input is unavailable even if a later outcome happened to be favorable.

For continuous upper thresholds round away from the anchor with ceil-to-tick; lower thresholds use floor-to-tick. Midpoint entry limits round toward the less favorable fill: a buy limit rounds down, sell limit rounds up. Protective stops round outward from the protected structure. Preserve continuous and rounded values together. An exact order price is always tick-aligned.

Each immutable object has `object_id=sha256(instrument_key,type,formation_start,formation_end,config_hash,version)`, `born_at`, `available_at`, geometry, input hash and lifecycle fields. Updating a developing profile produces a new snapshot version with a parent ID; it does not rewrite an earlier snapshot.

## E01. Clock ranges and reference lifetimes

For complete formation bars, `H=max(high)`, `L=min(low)`, `W=H-L`, `EQ=(H+L)/2`, `Q25=L+0.25W`, `Q75=L+0.75W`. `build_open` is the first trade/bar open at formation start; `finish_open` is the first trade at or after formation end **within that first minute**, with actual availability. A missing first minute is unavailable; do not replace it with the previous close. All high/low-derived geometry is frozen at formation end. W<=0 is ineligible.

Outer projections are `U(k)=H+kW`, `D(k)=L-kW`; the baseline ladder is `{0.1,0.2,0.3,0.5,1,1.33,1.66,2}`. These are independent levels, not a daily OR. A projection's width is the width of its own parent range.

All research wall clocks below are **America/New_York with date-specific DST**. They are chosen research clocks; source screenshot axes do not establish a universal timezone.

User-required active high/low lifetime: from its formation end, a high is consumed by the first execution at or above H, a low by the first execution at or below L. Equality counts. A jump across a level consumes it with `cross_without_exact_print=true`; this does not establish a limit fill at the skipped price. Scan **all** subsequent tradable observations, including ETH, independently for both sides. A high already consumed at 02:00 cannot be a new target at 09:30. Unknown coverage leaves freshness unknown. Close-break and strict-sweep fields are separate and can occur later than consumption. Do not let a forming reference visit itself. Expire remaining session references at the next corresponding completed reference, or after five trading sessions for explicitly retained old references, whichever comes first; the card may choose a shorter lifetime.

The geometry remains available for historical path statistics after consumption. A first visit may start a pending failure or breakout episode. The episode can confirm after the line ends. It cannot spawn a second “fresh sweep” of that ID. Profile bands, open-price lines, model projections and FVGs use E02/E09 lifecycles and are not automatically retired by this high/low rule.

## E02. Interaction, reaction, sweep failure and area state

`TOUCH(B,t)` means an execution inside closed band B, or a price transition crossing B. Save whether actual prints occurred inside. With bars only, span overlap is a descriptive touch; any required intrabar order is ambiguous. Tolerance pads each edge by `tol_ticks*tick`, default one tick for recipe interactions. **Tolerance never changes the E01 exact first-visit lifetime.**

`EPISODE(B,d)` starts at the first eligible touch and freezes the band snapshot. All consecutive in-band prints/bars belong to that episode. A reusable area may start another episode only after price has stayed entirely outside the padded band for 60 seconds and reached at least four ticks away from it. Re-entry without that separation is still the same episode. External H/L references do not rearm; later retests belong to the original parent episode. Areas expire at RTH close unless a card specifies earlier. A sustained accepted break invalidates an area in that direction; new area IDs require genuinely new, already-observed formation data.

`REACTION(B,d)` observes from touch for `confirm_minutes` (default 5). For d=+1 require a completed one-minute close >= upper(B)+`response_ticks*tick` (default 4); for d=-1 require close <= lower(B)-response. The first such close confirms. Before it, a close beyond the far protective edge by `invalidation_ticks*tick` (default 2) fails the episode. Same-close confirmation/invalidation conflicts fail. If neither occurs by the deadline, expire without confirmation. The structural extreme is the min low/max high observed from touch through confirmation; it is available at confirmation, not at touch. Stop is that extreme plus two outward ticks by default. An overshoot-and-return can confirm; do not require all bodies to stay on one side of a line unless the card selects strict rejection.

`SWEEP_FAIL(reference,side)` requires that reference was fresh immediately before its first visit. High-side sweep direction is a=+1, reaction d=-1; low-side sweep a=-1, d=+1. After first visit, require an execution strictly beyond the edge by at least `sweep_ticks` (default 1), then a complete `close_minutes` candle (default 5, clock aligned) closing back inside the frozen range by `inside_ticks` (default 1), before `fail_deadline_minutes` (default 30) from first visit. Save first visit, strict sweep, confirmation and maximum excursion separately. A first equality visit consumes the reference even if the subsequent strict sweep never occurs. The same pending episode can include that later strict sweep. If the opposite range edge is visited before confirmation, flag `opposite_consumed_before_confirm`; baseline single-failure trades reject that episode. Stop is the full excursion observed before confirmation plus the card's buffer; local-control stops are separate E08 variants.

`HOLD(B,d,n)` starts after a qualifying completed close beyond the band on side d and requires n subsequent **complete consecutive one-minute closes** still beyond that edge. Equality is not beyond. Gaps are unavailable. The qualifying close itself is not counted among the n subsequent closes. The hold becomes known at the last required close. For an inside hold, each close must lie in the closed frozen interval; wick excursions are recorded but do not fail a close-only hold.

For n=0 the hold completes at the qualifying close itself. Unless a card declares another duration, a reusable profile/memory area's accepted-break invalidation requires 30 subsequent complete one-minute closes beyond its adverse edge by two ticks. A shorter reaction failure ends the current episode, not automatically the entire reusable area. Statistical P-zones, FVGs, microbalance boundaries and explicitly specialized S07 areas retain their stated alternative lifecycles.

`BREAK_RETEST(B,d)` requires a completed close beyond B by `break_ticks` (default 2), HOLD outside for `hold_minutes` (default 3), then the first later touch of B from the breakout side within `retest_minutes` (default 30), then REACTION in direction d within five minutes. Do not count the breakout bar as the retest. If a close returns beyond B's opposite edge before confirmation, fail. Stop is the retest excursion plus two ticks. If the card explicitly uses a strict 30-minute hold, that overrides the shared three minutes.

For repeated attempts, number distinct episodes, never near-level bars. Record `touch -> observation -> confirm/fail/expire`, with terminal timestamp and reason. A confirmation can reset consecutive failure counts only when the card says so. An unfinished final attempt is censored, not assigned zero response.

## E03. Profiles, nodes, ledges, shapes and TPO

Volume profiles use executed quantity at every integer tick from low to high, including zero-volume ticks. `V[p]=sum(size at p)`, `D[p]=buy[p]-sell[p]`; unknown-side quantity remains in V. Profile snapshots include only events with availability <= snapshot cutoff.

`VP70`: POC maximizes V. On a tie choose the price closest to trade VWAP, then the lower price. Start value area at POC and expand one adjacent tick on whichever side has larger V. On an exact tie add both sides in the same step, including zeros, until cumulative quantity >=0.70 total. Do not trim back the final tied pair to force exactly 70%. VAL/VAH are included tick centres. For volume=0 the entire profile is unavailable. Save achieved volume fraction and tie decisions.

Scope IDs: `prior_rth`, `rth_developing`, `j69_frozen`, `overnight_frozen`, and `balance_composite_N` (last N complete RTH sessions, same raw contract). Developing profiles refresh on each completed minute, are unavailable during the first five RTH minutes, and retain all earlier versions. Unless a card says otherwise, a balance uses N=2 with adjacent prior VAs overlapping by at least 50% of the smaller VA width and their POCs no farther apart than 25% of the mean VA width. Failure of this gate means no balance, not an arbitrary prior-day fallback.

Smooth the dense V array with a triangular kernel of odd width s (default 5 ticks): weights `w(j)=(m+1-|j|)/(m+1)^2`, j=-m..m, m=(s-1)/2. At boundaries renormalize available weights. Let v denote smoothed volume. Use type-7 quantiles of **all** support ticks, including zeros. A plateau is represented by its middle tick, choosing the lower when even.

- HVN candidate: local maximum in ±4 ticks and v>=q75. Prominence is v minus the larger of the minimum v on each side within 8 ticks. Require prominence >=0.25*v. Edge candidates without both shoulders are excluded. Expand its band while v>=v_peak-0.5*prominence, at most 8 ticks each side.
- LVN candidate: local minimum in ±4 ticks, v<=q25, and the maximum on each side within 8 ticks >=2*max(v,1 contract). Require both shoulders. Band expands while v<=v_min+0.5*(min(left_max,right_max)-v_min), at most 8 ticks each side.
- Shelf: a maximal contiguous run of >=4 ticks with v>=q75 and coefficient of variation <=0.25; evaluate maximal runs after splitting at any below-q75 cell. A zero mean cannot qualify.
- Ledge: adjacent smoothed cells with `abs(v[i+1]-v[i])/max(v[i],v[i+1],1)>=0.60` and the high-volume cell >=q75. Band is those two tick centres padded by one tick. Store which side has higher volume. Merge overlapping same-orientation ledges; outer bounds form the merged band.
- “Minor” node: HVN whose integrated band volume is <0.50 of the dominant HVN band's volume. This is a research definition, not a universal exclusion of all POCs. A card must explicitly request minor nodes.

Confluence is actual closed-band overlap after padding each object by the card's tolerance, or a declared signed distance. Default nearest-node selection minimizes distance from the primary reference, ties by stronger prominence, then lower price. The selected profile snapshot and node are frozen at first interaction; later totals cannot validate an earlier node.

TPO uses 30-minute RTH periods and a price row of `row_ticks` (default 4 ticks). Row index is floor(price_ticks/row_ticks); each period contributes at most one count to a row visited by executions. A bar-span implementation is a named approximation and is not pooled with tick TPO. For every row retain the set of period letters, not only its count. A tail is an edge-connected run whose rows each contain the same single letter; exclude the **entire** tail from interior single prints. Interior single-print zones are maximal adjacent rows with the same singleton letter, length >=2 rows. Poor high/low means the corresponding outer row contains >=2 distinct periods. Excess means a tail length >=2 rows. A poor extreme is geometry/context; any target at its external high/low obeys first-visit retirement.

Profile shape is a deterministic research label on a completed VP: normalize price x=(p-L)/W and compute volume-weighted mean, variance and skewness (variance zero -> degenerate). Classify in this precedence: B if two HVNs separated by >=0.30W each contain >=20% of total volume in their bands and intervening valley <=0.5*min(peaks); P if POC x>=0.65 and volume below x=0.5 <=35%; b if POC x<=0.35 and volume above x=0.5 <=35%; D if POC x in [0.35,0.65] and absolute skewness<=0.5; otherwise other. This replaces unresolved visual labels with testable features. A developing shape has its own as-of label; a final shape is not available earlier.

## E04. Statistical boundaries, EV, adaptive value and P-zones

All sample histories contain exactly N eligible **prior** completed same-clock sessions; default N=120 unless a card uses 60. Type-7 empirical quantile is `x[floor(h)] + frac(h)*(x[ceil(h)]-x[floor(h)])`, h=(n-1)q on zero-index sorted observations. Mean/median and quantiles are separate outputs. Require all N; do not substitute a partial 20-session warmup.

For each historical session i with open O_i and later H_i,L_i: `u_i=H_i-O_i`, `d_i=O_i-L_i`. At current anchor O, mean bounds are `O+mean(u)` and `O-mean(d)`; median bounds use medians. Define `min_excursion_i=min(u_i,d_i)` and its mean/median. This is the **per-session** smaller excursion; it is not min(mean(u),mean(d)). Export both if testing that explicit alternative.

Scale-normalized EV baseline uses prior session returns: `u_return=u_i/O_i`, `d_return=d_i/O_i`. Current expected upper/lower distance is `O * statistic(return)`. Volatility adaptation multiplies both by `s=clip(RV_pre_today/median(prior_N_RV_pre),0.5,2)`, where `RV_pre=sqrt(sum(r_1m^2))` in the complete 06:00–09:00 formation and `r=log(C_t/C_prev)` using only contiguous closes. Current future RTH returns never enter the scale. The unscaled historical-return model is the baseline; adaptive scaling is a preregistered variant. Zero or incomplete RV is unavailable, not s=1.

An inner-value range is **explicitly** VP70 from the completed J69 trade profile: VAH/VAL/POC with E03 ties. Its projection width is `VAH-VAL`, centre/reference is POC, and finish-open is separate. It is a testable value model, not a reconstruction of the source's unknown blue line. Compare outer-price-width versus inner-value-width ladders as separate geometries.

P-zone baseline at a declared finish-open O builds side-specific historical normalized excursion quantiles: T1=[q50,q65], T2=[q65,q80], T3=[q80,q90], T4=[q90,q97.5]. For an upper zone use O+O*q; for a lower zone use O-O*q with sorted endpoints. The same volatility scale s can be applied only in the adaptive variant. Zones may lie inside the completed range. Their baseline geometry freezes at anchor and does not trail price. An optional update variant recomputes s on completed 15-minute prefixes using a separate matched-clock historical RV ratio, creates a new version only for untouched zones, and never moves a touched episode's geometry. Invalidation is two complete consecutive one-minute closes beyond the far edge by two ticks, or session expiry. Do not define zone validity from the final daily high/low.

Model grids may replace quantile-pair sets with the complete named alternatives in the card; never optimize each endpoint independently to fit a screenshot. EV extension k uses `(upper_EV-lower_EV)*k` from the respective EV boundary, explicitly separate from W69.

## E05. Footprints, delta, VWAP and bar observations

Footprints aggregate buy A[p], sell B[p], unknown U[p] inside complete clock-aligned candles. Candle POC uses total volume and E03 ties. `delta[p]=A[p]-B[p]`; candle delta is the sum. Unknown-side quality must pass D02. Never use candle color as delta.

Diagonal buy imbalance at p compares A[p] with B[p-tick]; sell imbalance at p compares B[p] with A[p+tick]. Require numerator >=`min_cell` (default 10 contracts), denominator >0 and ratio >=`ratio` (default 3). Zero denominators are undefined, not infinity. A stack is >=`stack_rows` (default 3) consecutive tick rows of the same imbalance direction **within one candle**. Freeze its band at candle close; activate for later revisits only. Store every contributing pair. A separate zero-denominator extension must have its own name and is not baseline.

Intrabar POC flip uses prefix snapshots every one second inside a candle. A flip requires the prefix POC to cross a specified line by at least one tick and stay on the new side for the next two completed one-second snapshots. It becomes available at the last confirming snapshot. A final candle POC cannot be backdated as a flip. Delta disagreement at a level compares local delta in the padded band with candle/body direction as of the same completed observation, preserving positive/negative local cells independently of net candle delta.

VWAP uses executions: `mu=sum(v*p)/sum(v)` and population weighted `sigma=sqrt(max(sum(v*p*p)/sum(v)-mu*mu,0))`; bands mu±k*sigma. This is volume dispersion of traded prices, not volatility of returns. Numerically stable weighted Welford accumulation is equivalent and preferred. Session anchor is the scheduled Globex open for ETH or 09:30 for RTH as card-specified; weekly anchor is the first scheduled Globex session of that exchange week. A confirmed-pivot anchor can only become tradable when the pivot is known; all statistics replayed from its historical anchor retain `available_at>=pivot_confirmed_at`.

Small-body/high-volume observation: on a complete bar, `body_ratio=abs(C-O)/(H-L)`; zero-range bars are ineligible. Volume baseline is the arithmetic mean of the previous n complete bars, excluding current, carried across session boundaries unless the card explicitly resets. Body <=b and volume >=k*baseline are inclusive thresholds. This observation does not by itself prove absorption or reversal.

## E06. CVD, flow baselines and absorption/reward

CVD at t is sum of signed executed volume since the selected session anchor. Unknown volume is tracked separately. Invariants: terminal CVD equals total buys minus total sells; partition concatenation equals a single pass; prefix append does not change prior CVD; an aggressor sign reversal negates CVD. No expected crossing rate or candle-color agreement is used as a validity test.

Build flow baselines from nonoverlapping 30-second windows in the previous 60 complete sessions, grouped by 15-minute New York wall-clock slot. Each window records buy/sell/total quantity, trade count, size quantiles, absolute price travel and signed displacement. Include a zero only when feed health establishes no trading. Require all 60 sessions and >=120 valid windows in the slot. Quantiles use E04. For a longer/shorter observation duration use that same duration in both training and live windows; no square-root shortcut for volume thresholds.

F10 uses a distinct `extreme_band_delta_1m` baseline. In each valid completed one-minute candle in the matched 15-minute slot of those 60 prior sessions, centre a closed five-tick band on that candle's high for a candidate high, or low for a candidate low. Sum signed executions inside the band during that candle only. Defender direction is d=-1 for a high and d=+1 for a low. The historical sample is `max(d*band_delta,0)`, including verified zeros; retain at least 120 valid candles with every one of the 60 sessions represented. The live candidate uses its own pivot candle and identical band/duration/side convention, requires `d*band_delta>0`, and compares it with the card's type-7 quantile of that historical sample. Side coverage must pass D02 within each band. Neither the recognition candles nor the escape/quiet interval contribute to candidate delta. This five-tick aggregate must not be compared with a single-price or full-market volume distribution.

`ABSORB(B,a,t0)` freezes B at its first eligible touch. Observe `[t0,t0+flow_seconds)` (default 30 seconds) with actual completion time. Let Q_a be aggressor-a executed volume **inside B padded by two ticks**, Q_-a opposite volume there, and total include unknowns. Require Q_a >=the q85 of same-side full-market volume in the matched historical duration/slot, Q_a/(Q_a+Q_-a)>=0.65, at least three signed trades, and side coverage>=0.95. Compute adverse-to-defender excursion from the first in-band trade to the most extreme subsequent trade over the entire observation window, including outside B: in direction a it must be <=`max_excursion_ticks` (default 4). The observation is a named absorption proxy. It cannot exist before observation end. Follow it with a separately measured REACTION in direction -a; do not include that later reward in the absorption feature.

`REWARD(print,a)` begins after the print/flow observation becomes available. Initial reference price is its volume-weighted executed price in B, frozen at completion. Favorable reward is the first subsequent executable price at least `reward_ticks` (default 3) in direction a, before price goes `adverse_ticks` (default 3) against a and before `reward_seconds` (default 60). Ties in an unordered timestamp group are adverse-first. Rewarded band is the original print band, not a future price extremum. For defenders after ABSORB use direction -a and keep the two roles explicit.

Large trade means a **single execution** size >=the card's threshold; splitting or summing executions is not allowed for that baseline. Alternative size threshold is prior 60-session same-slot execution-size q99, with a minimum 20 contracts. Store a print separately from a cumulative volume cluster.

Delta divergence uses two E08 confirmed same-side price pivots. For bearish divergence price makes a higher high by >=2 ticks while CVD at the second pivot is <=CVD at the first minus the prior-60 same-slot q50 absolute five-minute CVD change. Bullish mirrors. Evaluate only when the second pivot is confirmed; a later pivot/print cannot retroactively be a signal. CVD range break uses the completed prior 15-minute CVD min/max and an expansion of at least the same q50 threshold.

## E07. Displayed reload, refill and catalyst sequences

`RELOAD(B,a)` concerns aggressor a hitting the opposite resting side at a fixed price p inside B. At distinct, strictly increasing timestamps record displayed queue q_before, aggressive executed volume x at p, depleted q_after and subsequent displayed q_rebuilt. A cycle requires x>=1, q_after<=0.5*q_before, and q_rebuilt>=0.8*q_before within one second while that same resting price remains best. Do not sum state updates as traded volume. Unknown within-timestamp ordering, book reset, crossed market, price leaving best, or a feed gap terminates/censors the cycle. Require >=3 distinct cycles within 30 seconds and cumulative aggressive execution >=2*max_observed_displayed_size. This is displayed reload **inference**; additions, cancellations and hidden executions cannot be uniquely attributed from these fields.

`REFILL_ZONE(d)` baseline uses a fixed two-minute formation window starting at the first rewarded aggressive print in direction d after a qualifying parent release. All same-direction large/rewarded prints available in that window define bounds min/max executed price, padded one tick. Require >=3 distinct prints at >=2 distinct timestamps; opposite prints remain in diagnostics but do not widen the band. Freeze only at the full two-minute end. A stream-threshold variant can freeze at the third qualifying print, with that actual availability; it is a different variant. After freezing require departure >=4 ticks in direction d, then first later return within 30 minutes and either ABSORB with a=-d or RELOAD with a=-d. Confirmation is the resulting defender-direction REACTION. Stops use the actual return excursion, never a later day extreme.

`CATALYST(d)` is a rewarded aggressive cluster: a 30-second window beginning with a large trade, >=3 same-direction qualifying executions, signed volume share>=0.70 and a subsequent REWARD in direction d. The cluster band is their min/max prices padded one tick; its reference price is their volume-weighted mean, not the band midpoint. Frozen geometry and reward completion time define availability. A passive catalyst variant uses ABSORB/RELOAD and its subsequent defender reward; it does not require fast CVD or gamma.

`FAST_APPROACH(B,d)` measures only the last complete 30 seconds before first touch: absolute displacement /tick/seconds and total absolute tick travel /seconds, plus signed volume. Speed class is fast if displacement rate >=prior matched-slot q80; drift if <=q30; otherwise normal. Direction is sign(last-first), zero -> flat. A fast short approach to a support band does not logically establish whether support holds; both response outcomes are tested.

`FAILED_SQUEEZE(d)` after a catalyst requires an attempted move >=3 ticks in d, then a completed one-minute close back through the catalyst's adverse edge within five minutes, without first reaching 8 ticks reward. Save the failed leg's actual extreme. `RESQUEEZE(d)` must occur after failure and a later qualified REFILL_ZONE return, with a new fast approach/flow window and a close beyond the refill band in d. An independent fast move without prior failure belongs to F18, not this sequence.

`CONTROL_STOP(B,d)` chooses the nearest confirmed one-minute pivot adverse to entry formed inside the current reaction episode, using one left/one right bar, or if none, the episode's observed adverse extreme. Stop is two ticks beyond that level. It may be inside a broader historical area; the area invalidates only under its own accepted-break rule. A stopped trade is not evidence that all future area tests are invalid. Each re-entry still needs a new distinct episode and its own confirmation.

## E08. Swings, microbalances, FVGs and control shifts

Fixed-time bars align to exchange-local clock boundaries, never row groups. A strict pivot high at bar i exceeds the highs of the preceding l and following r bars; low mirrors. Default 5-minute, l=r=2; ties are not pivots. It is known at close(i+r). Consecutive same-side pivots: retain the more extreme; on equal price retain the earlier; only confirmed endpoints can form a swing. Alternating pivots define ordered legs. A confirmed old pivot high/low is an external reference: scan price from its **formation end through confirmation**, and if already revisited before it could be used, mark consumed-at-recognition. Do not make it fresh by delaying recognition.

MSS uses a completed one-minute close beyond the most recent previously confirmed opposite pivot by two ticks after the parent sweep. Freeze the pivot ID before testing the close. The pivot can have been consumed as a target; it remains historical structure for the same episode. A first gap after MSS must be formed later, not borrowed from an earlier unrelated morning gap.

Bullish three-candle FVG: `low(C3)>high(C1)` by at least one tick; band `[high(C1),low(C3)]`; bearish mirror. Available at C3 close. Keep only fixed 1m/5m/15m/60m variants requested by the card. First contact, midpoint and full-fill times are distinct. A gap becomes inactive for first-touch entries at its first contact, but a pending original episode may continue. Full fill/expiry retires its geometric objective; expiry is five sessions for HTF, same session for <=5m by default. Drawings never imply fills.

Order block bullish: C2.low<C1.low and C3.close>C2.high; bearish mirror. Block is full C2 range, known at C3 close. Rejection block is only C2's sweep-side wick, known after a close beyond the full C2 candle. Conservative stop baseline is beyond the entire C2 adverse extreme plus two ticks; midpoint/local-control alternatives are explicitly separate grids. Do not reuse a full-block midpoint as a wick boundary.

`MICROBALANCE` scans complete one-minute bars in time order. Maintain a maximal contiguous run of at least `min_bars` (default 5), all with high-low union width <=`width_fraction` (default 0.15)*frozen W69, and each close inside the union. Start with the first five-bar qualifying run, freeze its H/L once qualified, then extend time while every new bar stays inside those frozen bounds. First completed close beyond either edge by two ticks ends formation and signals that direction. Do not widen a frozen box to absorb its breakout. After termination search afresh using only later bars. A rolling-width alternative can update bounds before minimum-bar qualification, never after. Box width must be >=4 ticks. The first breakout is eligible; later returns require a separately named break/retest episode.

More precisely, before qualification test each rolling `min_bars` contiguous window in chronological order; choose the first qualifying window. After freeze, an outside wick does not change the bounds. Each boundary's first visit starts a five-minute pending breakout episode; a qualifying close in that interval may confirm even after the active line ends. Without such confirmation, that boundary is consumed and cannot rearm. The box terminates on the first confirmed breakout, after both side episodes fail/expire, or at the card's search-window end. Later boxes use bars starting strictly after termination. Historical box shading can remain visible after termination, with its inactive state explicit.

Golden pocket on an already confirmed directional leg from A to B: retracement prices are `B-0.50*(B-A)` and `B-0.618*(B-A)`, sorted into a band. B must have been confirmed before the retrace. Earliest qualified post-confirmation touch plus REACTION in sign(B-A) defines continuation. Stop beyond A by two ticks; target B only if still a valid unvisited target at decision, otherwise the explicit 1.5R target variant. A profile POC, previous-hour midpoint or final daily range is not an automatic leg anchor.

## E09. Gaps, target selection and relational state

NWOG is the interval between the last eligible execution before the previous scheduled Friday Globex close and first eligible execution at Sunday's scheduled Globex open, same raw contract. If either exact opening/closing minute is missing or the contract changes, unavailable. Gap direction and near/mid/far edges are separate. The forming Sunday print does **not** count as its own return. Let O be Sunday open, F Friday close and g=sign(O-F); O=F means no gap. For later executions, first contact is the first `g*(price-O)<0` (movement into the gap), or the first `g*(price-O)<=0` after price has first departed at least one tick away from the gap. Repeated opening-edge prints without departure or penetration do not manufacture a return. A price jump across the whole gap counts contact/full fill without assuming an executable fill at every skipped price. Track all subsequent observations, including overnight trading. “Unfilled” means the far edge F has not been reached; “untouched” means no return under the preceding definition. A partially visited gap can be a far-edge destination only in the explicitly allowed partial-fill variant. At entry the baseline requires untouched gap ahead; do not use a completed gap because it is still drawn.

For target selection, filter reference candidates available and fresh at decision, in trade direction beyond expected entry, and within the parent expiry. Select nearest distance, ties by earlier formation end then lexical object ID. Store the whole candidate set and rejection reasons. No eligible target -> `no_target`, never today's final high/low. A profile's fixed POC can remain a reusable internal destination according to area rules; external high/low freshness is independent.

For a stacked-sweep episode require >=2 different fresh reference IDs within a 15-minute span, in the same sweep direction. Each must have been active immediately before its first visit. The earliest starts the episode; both sweeps must precede MSS and target selection. Consumed references from earlier overnight trading cannot be counted again. Distinct references at the same price remain separate IDs but count as **one price cluster** for the baseline minimum of two reasons/sweeps; clusters within two ticks are one cluster.

## E10. Native options and regime scenarios

Every option snapshot is per native underlying, expiration, strike and right, with `available_at` from all inputs. Never add raw strike coordinates across SPX, SPY, NDX, QQQ, NQ and ES. Main baseline universe is same-day expiration, plus a separately reported 1–7 DTE cohort. Do not silently pool 0–14 DTE. Exclude expired contracts using the true UTC settlement/expiry instant and T<=1 minute. Store observed scoped-chain OI share against the daily eligible contract list; require >=90% of known OI within the declared strike/DTE scope. This is scope coverage, not whole-market coverage.

For European cash-settled index options use synchronized call/put midpoint pairs at the same K,T with both quotes passing D06. With annual continuously compounded rate r and year fraction T=seconds/(365*86400), parity-forward candidate is `F_K=K+exp(rT)*(C_mid-P_mid)`. Select pairs with absolute log(K/F_initial)<=0.03 using previous-close native cash grown at r as F_initial; require >=5 distinct strikes. Estimate F as the median F_K; reject if median absolute deviation /F>0.002. Run a second pass selecting near-ATM strikes around that F and take their median once. This creates an option-implied **forward**, not cash spot. Put-call parity can be noisy; failure is unavailable, not a futures-price substitution.

European forward Black formula: `d1=(ln(F/K)+0.5*sigma^2*T)/(sigma*sqrt(T))`, `d2=d1-sigma*sqrt(T)`, call `e^-rT*(F*N(d1)-K*N(d2))`, put `e^-rT*(K*N(-d2)-F*N(-d1))`. Forward gamma is `e^-rT*phi(d1)/(F*sigma*sqrt(T))`. For native cash spot with a verified continuous yield q, use Black-Scholes-Merton spot gamma `e^-qT*phi(d1)/(S*sigma*sqrt(T))`, d1 including (r-q)T. Keep the exposure coordinate explicit.

Forward deltas are `e^-rT*N(d1)` for calls and `e^-rT*(N(d1)-1)` for puts. Spot deltas use `e^-qT` instead. American-tree delta is the central finite difference with the same h used for its gamma. These Greeks describe the selected pricing model; they are not observed hedge positions.

Solve IV by bisection on [0.0001,5.0] annualized volatility, maximum 100 iterations, stopping at price error <=max(0.0001,1e-6*mid). A quote outside the model's attainable bounds is invalid; never clamp it to a plausible IV. No-arbitrage bounds, put-call parity residuals and convergence are saved. Annual rates are decimals, not percentages. Rates use the nearest maturity in days, linearly interpolating zero rates between available tenors and flat at the nearest endpoint; only prior-available observations.

For standard American ETF options use a Cox-Ross-Rubinstein tree with N=500 steps: dt=T/N, u=exp(sigma*sqrt(dt)), d=1/u, p=(exp((r-q)dt)-d)/(u-d), discounted backward expectation with early exercise max(intrinsic,continuation). Require 0<p<1. Baseline continuous yield is `q=sum(eligible_cash_dividend_per_share)/current_spot` over the previous 365 calendar days; the window is one year, so no further time multiplier applies. An eligible dividend has an ex-date in that past window and conservative availability at the next scheduled ETF session open after its ex-date, or its documented publication time if later. D10 ex-dates do not establish earlier announcement knowledge. Use amounts on the current share basis with only already-effective split adjustments. Require complete trailing action coverage; verified absence means q=0, missing coverage is unavailable. Label this `trailing_ex_dividend_continuous_yield`, a pricing approximation. If a future ex-date has a documented announcement vintage available at the snapshot, exclude expiries crossing that known date within the next seven days. Without such a vintage set `future_dividend_calendar=unavailable`, retain the named continuous-yield approximation and report gamma sensitivity at q=0 and 2*q; never use the archive's later-revealed ex-date as a past exclusion. A discrete-dividend pricer is a separate extension. Use the same IV bounds, N=1000 convergence check on a fixed calibration fixture set, and central finite-difference gamma with h=max(0.001*S,0.01). American CME options need an appropriate futures-option American pricer; until built and verified, use their contract/OI distribution only, not fabricated European gamma.

Dollar gamma per 1% coordinate move for option i is `g_i=gamma_i*OI_i*multiplier_i*X^2*0.01`, where X is the declared spot or forward. Aggregate three disclosed position-sign scenarios: `call_plus_put_minus` (call +, put -), `all_short` (both -), `all_long` (both +). These are hypotheses, not observed dealer positions. A trade-quote signed-flow overlay is a fourth separate model and must not overwrite OI positions or assume all trades open new positions.

At a snapshot, strike wall is the strike with maximal absolute aggregated g within each right; ties choose nearest X, then lower strike. Also export unsigned OI wall. Gamma-flip search revalues gamma at X' on a 401-point uniform grid [0.90X,1.10X], keeping each snapshot IV fixed. A flip requires a genuine sign change between adjacent finite totals; zero followed by positive is not a negative-to-positive flip. Linear interpolation locates each crossing; choose nearest X, ties lower. If no crossing, `no_flip`. Report all roots and sensitivity to the grid and scenario. Do not call a wall a support/resistance fact; test its later conditional response.

Regime baseline is categorical signed scenario gamma `{negative,neutral,positive}` using `abs(net)/sum(abs(g))`: neutral if <=0.10 or total=0; missing inputs are unavailable. Keep native price location relative to walls/flip, prior VIX band and time-to-expiry as separate features. No forced trading gate until a named consumer card specifies one.

Two explicit replacements cover additional unpublished pressure/“volatility trigger” concepts without pretending to recover their formulas:

- **Gamma stress boundary:** on the same repricing grid compute `z(X')=net_gamma(X')/gross_absolute_gamma(X')`. Find crossings of `z(X')=-0.25`, interpolating and selecting nearest X exactly as for flip roots; zero gross exposure is unavailable. Export the threshold and every root. A grid may test thresholds -0.10/-0.25/-0.50. This is a scenario threshold level; test whether later realized volatility is higher near/after it. It is not asserted to be a physical volatility switch or the original author's line.
- **Option-flow directional pressure:** use the previous complete five-minute native-product option trade-quote window. Require `valid_for_volume=true`, a noncrossed positive-spread quote with `ts_quote<=trade_ts` and age<=one second, and a model delta snapshot available at/before the trade. Classify execution at/above ask as buy +1, at/below bid as sell -1; within-spread executions remain unknown. For signed eligible trades let `w_i=delta_i*size_i*multiplier_i*X_i`; pressure is `sum(aggressor_i*w_i)/sum(abs(w_i))`, denominator>0. Require at least80% of total contract quantity in the window to have known side and valid Greeks, otherwise unavailable. Report this coverage and excluded quantity. Pressure ranges [-1,1] and describes signed transaction exposure, never dealer ownership or an OI update. It is optional; missing trade-quote coverage must not suppress otherwise valid OI/gamma snapshots.

For the pressure/stress comparison use next complete 30-minute native squared-log-return sum as the volatility outcome, constructed from native minute prices or the named minute parity-forward series consistently. A missing native series leaves that outcome unavailable; NQ realized volatility may be a separately named futures-consumer outcome. Estimate training-only conditional distributions versus unconditional same-clock volatility, with the same proper-loss and coverage protocol.

## E11. Signals, fills, stops, outcomes and management

All signal cards use the following default execution contract unless they explicitly select limit entry. At confirmation, freeze side, structural stop, target policy and latest valid BBO. Preflight requires BBO age<=1 second, spread<=4 ticks, positive planned risk and target in the trade direction. Expected entry is ask for long, bid for short. `R0=abs(expected_entry-stop)` in points, fixed before execution; it must be >=one tick. Reject at decision with an explicit reason if these conditions fail.

Submit one-contract market entry after 250 ms. Fill at the first later valid uncrossed BBO with sufficient displayed size, on the adverse side, plus one adverse tick slippage. Do not wait for a smaller spread after submission. If no valid BBO within five seconds, cancel as unfilled and report execution unavailability. Report alternate delays {0,250,1000} ms and additional slippage {0,1,2} ticks as execution sensitivities, not alpha parameters. Where only bars exist, keep descriptive event/outcome metrics; do not merge optimistic bar fills with the BBO baseline.

If the arriving fill has crossed a stop or target, retain the fill and immediately process protective exit at the next eligible quote. Do not delete a bad slippage event by reevaluating entry eligibility with future knowledge. Stops trigger on executable adverse BBO (long bid<=stop, short ask>=stop), and fill at first eligible quote after the same delay with adverse slippage. Gap-through stops fill at the observed price, not the requested stop. Exit spread is never capped. A quote gap past a required exit creates `execution_censored` plus marked exposure; never score it as a surviving winner.

Target limit fills require a marketable opposite BBO beyond the target, not a last-trade touch: long bid>=target, short ask<=target. For a resting entry limit require trade-through by at least one tick after order arrival and a valid executable quote at/better than the limit; no queue priority is assumed from a touch alone. Cancel unfilled entry after five minutes or parent expiry. A midpoint limit that never fills is not a trade. When stop and target are inside one unordered event group, baseline processes stop first and separately reports the ambiguous count and target-first bound.

Default round-trip fee is **$5 per NQ contract as a disclosed research assumption**, stress $10; replace with a documented fee schedule before live evaluation. These values are not a quote from a broker. Net PnL=`d*(exit-entry)*multiplier-fees`; normalized outcome is net PnL/(R0*multiplier). Also report points, dollars and realized adverse fill slippage so changing risk definitions cannot hide losses. One contract, one position per model at a time, first eligible signal wins; no pyramiding. At most three filled entries per session and at most one per distinct episode. Model-specific entry windows end new entries; existing positions exit by their declared outcome horizon, capped at calendar RTH close unless explicitly overnight.

Resolve competing eligible signals deterministically by ascending `(confirmation_available_at, first_touch_at, primary_object_id, side_rank, episode_id)`, with long side_rank=0 and short side_rank=1. A signal without a touch uses its confirmation time for first_touch_at; primary_object_id is the lexically smallest frozen parent ID if there are several. A card may explicitly replace this priority. Pending entry orders reserve the model's single position slot until filled, cancelled or expired. Signals arriving while that slot is occupied are recorded as `position_or_order_active` and are not queued for a later fill. This administrative priority never fabricates order inside an unordered market timestamp group; E00/D02 ambiguity rules still apply.

Stops never widen. Baseline uses one fixed structural stop and one fixed card-selected target. Management variants are explicit: break-even only after executable +1R0, moved to entry+fee-equivalent points on the next quote; trailing only from newly confirmed pivots; partial exits require integer contract simulations with a declared multi-contract unit and are **not** approximated by fractional fills in the one-contract baseline. J24 tests management on the exact same parent entry ledger.

MFE/MAE start at actual fill, not morning open. For long use max(0,max(executable bid)-entry) and max(0,entry-min(executable bid)); short uses ask symmetrically. Values are nonnegative. A horizon before entry is not applicable. Trade exit ends realized exposure; separately labelled shadow excursions may continue to the horizon. A favorable excursion is not automatically profit.

## E12. Common output tables

Implement these append-only tables, keyed by config/input hashes:

- `references`: object schema from E00, type/side/price/band, formation interval, known_at, first_visit, strict_sweep, consumed_at, expired_at, coverage and provenance.
- `snapshots`: profile/VWAP/vol/option objects with as-of timestamp, all numerical inputs, sample dates/count, scope and quality.
- `episodes`: model_id, variant_id, instrument, session, parent/reference IDs, side, state transitions with timestamps, failure/invalidation cause, earliest available time and terminal state.

- `orders_fills`: order ID, submit/arrival/cancel/fill times, requested/actual prices, quantity, BBO/trade evidence, fees and ambiguity flags.
- `outcomes`: horizon, event/target/stop times, PnL/MFE/MAE, censoring, final historical path labels and outcome coverage.
- `experiments`: all candidate configurations, attempted/rejected counts, split dates, controls, metric values, intervals, selection and promotion decision.

Model-specific output columns are declared in each card. An event record carries one side and one reference/episode; daily summaries are derived with an explicit denominator, never the canonical evidence itself.
