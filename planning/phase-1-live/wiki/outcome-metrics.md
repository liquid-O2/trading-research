# Outcome distribution of a declared process

Object in [Sires — thesis, risk and order flow](method-sires-thesis-flow.md) · [Unnamed member — reaction area plus minor HVN](method-member-two-reasons.md) · [Sires × TeamVOT — The Refill Effect](method-refill-effect.md) · [Stoic — data engine / quantifying fundamentals](method-stoic-data-engine.md) · [Stoic — asymmetric compounding](method-stoic-asymmetric-compounding.md).

Sires's codex and review use all trades under a bias/model, with R outcomes, average win/loss, EV, profit factor and drawdown. Stoic requires win rate and average R:R from an adequate sample. The Refill correction reports causal mechanical-entry results around −0.16R to −0.54R out of sample. [C1] p.6; [AVG] pp.27–30; [DATA] pp.3–4, 8; [OFM] p.18.

**Not a standalone trade.** Source-sequence fidelity and profitability are separate. A component touch rate or a payout screenshot is not the whole method's win rate.

**Record before use.** Process/cohort version, all candidate/order/fill denominators, source entry-risk unit, later outcomes, missing/censored cases, costs and evaluation window.

**Phase 1 observation.** Only outcomes after the relevant decision belong to that episode. Preserve losing quarters, unselected cases and causal rebuilds; no rate or P&L is recomputed in this compilation.
