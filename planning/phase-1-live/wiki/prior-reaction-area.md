# Prior defended reaction area

Object in [Sires — thesis, risk and order flow](method-sires-thesis-flow.md) · [Unnamed member — reaction area plus minor HVN](method-member-two-reasons.md) · [Sires × TeamVOT — The Refill Effect](method-refill-effect.md).

A previously observed clean reaction or defended band supplies a location for a later test. The unnamed member pairs this history with an independent minor HVN; Sires requires fresh same-band defense on a return. [K10] pp.5–8; [ANAT] p.7; [CONT] pp.4–10.

**Not a standalone trade.** Remembering a prior bounce does not authorize another entry; the new touch must satisfy the current thesis and confirmation.

**Record before use.** Band_id and bounds, prior reaction/defense event, side, known_at, independent node if used, later contact and fresh confirmation.

**Phase 1 observation.** The earlier defense must precede the current test, and both must concern the same source band. Do not use the outcome of the new touch to invent its prior memory.
