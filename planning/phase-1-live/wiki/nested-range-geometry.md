# Nested source range geometry

Object in [JJumboFX — SDRange / Time-Based Ranges](method-jumbo-tbr.md).

Later charts layer inner profile/range spans inside the outer 6–9 framework and project the selected span independently. These are refinements of the same range loop. [JR] pp.11, 14 and the May 2026 charts; posts 2055344660986364371 / 2026059018750378427.

**Not a standalone trade.** An inner gray box or blue line is not automatically outer-range EQ or EV. Layering is not evidence of another operating method.

**Record before use.** Outer and inner range IDs, literal source labels, independent bounds/widths, projection parent, version, drawing/known_at and unresolved construction.

**Phase 1 observation.** A figure-annotated span can support a fixture observation. Automatic generation remains unknown without its source construction; never silently substitute the outer width.
