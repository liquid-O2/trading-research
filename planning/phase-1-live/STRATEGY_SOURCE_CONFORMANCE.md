## Version 1.1 measurement repairs

Published same-contract OHLCV resolves ambiguous native candle endpoints only after the interval closes and after high, low, volume and already-known endpoints reconcile. It does not recover exchange ordering. Local flow uses timestamp-batch execution VWAP, with an adverse-side observed tick for the modeled entry. Stage absence is evaluated condition by condition: a known violated conjunct proves no setup even when an unused operand is unavailable.

Prior day/week/month price context may use the acquired unadjusted continuous chart across contract changes. This is explicitly separate from same-contract native volume-profile context and from P-zone normalization. A missing published session requires covered native extrema or a documented empty post-expiration chart window. The December c.0 series retains the expiring contract through Friday after its 09:30 expiration; those empty RTH windows have expiration and roll-map receipts and supply no synthetic price. Other missing windows are not discarded.

The 2021 and 2026 prior-month candidates previously referenced only the post-roll part of December. Rebuilding the complete acquired chart reference changes their reference identities. The release records both old candidate IDs and their corrected dispositions.

Judas outbound uses the opening price. In its compact confirmation record, the legacy `C` slot carries that entry price, so the supplemental audit checks it against `endpoint_evidence.published.O`. Complete candle records and close-confirmation branches are checked against their corresponding published O/H/L/C/V fields. This mapping does not claim the opening price was the one-second close.
