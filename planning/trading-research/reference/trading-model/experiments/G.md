# Mixtures, calibration and selection: individual experiment phases

All phase records below are **planned trading-system checks**, not completed test results. Each parent retains its ten-part specification; named refinements isolate particular information, definitions or model/policy alternatives. They do not imply one process or model per row.

[Phase rules and model quality](../SPECIALIST_EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM.md) govern chronology, metrics, interactions, useful effects, failure decisions and future testing. The [complete upgrade paths and specific child bindings](../UPGRADE_PATHS.md) and [implementation constructions](../UPGRADE_CONSTRUCTIONS.md) bind every unit below. Every listed local case needs separate named assertions at implementation; a test bearing the component name alone is insufficient.

| Unit | Kind | Individual target |
|---|---|---|
| [G01](#g01) — Target-specific Context and path mixtures | predictive-specialist | Calibrated mixture distribution, weights, each expert prediction/version, between/within-expert dispersion, effective expert count and missing-pattern applicability. |
| [G01.DIVERSITY](#g01-diversity) — Specialist diversity and shared representations | predictive-specialist | Calibrated common-target distribution and complete-policy gain. |
| [G02](#g02) — Rich volatility and excursion ensembles | predictive-specialist | G02.V: forward variance distribution. G02.PATH: joint excursion/path distribution. Each carries calibration, horizon, contribution/disagreement and port identity; only the declared downstream consumers can use it. |
| [G02.MULTITARGET](#g02-multitarget) — Variance, tail and excursion ensemble roles | predictive-specialist | Positive variance, calibrated tails/excursions and ordered path only when supported. |
| [G03](#g03) — Forecast calibration and uncertainty service | predictive-specialist | Calibrated Forecast, calibrator fit interval/version, reliability/support diagnostics and abstention domain. Exact deterministic measurements do not receive invented probability calibration. |
| [G03.CONDITIONAL](#g03-conditional) — Calibration and uncertainty diagnostics | predictive-specialist | Forecast probabilities/quantiles and conditional coverage at actual deployment prevalence. |
| [G04](#g04) — Level reach and first-passage expert | predictive-specialist | Reach probability/CDF, arrival-time and possible arrival-state distribution, gap/censor flag, horizon and uncertainty. No reached-only training denominator masquerading as unconditional reach. |
| [G04.ORDER](#g04-order) — Reach, arrival and ordered first passage | predictive-specialist | First contact/time/state, gap/no contact and competing nearer/deeper arrival. |
| [G05](#g05) — Conditional departure, continuation and runner-quality experts | predictive-specialist | Calibrated conditional path distribution/quantiles, excursions and remaining-payoff features, with target/version and uncertainty. It forecasts future behavior; it is not mandatory Response pattern recognition. |
| [G05.RUNNER_DURATION](#g05-runner_duration) — Reaction versus runner quality | predictive-specialist | Joint ordered outcome, return/excursions and duration over fixed boundaries. |
| [G06](#g06) — Executable action-value and cost model | policy-or-value | `VALUE` distribution, expected net, lower/scenario bound, loss/tail probability, cost decomposition, fill uncertainty and infeasible reason. Replay label is simulated value, not observed causal counterfactual. |
| [G06.COST_DEPENDENCE](#g06-cost_dependence) — Joint fill and action-value uncertainty | policy-or-value | Net increment/duration/terminal state, tail loss and non-fill. |
| [G07](#g07) — Value of waiting, deeper levels and additional information | policy-or-value | Comparable Q/value distributions, chosen wait trigger/deadline/cancel conditions, opportunity-cost diagnostics and uncertainty. Counterfactual alternatives remain model-based where fills/market impact unobserved. |
| [G07.DURATION](#g07-duration) — Common-boundary waiting and occupancy | policy-or-value | Comparable finite-horizon action Q and chosen waits/deadlines. |
| [G07.INFORMATION](#g07-information) — Value of observing another stage | policy-or-value | Net value of act now, observe, cancel or stay flat. |
| [G08](#g08) — Candidate ranking and constrained action selection | policy-or-value | Full ranking, selected action/plan, uncertainty/alternative value gap and reason for every rejection. Risk vetoed candidate remains logged. |
| [G08.RANKING](#g08-ranking) — Candidate and action selection objectives | policy-or-value | Selected action utility and complete eligible-day net/risk. |
| [G09](#g09) — Applicability, disagreement and abstention gate | policy-or-value | Available/limited/abstain status, reasons, calibrated uncertainty/applicability and permissible fallback set. Never changes one-mini sizing into fractional micros or broadens stops automatically. |
| [G09.SUPPORT](#g09-support) — Applicability and abstention | policy-or-value | Coverage-risk-value tradeoff and correctness of abstention reasons. |
| [G10](#g10) — Per-expert adaptation, recalibration and retirement policy | policy-or-value | Proposed model/calibration version transition, evidence/fit interval, effective time, rollback and retirement reason. No autonomous live replacement authorized by this plan. |
| [G10.ADAPTATION](#g10-adaptation) — Controlled per-expert change policies | policy-or-value | Prequential proper score, drift recovery and complete adaptive-policy economics. |
