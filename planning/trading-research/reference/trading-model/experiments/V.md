# Research evidence and operations: individual experiment phases

All phase records below are **planned trading-system checks**, not completed test results. Each parent retains its ten-part specification; named refinements isolate particular information, definitions or model/policy alternatives. They do not imply one process or model per row.

[Phase rules and model quality](../SPECIALIST_EXPERIMENT_PROGRAM.md) govern chronology, metrics, interactions, useful effects, failure decisions and future testing. The [complete upgrade paths and specific child bindings](../UPGRADE_PATHS.md) and [implementation constructions](../UPGRADE_CONSTRUCTIONS.md) bind every unit below. Every listed local case needs separate named assertions at implementation; a test bearing the component name alone is insufficient.

| Unit | Kind | Individual target |
|---|---|---|
| [V01](#v01) — Label, opportunity and evidence artifact service | measurement-or-integrity | `Outcome` tables, dataset/label manifests, censor/ambiguity counts and provenance graph. No-training target exists for exact bookkeeping; analytic reference fixtures validate labels. |
| [V01.LABEL_COVERAGE](#v01-label_coverage) — Candidate and label completeness | measurement-or-integrity | Exact reachable/ordered/value/OI label population. |
| [V02](#v02) — Chronological splits, purging and OOF dependency builder | measurement-or-integrity | Fold manifests, excluded-row reasons, upstream OOF prediction keys and leakage-audit report. Same row cannot have both training and evaluation roles for the same fitted decision chain. |
| [V02.LINEAGE](#v02-lineage) — OOF graph and legal overlaps | measurement-or-integrity | Time-honest full-chain predictions and reproducible training state. |
| [V03](#v03) — Hypothesis, search-budget and multiple-testing registry | measurement-or-integrity | Trial manifest, search history, paired comparison report, selected/frozen configuration and rejection/deferral reason. Report known missing prior trial history as a limitation. |
| [V03.SEARCH](#v03-search) — Honest model/definition selection | measurement-or-integrity | Reproducible selected version and uncertainty accounting. |
| [V04](#v04) — Evidence gates and economic feasibility decision | measurement-or-integrity | Signed/versioned research decision with effect intervals, failure ownership, required next evidence, no-go conditions and objective gap. Explicitly distinguish proposed tests from completed ones. |
| [V04.POWER](#v04-power) — Useful-effect and sample-support decisions | measurement-or-integrity | Supported/not-met/inconclusive promotion and $2,000/day objective gap. |
| [V05](#v05) — Drift, calibration monitoring and controlled adaptation | measurement-or-integrity | Component-specific health state, alert evidence/window, fallback choice and proposed research ticket. Delayed labels remain pending; no future performance proxy substituted as truth. |
| [V05.DRIFT](#v05-drift) — Component-specific drift and fallback | measurement-or-integrity | Detection delay, false intervention and adaptive-policy net/risk. |
| [V06](#v06) — Historical/live parity, restart and deterministic replay | measurement-or-integrity | Parity report with first divergent event/component, numerical tolerance, artifact hashes and arrival-model scenario. Exact real historical arrival cannot be claimed where receipt times are absent. |
| [V06.PREFIX_RESTART](#v06-prefix_restart) — End-to-end replay equivalence | measurement-or-integrity | Causal and restart-equivalent outputs with declared arrival scenario. |
| [V07](#v07) — Failure attribution and controlled diagnostic interventions | measurement-or-integrity | Per-component/family/date failure report, candidate-density/missingness changes, paired effect intervals and unresolved interactions. Losses alone do not prove a process fault. |
| [V07.ORACLE_SCOPE](#v07-oracle_scope) — Exact in-set diagnostic ceiling | measurement-or-integrity | Best in-set hindsight value and gap attributable to candidate/selection constraints. |
| [V07.PLANTED_SIGNAL](#v07-planted_signal) — Synthetic positive and null controls | measurement-or-integrity | Recoverable direction/policy effect in the synthetic setup only. |
| [V08](#v08) — Resource-aware reproducible experiment orchestration | measurement-or-integrity | Job manifest, provenance, resource telemetry, peak memory, throughput, compressed/uncompressed storage, success/failure checkpoint and estimated remaining cost with uncertainty. |
| [V08.RESOURCE_FRONTIER](#v08-resource_frontier) — Measured accuracy, latency and resource tradeoffs | measurement-or-integrity | Reproducibility and quality/value per feasible compute/latency budget. |
