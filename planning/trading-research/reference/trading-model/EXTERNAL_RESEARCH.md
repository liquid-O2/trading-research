## Visual evidence supplement

All 87 captured documentation images were actually viewed in 29 labeled sheets; IMG016, IMG018, and IMG027 were also inspected as originals. [Image inventory](review/external/skylit/images.json) records exact source-page URLs, hashes, paths, and review status. This is visual review of the captured documentation images, not unseen linked videos or private posts.

- **EXT-V004 (IMG001–004):** Charts show turns near, sometimes before, a strike; a midday box sits between stronger concentrations; the displayed target line and heatmap strike need not coincide. Test causal zone width and mapping instead of pixel-exact contacts.
- **EXT-V006 (IMG005–020):** Visual sequences add intervening rejection, post-rally lower support, changing same-strike magnitude, opposing distant expiry clusters, and sparse-exposure corridors. A SPY three-snapshot example accompanies an upward move while a negative lower node shrinks. The 15:30 annotation asserts liquidation without participant evidence. IMG018 lacks expiry headers. Color is relative to board scaling; read signed values.
- **EXT-V009 (IMG021):** The LLY example visibly includes a substantial earlier rally and a still-distant target, motivating remaining-range and reach-horizon controls.
- **EXT-V013 (IMG022–028):** Selected TSLA and three-board index paths include intervening levels and retests; NFLX shows a later gap and a separate longer-expiry upper concentration. These are fixture narratives, not an unbiased event sample.
- **EXT-V015/016 (IMG029–034):** The small boards distinguish separated concentrations from densely intermingled signs. No subsequent realized path appears in these panels. Topology alone must not be assigned an observed outcome.
- **EXT-V017/018 (IMG035–040):** Price can reverse at the nearer zone without reaching the strongest displayed strike. Distant-target examples have explicit future horizons and prior price structure. Zone drawing and snapshot timing remain part of the hypothesis.
- **EXT-V019/020/022 (IMG041–048):** Trend boards, SPX/SPY/QQQ paired rejection, and multi-expiry descending sketches preserve cross-board and cross-expiry structure. A drawn path is a forecast illustration, not observations over those future dates.
- **EXT-V032 (IMG049–069):** Screens show negative historical DTE values, a historical drilldown with later daily-history rows, and a next-IV field. These motivate strict decision-time filtering. Repeated equal-size hits and size-over-OI do not prove common identity or opening positions. Some column descriptions are visibly clipped.
- **EXT-V085 (IMG070–083):** Switching expiration count changes visible dominant strikes. Orb controls clamp size/opacity; mapped futures dots move vertically. Projection shading has no demonstrated coverage. A dark-print overlay contains dates later than the visible price segment, requiring separate replay filtering.
- **EXT-V086 (IMG084–087):** Golden-pocket, classic, extension, and editable presets demonstrate mutable ratio/visibility choices, not a fixed signal definition.

These are focused methodological checks, not claims that every page of every external paper was reviewed. The exhaustive page-and-image protocol was completed for the supplied bundle. The proposed engineering choices below are our research recommendations; the cited papers do not establish an NQ/ES trading edge.
