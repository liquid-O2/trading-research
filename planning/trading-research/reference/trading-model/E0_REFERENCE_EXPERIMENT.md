# E0: reproducible initial integrity and economics experiment

This is a deliberately small, fully specified research reference, not a selected trading strategy. It makes the initial end-to-end harness concrete so later improvements have a reproducible comparison. No E0 training, backtest or orders have been run. The wider Context/Location universe remains in E1–E4.

## Freeze the manifest before replay

Use NQ outright futures with verified contract definitions, calendar and full acquired MBP-1 event semantics under the [market-data source contract](MARKET_DATA_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md). Derive eligible trades and the current BBO state from MBP-1, retaining action, reported side, flags, source order and every supplied field. Standalone trades reconcile or explicitly replace the trade view; never sum both copies. Select the active contract from the preceding completed session's volume among eligible unexpired outrights; ties use earlier expiry then canonical ID. Fill at actual contract prices. Freeze a deterministic eligible-date list using data/schema completeness alone. Start with the first five and last five eligible dates of each calendar quarter in 2022–2025 as a bounded harness cohort, recording every exclusion before outcomes are generated. These dates are already historical/development evidence. A full-calendar challenger follows only after the harness passes.

The rule benchmark needs no fitting. For the small learned comparison, use eligible 2022 dates for fitting, 2023 for development selection, 2024 for calibration and 2025 for retrospective evaluation. Implement the V02 interval exclusions and correlated-date grouping. The whole pipeline has already been conceptually inspected using historical material, so even the final historical cohort is not represented as genuinely untouched future. Freeze exact day IDs, file hashes, fees/latency scenarios, model parameters and metric definitions in the run manifest.

Sessions use America/New_York with exchange/business-date mapping. The E0 entry window is 09:31–15:30 NY on valid RTH days; this narrow harness window is an experimental baseline, not a restriction on the later all-session program. Force cancellation/flattening at the earlier of 15:55 NY or the verified required boundary minus the configured safety buffer. On shortened days use the corresponding calendar; no inferred regular close. Insufficient remaining time produces no entry or a explicitly truncated alternative trial.

## Exact features, candidates and labels

At completed one-minute decision cuts, generate frozen 06:00–09:00 range H/L/EQ/quarters, edge±.5W and edge±W, and prior-RTH H/L/open/close. Use one tick contact tolerance and retain source identity at overlaps. The completed-range W must be positive; otherwise range-derived candidates are unavailable. Include all candidates and no-touch outcomes. Background rows at every decision cut permit complete clock-population diagnostics.

Predict 15-minute fixed-end reach and ordered departure for both sides using CC-04. The departure observation ends at the original `t+15min`; no-contact is observed when the full interval is present. Exact-event ordering governs barrier labels; otherwise preserve ambiguity. For the initial learned classifier, use side, object type, distance/W, current position within the 06–09 range, prior-RTH normalized distance, completed prior five-minute return, preceding 30-minute realized variance, minute-of-session sine/cosine and time remaining. Scaling fits on training only. No options/Response feature is required for this baseline.

The transparent benchmark uses shrunken conditional frequencies by object type, side and four fixed distance/W buckets: [0,.25), [.25,.5), [.5,1), [1,infinity). Back off to the training overall frequency when a cell is empty; use a Beta(1,1) smoothing benchmark for binary targets. The challenger is regularized logistic/discrete-time hazard using the same features, L2 strengths .1/1/10 selected on 2023 proper score. Fit sigmoid calibration on 2024 predictions without changing the selected feature/model configuration. More flexible end-to-end tabular alternatives are separately registered and input/search matched.

## Exact action reference

E0 uses the current eligible BBO midpoint derived from native MBP-1 at each completed one-minute decision cut as its contact observation. This is a price-only control at sampled decision times, not a replacement of MBP-1 by the vendor bbo-1m schema. It does not backdate an entry to an intervening tick touch; the full eligible native events still drive fills, protection and future-path labels. The deterministic rule benchmark acts only on the first inward contact observed on this sampled decision grid in a new visit with an E0 object while flat: fade the approach direction. Define approach from the previous valid observation outside the band; unknown approach produces no rule trade. A new visit requires leaving by max(4 ticks,.05W) before returning. Simultaneous candidates are ordered by shortest current distance, then oldest birth, then stable ID. Same-price sources remain logged. This rule is only a source-comparison policy; it is not adopted as a universal fade thesis.

The learned challenger may choose long, short or flat from the same available contact set using the estimated complete bracket outcome distribution and conservative expected net value >0. One full-unit marketable entry, one full-unit target and one stop-market protective exit; no scale-in, partial exit or direct position reversal. Initial stop distance is `ceil(max(8 ticks,.10W)/tick)*tick`; if range W is unavailable this bracket is unavailable. Target distances are 1× and 2× stop distance, evaluated as separately registered fixed policies rather than selecting the better realized exit. Exit at stop, target or 15-minute deadline, whichever is first observable and executable, also respecting the earlier forced boundary. No discretionary stop widening or trailing in E0. After closing, a new decision may enter on a new eligible visit.

The order replay uses the standing venue BBO at arrival, with separate feed and order latency scenarios. The initial reference assumes 250ms source-feed lag, 250ms decision-to-venue delay and one extra adverse impact tick per side. Stress feed lag at 0/250/1,000ms, outbound delay at 50/250/1,000ms and impact at 0/1/2 ticks separately, plus the joint 1,000ms/1,000ms/two-tick adverse corner; these are assumptions pending telemetry, not a full unbounded Cartesian search. Completed bars become available only after their completion plus the chosen feed/processing delay. Explicit spread crossing is already in bid/ask prices and is not added twice. Actual commissions/exchange/broker fees are a required numeric manifest input drawn from the verified product schedule; absent fees block the economic run rather than defaulting to zero. Exact receipt/queue claims are unavailable where the archive lacks them. E0 uses marketable orders, while passive alternatives belong to a separate P05 experiment.

P08 enforces one mini and the $1,000 daily loss objective through current marked net, stop/cost/tail reservation and any stricter verified account floor. The planning manifest sets a conservative scenario reserve and rejects a bracket that cannot fit; no outcome-driven reserve calibration. Measure breaches under gap/slippage scenarios rather than claiming stops guarantee a maximum realized loss. Before a firm is selected, report a synthetic research account separately from named-product scenarios. P11 counts every eligible day, including flat, outage and halted days, with trading net and business cash separate.

## What passing E0 establishes

All raw-to-label/order/account reference fixtures must reconcile; then report always-flat, deterministic rule, conditional-frequency and regularized-model results on identical dates. Include labels, no-contact/censor counts, complete decision sets, fills, costs, daily net, occupied time, risk breaches and processing latency. Positive or negative performance does not by itself validate or refute the richer architecture. A failed source baseline can coexist with a useful interaction or improved generator; tests must establish that increment.

Use the [quality framework](MODEL_QUALITY_AND_STOPPING_RULES.md) for planted-signal controls and any exact in-set oracle diagnostic. The first expansion compares all eligible calendar dates and other sessions before richer experts are credited with a different sample. M01/M02/M09/M10/M13 event measurements are built in B02 and may inform Context/Location before detailed Response. Compare trade-only, quote-state-only and combined action/flow features at identical decision cuts; change event-triggered cadence in a separate registered comparison with correct availability and recalibration. E1–E4 replace one information/definition/model/policy family at a time and run registered interactions. Detailed Response remains E5. Genuine future evaluation begins only after the final protocol and versions are frozen.
