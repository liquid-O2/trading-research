# Primary research added during the third design review

Reviewed 2026-09-06. These bounded primary-source checks clarify product identity and inference. They supplement the existing 114 external findings; they do not replace the source ledger or constitute trading evidence. Proposed modeling extensions below are our engineering hypotheses, not claims that a cited author proved NQ/ES profitability.

| ID | Primary source and inspected scope | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| R3-TECH-01 | [Cboe VIX option specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-options/specifications), product identity, multiplier and current trading/expiration hours | F02/O02/C16 must distinguish VIX/VIXW index options and their exact settlement clock. Version historical conventions; current hours cannot simply be imposed on all old data. |
| R3-TECH-02 | [Cboe options on VX futures white paper](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/membership/Options_on_VX_Futures_Whitepaper.pdf), product comparison table on page 5 and settlement discussion on page 17 | VX options have a distinct payoff/underlying and physically deliver the front-month VX future; VIX options are cash settled. The acquired VIX OPRA files do not establish an acquired UX options-on-futures stream. Keep that extension behind its own data gate. |
| R3-TECH-03 | [Cboe VVIX paper](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/documents/vvix-termstructure.pdf), introduction and Appendix 1 | Liquid VIX-option prices support implied volatility-of-volatility research. Use an option-implied forward and an explicitly defined strip/tenor calculation; VIX spot is not the default pricing underlying. An internal reconstruction must disclose quote and methodology differences. |
| R3-TECH-04 | [Cboe broad-based volatility-index methodology](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/governance/Volatility_Index_Methodology_Selected_Broad_Based_Index_Equity_and_ETF_Volatility_Indices.pdf), VVIX constituent, source, selection and dissemination entries | Current VVIX uses standard monthly AM-settled VIX options, excludes series below eight days, uses Cboe C1 data and a specified nearest-term method. A generic all-expiry NBBO calculation is a different internal index. Exact replication requires the historical rule version and source filtering. |
| R3-TECH-05 | [Howard, Ramdas, McAuliffe and Sekhon, confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08240v3), introduction and Section 2 assumptions | Extending a test until a conventional confidence interval looks favorable requires a different inferential procedure. Time-uniform methods have explicit process/tail assumptions and estimands; do not assume a generic trading-P&L sequence satisfies them. Use a fixed endpoint as the initial protocol, or justify a preregistered sequential alternative. |

The 2012 VVIX exposition and the current methodology serve different purposes. The former explains the construction; the latter supplies current constituent/source rules. Historical replication must use effective-dated rules. The bounded archive exercise in [VIX feasibility](review/third-review/VIX_FEASIBILITY.md) is an internal parity/IV feasibility check with declared assumptions, not an official VVIX replication, historical arbitrage claim or new market-data acquisition.
