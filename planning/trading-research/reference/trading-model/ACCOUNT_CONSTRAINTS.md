# Account constraints and economic accounting

Research snapshot: **2026-09-06 UTC**. These are public rule observations, not confirmation of the user's account, purchase version, permissions, or signed agreement. Re-check the selected account before any later deployment. No account has been purchased, connected, or traded.

## Primary economic experiment

The user's clarified experiment is one account and one mini contract in one selected instrument, preferably NQ: position is flat, long one, or short one. There is no micro substitution, pyramiding, partial exit, or copied-account scaling. The target remains average net P&L of at least $2,000 over eligible trading days, including zero-trade days; the daily loss objective is at most $1,000. The target is a feasibility question, not an achieved result. Mandatory flat times apply at the trading-day boundary, while activity during evening/Asian/London hours within the same trading day remains eligible.

CME specifies $20 per NQ index point and 0.25-point/$5 ticks; ES is $50 per point and 0.25-point/$12.50 ticks. Thus the target requires an average 100 net NQ points or 40 net ES points per day before translating monetary costs into extra gross points. A $1,000 loss is 50 NQ points or 20 ES points before costs. These are arithmetic translations, not minimum daily range or a mandated trade count. [NQ contract specifications](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/nasdaq/e-mini-nasdaq-100.contractSpecs.html), [CME multiplier and tick explanation](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-equity-index-products/discover-equity-index-notional-value-and-price).

Maintain three distinct ledgers: (1) strategy net trading P&L after execution costs, (2) account equity and rule state including unrealized P&L, and (3) withdrawable cash after firm splits, payout caps, subscription/evaluation/data costs, and withdrawals. The planning recommendation is to use (1) for the primary research target and report (3) alongside it; the user has not specified that $2,000 must be daily withdrawable income. Do not hide that accounting choice. No simulated profit or evaluation pass is a received payout.

## Rule-state interface to specify and test

`AccountRuleState` must include version/provenance, session id, current position/orders, realized and marked unrealized P&L, fee treatment, EOD high-water mark, current floor, lock state, internal daily budget, optional firm DLL, payout-cycle profit/day maxima, winning/active day counters, duration eligibility, maximum permitted position, news windows, and flatten deadline. Rule updates require a versioned transition, not mutable constants applied retrospectively.

Position entry is admissible only if worst planned stop loss plus execution reserve fits the smaller remaining internal daily budget and account-floor distance, while all operational rules permit entry. Gap losses can exceed a planned stop; record breaches and exceedance magnitudes rather than claiming a guaranteed hard monetary cap. Risk exits override payout optimization. Fees and spread/slippage scenarios must be selected and frozen in the validation plan, with actual platform charges verified before deployment.

No firm is selected. Outstanding account-dependent details are purchase/reset version, platform, optional DLL choice, payout schedule/caps and fee schedule, exact signed automation terms, and any ambiguous time-zone wording. These do not block architecture or historical research; they block claims of deployment readiness for a particular account.

## Final LucidPro payout and drawdown check (2026-09-06)

The current [Pro drawdown page](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890136-lucidpro-drawdown) specifies EOD trailing floors: a $50k account has a $2,000 MLL, a $52,100 initial trail balance and a $50,100 locked floor. Its wording says exceeding the trail locks it, while reaching MLL breaches. Equality and intraday balance semantics belong in the signed account-version fixtures.

The [Pro payout page](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890092-lucidpro-payouts) states a 90/10 split, profits above the buffer, a $500 minimum withdrawal, and cycle-dependent caps. For $50k, the buffer is $52,100; the first payout cap is $2,000 and subsequent cap $2,500. A $500 minimum cycle profit also applies. Payout requests are final and pending payouts must be reserved from usable risk capital. These cash rules are separate from daily strategy P&L.

The [Pro consistency page](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890109-lucidpro-consistency-percentage) applies 40% largest-day/cycle-profit for accounts purchased or reset on/after 2025-11-28 15:00 EST, with 35% for older versions; funded consistency ceases after live promotion. The page's exact-boundary wording overlaps ('on or before' and 'on or after'); preserve this as an account-version ambiguity if relevant. No account was selected or purchased.
