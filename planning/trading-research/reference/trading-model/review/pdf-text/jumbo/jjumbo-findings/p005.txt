 4.3 Asia / midnight as live liquidity, then as the NY draw
 SDRange “does Asia, London, New York, plus any custom time” (4 Nov 2025 reply). NY recaps treat leftover Asia/London
 highs and lows as the target after a 09:40 reverse: “textbook longs after 9:40 to London/Asia highs” (30 Dec 2025);
 “unfinished business at London highs” (18 Nov 2025); “10 am p-zone > london low” (2 Jan 2026). Lines stay until purged,
 then they are done.

 4.4 09:00–12:00 statistical envelope (SessionStat)
 Not a trade clock. A target map. 6 Jul 2026: “range exhaustion levels and 9–12 statistical session boundaries both meeting up
  nicely.” A+ when today’s −0.5 / 1.33 sits on the 9–12 average/median extreme. SessionStat public page: custom sessions (up
  to four), or fixed 15m / 1H / 4H / Daily / Weekly; lookback configurable; simple or weighted average; expected high/low
 plus expansion. “London H4 stats” is this overlay on a 4-hour / London window.

 4.5 15-minute (sometimes 5-minute) opening range — mid only
 He said he does not really use classic ORs. 16 Jul 2026: “two retracement-to-mid stats at play from the lows, 6–9 range and
 15m OR” — and he still went one-and-done without taking the mid long. 19 May 2026: “don’t really use ORs that much, i
 sometime use the 5m OR for reference or the 15m… in that chart is a time based range.”

 4.6 Prior-day RTH and “in value / in range”
 28 Jul 2026 reply: “RTH open location in relation to previous day value/range and the current day 6–9.” 10 Jul 2026:
 “expected mean reversion day… RTH open inside previous day value/range, scalping territory, continuation trades got rekt.”
 28 Aug 2026 A+  list: “range double break expectancy (in value/in range).” This is Dalton-style location, not a holy
 premium/discount box. The PD RTH product later grew a premium/discount feature; treat that as optional paint, not the
 edge. Location vs prior-day value is the part that keeps showing up in recaps.

 4.7 Lunch / PM / MOC
 Lunch is a documented leak. 12 Jun 2026: caught the morning shorts range-high to range-low, “gave it all back during lunch
  trying to reach a daily target,” then next London session 3/3 for 354. AM expansion days often become PM consolidation.
