       Figure. Core sequences — single break (left) vs sweep-then-reverse (right). Red = untouched edge. Blue = swept edge. Green OP = range open.

 Left — single break. One edge taken, opposite edge still red/clean. Price breaks, returns to range open (OP), continues in
 the break direction. Open is an entry magnet, not a fade. Mid of the range is the other common entry (27 Jul 2026: “single
 break behaviour through A period, range mid provided the entry area”).
 Right — Judas / double. One edge swept (blue), opposite edge still clean (red) = the draw. Sweep extends to exhaustion
 (−0.5 / mean-reversal / P-zone), then reverses through mid toward the untouched edge.
