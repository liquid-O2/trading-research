 1. What the public record actually says
 JJumbo presents as an NQ futures day trader who productized a time-based range. The bio is the thesis: “Time-Based Ranges
   | 6-9 range ideator.” The 6–9 box is the SKU. The operating system underneath is larger.
 Three sentences from him do more work than most threads:

     “Context — location — confirmation.” (28 Aug 2026 recap)

    “Same principles, same logic, different times of action.” (13 Oct 2025, London range week)

     “Almost any time window between 5am and 9am will behave similarly, that’s not a wheel rediscovery.” (27 Aug
     2026)

 He is a reversal trader first. Continuation is allowed only after the overnight (or the prior session) has already done the
 stop-hunt work. He ends most A+ NY days in the first 20 minutes. He posts both +140 and −60 sessions. Management, not
  level selection, is the leak he names on losing days (“diabolical BEs”).
 Public tools named on X / TradingView: SDRange+ (later SDRange+²), SessionStat+, Absorption Zone+, PD RTH Range+,
 P-zones, 3-day liquidity map. Execution screenshots in 2026 are mostly NinjaTrader: RTH-scoped volume profile, delta
  profile, footprint, BigTrades bubbles.

 2. The portable machine (not just 6–9)
 Treat every session as the same four-step loop.
     1. Build. A clock window prints a high, low, open, mid, 25/75.
     2. Classify. Size, balance vs imbalance, which edge is still clean, where the next session opens versus prior-day value,
    whether sister indices already hunted the same side.
     3. Trade the next clock. Either fade a sweep (Judas / double) or participate in a single break from mid / open / quadrant.
    The “open analog” is 09:30 for NY, ~03:00 for London, 08:20 for gold, 09:00 for crude.
     4. Confirm at location. Wick / 3-candle reclaim, absorption or delta taper, VP shelf, P-zone, leftover session high/low,
     SessionStat envelope. One of these alone is not a trade.
  If you only run 06:00–09:00 into 09:30 NY, you are running one costume. Weeks exist where he says London is the cleaner
 move and NY AM is leftover draw. Weeks exist where he is in gold on Sunday using RTH range + 18:00 P-zones. The
 machine does not change.

 5. Classification layer (the 2026 edge)
 The naive read of early 2025 posts is “always fade 6–9 at 09:40.” Later posts are a filter on that idea.

 5.1 Range size
 8 Jun 2026, with VP + footprint attached: at ~1.2% range size, very low chance of double break; combined single-break
 (only high or only low) > 74% across his sample; retrace to midpoint still ~60.4%. 27 Aug 2026: small ranges that print a
 high double-break percentage are usually skewed samples — “most of it isn’t applicable as edge.” Base rate he published:
 “~45% of pre market ranges do in fact single break during AM. Don’t be surprised if your breakout isn’t failing.”

 5.2 Balance vs imbalance
 27 Aug 2026: “Balanced — imbalanced overnight matters.” 7 Jul 2026: “simple notion taken from Dalton’s book on market
 profiling… identifying range break behaviour (single/double) with a pretty good probabilistic %.” Balanced overnight +
 open inside prior-day value → mean-reversion / failed-break bias. Imbalanced / already-purged overnight + open outside
 value → discard mean reversion. 28 Jul 2026: “discard mean reversion and range double breaks when these things align.
 +138.”

 5.3 IB-stat cargo cult
 26 Aug 2026: “IB stats have been performing poorly recently. There was never sustainable edge… curve fit or not enough
 data sample. Don’t be fooled by it.” Use this day’s 6–9 size + balance + prior-day location, not a generic “IB always fades.”

 Upgrade: a one-line classifier before the open.

 TradingView changelog (public): fixed distance; volatility and volume filtered; multi-ticker; auto-learning lookback on
 previous N sessions; custom session times; used as exhaustion and as S/R for reversals.
 How he points at them on X, never lectured: “9:30 pzone,” “10am pzone,” “10 am p-zone > london low,” “9am p-zones,”
 gold “6pm P-zones,” “one and done on the early fade… P-zone + low > range open. Full reversal during 9:40–9:50.”

 Upgrade — approximate engine you can actually run:
 Anchor a session (6–9, London into 03:00, RTH, 18:00). Measure distance bands from open and from mid that kept reacting
 over the last N similar-vol sessions. Keep a band only if realized range and session volume of those lookback days sit near
 today (drop the band on a holiday tape). Overlay session VP: keep the band if it sits on an HVN or on the shelf next to an
 LVN. That stack — distance + vol regime + volume node — is the public definition. The unpublished part is only the exact
 auto-learn weights.

 A lone P-zone in air, with no TBR level and no node, is not his trade. On 2 Jan 2026 the fade was P-zone and 6–9 low above range open.

 10. Session rotation and unfinished business
 Ranges talk to each other. That is how NY stops looking random.
    Fade 6–9 at −0.5 toward leftover Asia/London high or low.
      If London already purged both sides, NY often will not Judas. That is the “discard mean reversion” alignment.
     Relative strength overnight between NQ and ES (and YM) is a model selector. He rarely writes SMT; the charts and the
    6–9 single-break scenario describe the same thing.
    Which session is clean this week is a real input. London-clean weeks: trade 03:00–06:00 as A+, use NY as management
     or as the draw. NY-clean weeks: ignore the urge to invent a London home run after 09:30.

  Unfinished business is a target, not an entry by itself.
