The concept behind the PD RTH Range+ is to only focus on the RTH (Regular Trading
Hours) price action (9:30 - 16:00) and its key levels to get your overall daily direction for
the entirety of the trading session.
You want to use its key levels especially highs and lows as draw on liquidity and
imbalances (M15 and H1)
