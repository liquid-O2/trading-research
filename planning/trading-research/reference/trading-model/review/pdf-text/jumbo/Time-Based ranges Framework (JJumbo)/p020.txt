Reversal scenario





In the reversal scenario, when we’re looking for price signatures, all we want to identify is how
price is behaving at our anticipated levels.
when we’re expecting a reversal, you can notice how candles form at the levels, constant rejection
and multiple wicks they are usually a hint of price not willing to expand further but rather about to
reverse not having enough fuel to continue its direction
