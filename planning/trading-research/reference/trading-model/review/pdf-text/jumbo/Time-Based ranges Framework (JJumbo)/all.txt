Framework Overview

The framework is based entirely on time-based ranges that appear through
out the day during electronic trading hours and regular trading hours (all
times i refer to during the entirety of this document will be in Eastern
Standard Time, ET).

Markets often exhibit rhythmic behavior tied to sessions, where liquidity and
volatility spike predictably for order accumulations and breakouts

The idea behind this and how i view ranges is that every range has
measurable exhaustion points where price absorbes orders accumulated
during the range window and reverses or breaks after hitting trapped stops
to then distribute to post-breakouts targets often corresponding to the range
extensions. It usually happens in the form of “False Breakout” or judas swing
as ICT describes it (which is one of the models later presented) or just “Range
breakout”.

Our job is to anticipate these moves in the exhaustion areas and position
ourselves during specific time windows during the trading session where
statistically these moves form.

This logic can be applied to multiple ranges present throughout the day
helping you identify these moves and the signatures in price at said levels.
This system is fairly simple and a rules-based system focusing on time and
price signatures can reduce overthinking, making it actionable if backtested
and followed consistently.

Trading Set Ups (section a.)
6-9 Model (main New York AM model)

Time range selection: 6:00am - 9:00am

    Judas Model




 This model is what i define as my A+ bread and butter, always terminating my
 trading session before 10am. Quick in and out and easy to spot providing on
 average 80 -120 pts, it usually presents two distinct trade opportunities, the
 judas trades from 9:30 to the reversal window and the actual reversal trade
between the 9:40 and 9:50.

   Trade #1

As stated above, this model presents two trades with two levels of difficulty, the
first trade is where we position ourselves right at the open where we participate
in the judas move (false breakout) towards the projections of the range where
the reversal will take place.
The time window of this trade spans from 9:30 open until the 9:40 -9:50 reversal
window where the real move will happen. I suggest to have positions closed as
we reach the reversal window, the yield of this trade outcome will vary
depending on the size of the 6-9 range (e.g. the distances from open to
projection levels) application of risk to reward needs to be considered)

   Trade #2

The second trade in this model is easier to trade, filters out the volatility of the
open and allows you to let price develop before taking place in any position
avoiding to be knocked out of your trade.
Here is where we participate in the reversal during the 9:40 - 9:50 window, levels
of interest will be the mean reversal levels or -0.5 projection.
By this time we are already in position to ride the actual move after the fake
breakout, price will move faster and cleaner also allowing you to build a larger
position towards the higher projections of the range and compound on the
winners.

    Single Breakout Model


In this model, taking in consideration the size of the overnight range is really
important (i will later discuss in the “expectation section”), the size of the ONS
range will give you a hint whether we will have a false breakout or just a
breakout distributing from the EQ or quadrants.
Always take in consideration the table below to know the size of the range

This is usually a slower trade where i lower my expectation in terms of profit
taking and extension reaching, you want to be in the trade before 10am an look
to close position around 9:40-9:50 window.


Scenario #1 (Extended overnight range)
In the case of having an extended overnight range, the EQ or quadrants become
our levels on interest.
This is usually where i lower my expectations during AM session, the 6-9 range
high/low are sufficient as target for the most part, the projections are off the
table and after 10am all interest in being in a position is not longer present.
Price tends to consolidate all the way through lunch/PM session


Scenario #2 (Stop Hunts - relative strength/weakness between indices
during overnight)
When overnight presents stop hunts/ swing failure scenarios and every
significant overnight liquidity has been purged , During AM session price usually
don’t present a fake breakout to knock people out of positions, we can
anticipate price expanding from the inner range levels (EQ, Quadrants).
In this case scenario my targets expectation increases, we can notice a shrank
overnight range leading to explosive expansions during the AM session
discarding the 9:40 9:50 window to be of any interest for reversals but rather
use it as continuation where i would look to add to my position.

As you can notice, on all set ups i keep “Show RTH candles?” toggled on to only
highlight candles from 9:30 onwards as i only look to enter positions after open.

Expectations (section e.)
“Refer to (section b. Market Conditions)”

In this section, we will see how to adjust our expectations based on conditions.
This is also important as it will dictate the outcome of your trades management
it will be useful to limit unnecessary losses, roundtrip trades, lock-in profits at
the right time.

Based on what we talked about in the previous section (“market conditions”)
we will adopt different approaches on the framework.

Extended Overnight Session (6am -9pm) usually present on 8:30 red folder news

    Targets
Targets in these conditions will be limited to the high and low of the overnight range
until lunch time.
Having an extended overnight range, these targets will suffice for the entirety of the AM
session, price will be slower and harder to engage in the market
    Risk
Risk should be considerably moderated, invalidations for you entries will be a lot larger
than usual depending on your entry model.
Price actin will be grindy for the most part with deep retracements knocking you out of
your position.


Range-bound price action leading to news days
  Targets
Targets in these conditions will be limited to the high and low of the overnight range or
inner range levels in case of outside of range openings
A scalping approach should be adopted with in and outs and considerably reduced
Risk/Reward trades
  Risk
Risk should be considerably moderated, invalidations for you entries will be a lot larger
than usual depending on your entry model.
Price actin will be grindy for the most part with deep retracements knocking you out of
your position.

Expansive days - favourable market conditions
    Targets
Targets in these conditions should be exponentially higher, higher projections of the
range will be highly probable allowing you to hold positions for longer, here is where you
want to compound on your winners and let runners run.
Risk/Reward increases and price action will be a lot easier to engage.
    Risk
Risk can be increased, and multiple entry opportunities will be provided.
In case of being knocked out of your position, a re - entry is considered to be plausible
due to fast and one-directional price action.                  RMC Strategy

Confluences

Here is where we will make use of the other indicators provided in the collection, to
reinforce our trade ideas.

The SDRange framework already is a standalone model, but with the use of additional
confluence we will have more confidence in the trade idea and direction.

Absorption zone+
The concept behind this indicator is based on "absorption" in trading, which typically
refers to when large players are absorbing (buying or selling) a significant amount of
volume without moving the price much. This often happens at key levels (Pairing
SDRange framework with its key levels) and can signal a potential reversal or
continuation by highlighting the candles/zone where this takes place.

The small body with high volume suggests that despite significant trading activity,
prices didn't move much in one direction - possibly indicating that large buyers or
sellers are "absorbing" the opposing pressure, potentially before a move in their favor.

Model and Set Ups Failure

Recognizing Failure Signals

Failed 6-9 Model Reversal
   No Rejection Signature: When price reaches the projection levels during the 9:40-
    9:50 window but shows no rejection candles or orderblocks
    Continuous Momentum: Price continues through projection levels with strong
   momentum and volume, showing no signs of exhaustion
    Extended Bodies: Instead of wicks at projection levels, candles show extended
    bodies in the direction of the trend
     Daily direction mis-interpretation

Time Window Violations
    Missing the 9:40-9:50 Window: When price reverses significantly before or after our
     critical time window
    Extended Moves: Price moves beyond expected extensions without respecting
    time-based pivots
    Premature Reversals: Reversal occurs well before or after reaching the expected
    projection levels (e.g 10am news)

Risk Management During Failures

Early Recognition and Exit
     3-Strike Rule: If price attempts to reverse 3 times at a level without follow-through,
    consider the setup failed
   Volume Divergence: When price reaches critical levels but without corresponding
    volume signature
    Tight Initial Stops: Using the aggressive stop placement mentioned in the entry
    models section to limit damage on failed setups
Adjusting After Recognition
     Exit and Observe: Once a setup is confirmed as failed, exit positions and observe for
    the remainder of the session
    Switch to Alternative Models: Consider using different setups like the Single
    Breakout Model or look for opportunities in PM session
    Reduce Position Size: If continuing to trade after a failed setup, reduce your risk
     allocation by at least 50%

Key Takeaways

   1. Failing Fast: Recognizing failures quickly preserves capital and mental energy
   2. Contextual Awareness: Understanding why a setup failed improves future decision-
    making
   3. Statistical Mindset: No setup works 100% of the time; respect the statistical nature
    of market edges
   4. Risk Management: Having pre-defined failure protocols prevents emotional
    decision-making

Conclusion

focusing on specific time windows and price behaviors, traders can develop a
structured framework that reduces subjective decision-making and emotional
interference.
Take your time to go through the content and train your eye to see the framework in
action and get a good grasp of it.

     significance of key time windows, particularly the 9:40-9:50 reversal window with

     clear guidelines for modifying expectations and risk management during different
     scenarios.
   4. Risk Management Integration: From precise entry models to failure recognition
     protocols, risk management is embedded throughout the entire trading process.
   5. Systematic Execution: Through consistent application of the tools and models
    presented, traders can develop a repeatable process for engaging with the
    markets.

Remember that success with this methodology requires:
    Consistent application of the principles and tools
    Proper risk management with each trade
    Recognition of favorable versus unfavorable conditions
    Regular review and refinement of your approach
