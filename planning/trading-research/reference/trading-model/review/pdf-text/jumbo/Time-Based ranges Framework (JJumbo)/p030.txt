Statistical Approach

 The information in this section are based on 20 years E-mini Nasdaq 100
 demonstrating and compounding the model characteristics over the course of
 the years.

 It displays the key times of the significant moves and behaviour discussed so
 far.

 All the data has been provided by “CME Group” which i then used and run
 through Python to gather statistics based on my model criteria.





 The two graphs above are displaying the highest count of reversal happening at a specific time
 window (left) which is what we discussed so far, the 9:40 9:50 reversal window.
 And on the right, the overall percentage of reversals happening at the -0.5 projection of the range
 (86.46%).
 This analysis was done on 4537 days of data.





In this graph instead, we can see how significant are also the “mean reversal levels” projections,
holding a very high percentage of reversal across all the days analysed.

All data statistics are based on the 9:00am - 12:00pm time window, which is where we frame most
of our set ups.
