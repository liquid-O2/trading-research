Delayed cycle 2 move with 10am news examples





 On news days with a 10:00 am release, we often observe a delayed cycle 2
 move. Instead of the expected reversal occurring between 9:40 and 9:50 (where
 a Judas swing typically happens), the real reversal takes place after the 10:00
 am news.
