In the graphs above i’ve highlighted the entire trading day, segmented into key
time windows, to define the price behavior during each period. This helps me
manage my positions with clear expectations.

I focus on the 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM window as a whole to frame my setups,
expecting specific price behavior during this period (which can be interpreted
as a 3-hour "PO3"). This window captures most of the morning session’s
volume and significant price movements.

Within the 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM window, I’ve identified two time-defined entry
and take-profit zones to guide my trades when paired with my framework. In
both scenarios, trade opportunities arise at the open (9:30 AM) and during the
9:40 AM to 9:50 AM window, with take-profit points along the way.

For each window, I manage my position by taking profits, adding to my
position, or closing it entirely. When combined with my framework/indicator,
this approach provides specific levels to act upon—essentially creating a matrix
where Time and Price are integrated. This allows me to dissect the trading day
into distinct cycles, during which price behaves differently.
