Sessions Correlation
Understanding the relationships between trading sessions provides valuable context
for anticipating price behavior throughout the day. The following represent common
correlations between AM (9:30-12:00), Lunch (12:00-13:00), and PM (13:00-16:00)
sessions. There are other Profiles throughout the day but this is what we will focus on to
trade my framework.

                          AM Consolidation → PM Expansion

                                 When the AM session trades in a
                                        narrow range with low directional
                                               conviction
                                       Lunch session often continues the
                                               consolidation
                              PM session frequently breaks out of
                                           the range with stronger directional
                                momentum
                                                  Target: Higher projections from the
                               AM session range





                          AM Expansion → PM Consolidation

                                            Strong momentum and range
                                           expansion during AM session
                                            Exhaustion of the move leads to
                                           choppy, range-bound activity in
                                       Lunch through PM
                              PM often consolidates between key
                               AM session levels
