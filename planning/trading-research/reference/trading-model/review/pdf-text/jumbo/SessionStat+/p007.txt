 Accuracy & Data Detection


The levels in the indicator may differ when switching to a timeframe
below 2 minutes by design, to gather additional accuracy in the
levels. Consider using the indicator above 2-3 minute time-frame
(unless using the 1 hour or 15 minute non-custom chosen session)

Bar-Based Calculations and Session Detection:
   The indicator uses bar_index and time-based conditions to
    detect the start and end of periods or sessions. On lower
    timeframes, there are more bars per session, which can lead to
   more frequent updates or slight variations in how session
    boundaries are detected due to timestamp granularity.
    For example, a 1-minute chart has 5 times more bars than a 5-
   minute chart, so the exact bar where a session starts or ends
   might differ slightly, affecting when the indicator initializes or
   updates session data.


Practical Implications:
    Visual Differences: On timeframes below 5 minutes, you might
    notice levels appearing at slightly different prices or extending to
    different bars due to the finer granularity of data and potential
    misalignment in session detection.
   Performance Considerations: Lower timeframes generate more
    bars, increasing computational load and potentially causing the
    indicator to process data differently, especially for custom
    sessions with frequent updates.
