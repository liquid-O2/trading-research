Absorption, the trap





Lesson 5 gave you the ladder. This lesson gives you the two behaviours on it that actually make entries: absorption,
and pulling and stacking.





Absorption is a trap. Retail attacks a level, institutions feed on the
attack.





  HOW THE TRAP WORKS

    Lots of retail traders buy at a key resistance, an old high, expecting the breakout. Big institutions use exactly those buy
    orders to sell into. The big side has more power, price drops quickly, and the retail buyers are left stuck in losing
    positions.





   THE SIGNATURE

   Big market orders hitting your level that do not push price through it. The aggression arrives, the level eats it,
    price reverses. That is absorption, and it is one of the highest quality triggers on the ladder.
