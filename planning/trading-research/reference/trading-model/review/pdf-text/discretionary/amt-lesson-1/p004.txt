Balance and imbalance


Price always moves to find balance. When it moves out of balance it is in imbalance, and it will do one of two things:
return to the old balance, or create a new one somewhere else. That is the whole engine.





Roughly 70% of the data in the bell curve is the balance. The fat part of the profile is where the market agreed.

The practical use: treat balance and imbalance areas as levels to play off with the DOM or the footprint. They are your
dynamic support and resistance. The area tells you where the trade lives, the tape tells you when to take it.





Balance and imbalance marked on NQ. Price dips out of balance, uses it as support, then pushes to seek the next balance.
The note on the right is the 80% rule at work.
