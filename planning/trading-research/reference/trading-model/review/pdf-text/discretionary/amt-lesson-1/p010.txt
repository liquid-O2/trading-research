Day types



Sessions are not all the same animal. AMT sorts them into day types, and knowing which one you are in by late
morning decides which layer of your edge is even allowed to trade.




   TREND DAY              Opens near one extreme, closes near the other. Value migrates all session, every
                                pullback is shallow and gets bought or sold again. The profile prints long and thin.
                                Continuation only, never fade it.





   NORMAL DAY           A wide early range, then rotation inside it for the rest of the session. The profile
                                  fattens into a D. Fade the extremes, target the POC.





   NORMAL VARIATION        The early range gets extended once, roughly doubling it, then the market balances
                                      in the new area. One directional push, then rotation. Trade the push, then switch
                                  to fading.





   NEUTRAL DAY             Range extension on both sides of the open, the auction genuinely undecided.
                               Close in the middle means confusion, close at an extreme tells you who won late.
                           Keep size small until it picks.





   NON TREND DAY          Nobody shows up. Narrow, quiet, usually in front of news. The edge is knowing
                                there is no edge, stand down or scalp tiny.





   THE POINT

   Most blown accounts are a day type error: running continuation on a normal day, or fading a trend day because
    price looks far from value. Name the day before you size the trade.
