What AMT actually is





First things first: auction market theory is not a strategy. It is a framework, a way of reading what the market is
actually doing at any moment. You do not trade AMT on its own. You use it to know where you are on the map, then
you let the entry triggers do the entering.



The market only ever does two things: balance and imbalance.

  WHY IT MATTERS

   Every tool in the method sits on top of this. If you cannot say whether the market is balancing or imbalancing
    right now, no entry trigger can save you, because you do not know which kind of trade the market is offering.
