03 The account and the session



  the MFFU series, and why not being eligible yet is part of the point


  This is a recap of a live AM session on a MyFundedFutures funded account, the first in a series

  that follows one account from a fresh evaluation pass through to its first payout request. He

  runs at least three live sessions a week for the mentorship, and this is one of them, unedited:

  the thesis before a single order went out, then the two trades that came from it.





  Where the account actually stood going into this session, payout requirements and the calendar together.
  Not eligible yet, on purpose shown here rather than skipped: two days since the first trade against a 14

   series is the process, not just the highlight.
