What the DOM actually is


Everything so far has been context: the auction, the profiles, the volatility. This is where the method gets its trigger. The
DOM, the depth of market, shows you the live orders at every price.

The market only moves for one reason: buyers and sellers. The DOM
is where you watch them do it.



   BIDS, THE LEFT SIDE                                  ASKS, THE RIGHT SIDE

   The list of buy limit orders below price. Buyers waiting         The list of sell limit orders above price. Sellers waiting
    to be filled, the liquidity underneath the market.                  to be filled, the liquidity overhead.

Aggressive vs passive





Every participant on the ladder is one of two kinds, and the whole game is watching how they interact.





   AGGRESSIVE, MARKET ORDERS

   They cross the spread and hit the ask or the bid because they want the fill immediately. Best ask 5000.25, best bid
   5000.00, an aggressive buyer clicks buy market and instantly pays 5000.25, they are lifting the offer. Aggression is
    urgency and conviction, this is what actually moves price.





   PASSIVE, LIMIT ORDERS

   They place a bid or an offer and wait. A passive buyer rests a limit at 4999.75 and waits for sellers to come to him.
    Passive because they never chase, they provide the liquidity the aggressive side consumes.





   THE INTERACTION IS THE MARKET

    Price moves when aggression eats through passivity. Every level that holds or breaks, every wick, every trend
    leg is that one contest, repeated.

Delta, the running score





Delta is the running score of that contest. It is the net volume of market buys minus market sells at each price level.





   POSITIVE DELTA                                     NEGATIVE DELTA

   More aggressive buyers than sellers. The buying is real,        More aggressive sellers than buyers. Market orders are
   market orders are lifting offers and pushing price up.               hitting bids, pressure is pushing price down.

   THE READ THAT MATTERS

   Delta against price is the tell. Heavy positive delta while price goes nowhere means someone is absorbing the

Speed of tape and the spread





Two things on the ladder that beginners never look at, and both change the read completely: the speed of the tape,
and the width of the spread.





   SPEED OF TAPE                                    THE SPREAD

   How fast the time and sales prints scroll. A tape that           The gap between best bid and best ask. It stays one
   suddenly rips at a level is urgency arriving. A tape that             tick when liquidity is deep and healthy. It widens the
    freezes at a level is hesitation, or a big passive order              instant liquidity thins out, which is your earliest
    soaking everything up. The footprint is the history, the          warning that a level is about to break or a fast move is
   speed is the pulse.                                         coming.





   FAST TAPE INTO A LEVEL    Aggression is committed. If the level still holds, that is absorption worth trading. If
                                                   it breaks, the speed carries the continuation.





   SPREAD WIDENS              Liquidity is pulling. Do not lean on the level, and do not chase into the gap, the fills
                                get ugly and the stops get run.





   THE FULL PICTURE

   Depth tells you what is resting, delta tells you who is aggressive, speed tells you how urgent, and the spread
    tells you how healthy. Read all four and the ladder stops being numbers and starts being a conversation.

The ladder as your trigger




The DOM is the trigger layer of the whole method. The profiles give you the level, the ladder tells you whether the
level is actually being defended.


       Bring a level to the ladder
        Never watch the DOM in a vacuum. Come to it with the shelf, the ledge or the value edge already marked.




      Watch who is aggressive at the level
        Are market orders attacking it or drying up? Urgency arriving at your level is the start of a trade.




      Watch what the passive side does
       Does resting size hold, reload and stack, or does it pull? Liquidity behaviour is the defence being shown or
        abandoned.




       Score it with delta
        Aggression with movement is continuation. Aggression without movement is absorption, somebody big is
       on the other side.




      Then execute, small and precise
       The DOM gives tight entries with tiny stops. That precision is the whole reason this method ends at the
         ladder.
