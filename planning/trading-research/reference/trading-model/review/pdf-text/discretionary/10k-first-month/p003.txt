03 What his numbers actually say



  10k plus, on 1,300 dollars of eval fees


  This is a case study built around one question put to a member directly on a call: how did you

  get to a 10,000 dollar plus month in your first month inside the mentorship. His answer starts

  with a number most traders never actually check. He spent around 1,300 dollars on evaluation

  fees that month. Before joining, the same kind of month cost him closer to 7,000 dollars to

  make 9,000 to 10,000, which is barely a return at all once the eval spend is counted honestly.
