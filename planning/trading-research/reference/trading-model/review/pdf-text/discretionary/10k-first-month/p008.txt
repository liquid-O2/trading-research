08 Trade 2: the expansion leg



  a second tap, absorption, and a thesis that outran its own target


  Going into the session he'd also written down a second possibility: if price retraced, there was

  room for a bigger move higher, a backtest of the same structure from the other side. When price

  came back down to that level and he saw absorption there, buyers stepping in and holding, he

  took the long. Same 1.5R target as the first trade, same process, same discipline of only acting

  once the level actually printed the reaction he'd written down beforehand.


  What happened next wasn't part of the plan. The move expanded well past the 1.5R target on

   its own, an outcome he credits to the setup being right, not to predicting the size of the leg in

  advance. The target was 1.5R. The market gave him considerably more, which is a different

  thing from having called it.





  The long, entered on absorption at the level. The green zone marks how far the actual move ran past the
   original plan.
