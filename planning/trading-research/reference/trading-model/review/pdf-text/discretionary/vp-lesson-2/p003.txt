What the profile is showing you





Lesson 1 gave you the auction: balance, imbalance and the value area. This lesson is about the tool that draws it, the
volume profile, and the two structures inside it that most traders never learn to read.




The profile shows you volume at price, not volume in time. It is a map
of where the business actually happened.





   HVN, HIGH VOLUME NODE                                 LVN, LOW VOLUME NODE

   A price where volume built up. The market spent time        A price where volume fell away. The market rejected it
    here and agreed here. Price gets drawn back to these          and moved fast. Price tends to react at these and
    shelves and slows down inside them.                            traverse them quickly.





HVN and LVN marked on NQ. The build-ups and the fall-offs are the map: value sits where the volume sits.
