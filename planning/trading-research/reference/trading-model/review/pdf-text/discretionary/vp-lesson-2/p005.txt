Real extremes vs lagging levels


Here is the part that actually builds an edge. VAH, VAL and POC are useful, but they recalculate as price moves. They
drift with the session, like a lagging indicator. A ledge does not move.

Shelves and ledges are real extremes. They are structure, not
statistics.





Ledges marked with the red lines. These edges were set by real business and they stay where they were set.





The simpler view again: four ledges around the POC. Fixed reference points on a profile that otherwise keeps changing.
