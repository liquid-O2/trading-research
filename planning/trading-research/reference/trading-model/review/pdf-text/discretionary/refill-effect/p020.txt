Size sets the trade-off between odds and speed

Bet size is the one dial you fully control, and it works in two directions at once. Risk less
per trade and the trailing drawdown almost never catches you, but it takes many more
trading days to grind out the $3,000 target. Risk more and you get there faster, at the cost
of a few percent of pass rate. There is no free lunch, only a curve you get to choose a
point on.
