06Why this is not overfitting

 A profitable backtest is the cheapest object in finance. Anyone who mines enough
  combinations finds a curve that goes up. So we attacked our own result seven ways. It
  survives all seven. This is the strongest claim in the paper.


  01   The hindsight audit. Every feature was tested for information leaks. One failed: a stored field that
         predicted outcomes even at a zone's firsttouch, impossible without contamination. Removed and rebuilt
          cleanly. The edge stood without it.

  02   The scrambled-label placebo. Retrain the identical pipeline on shuffled labels: grading skill collapses to
        a coin flip, and only 0.2% of shuffles match the real result. The machinery cannot manufacture an edge
        from noise.

  03    Strict chronological split. All choices were made on the first half of the data; only the untouched second
          half is quoted throughout this paper.

  04   The reverse split. Train on the latedata, test on the earlydata. The edge holds, slightly stronger. A one-
        regime artifact fails exactly this test.

  05    Rotating folds. Three time-slices, each held out in turn: mean R +0.14 to +0.22, win rate 63–67%,
         grading skill 0.61–0.64 in every slice. No single lucky period carries the result.

  06   The parameter plateau. All 100 exit configurations: performance on the first half of the data predicts
        the second half with rank correlation 0.92, and the whole neighbourhood of the deployed setting stays
          positive. The next page shows it.

  07   A mechanism, not a pattern. Overfit patterns do not care how you execute; a real liquidity mechanism
        does. The profit lives exactly where the mechanism says it must, on the passive side of the level, and
         dies when you chase.
