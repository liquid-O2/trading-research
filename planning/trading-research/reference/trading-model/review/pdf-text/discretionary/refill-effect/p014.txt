An independent engine agrees

Equity curves from your own research code deserve suspicion. So we embedded the
same signals into a strategy for Quant Charts' tick-level backtester, an engine we did not
write, with its own fill logic, and ran it over the full year at its default sizing.


  PERIOD                   TRADES        WIN RATE           PROFIT FACTOR         NET P&L

  Q1 Dec–Mar                 64           68.8%                     1.80         +$2,112

  Q2 Apr–Jun                 60           55.0%                     1.28         +$928

  Q3 Jul–Sep                 65           55.4%                     1.73         +$1,410

  Q4 Oct–Nov                 34           52.9%                     0.76         −$647

  Year                       223          58.7%                     1.45         +$3,803


Per-trade expectancy on this engine ≈ +0.07R, about half the research simulation's +0.14R, because its fills are stricter. We
quote both rather than the friendlier one.


   NOTE THE LOSING QUARTER

   Q4, the most recent slice, lost money at every parameter setting we tried. The edge is regime-dependent:
   weakest when volatility compressed late in the sample. We flag it and count it in every statistic, because
   an edge that only appears when losing periods are trimmed away is not an edge.
