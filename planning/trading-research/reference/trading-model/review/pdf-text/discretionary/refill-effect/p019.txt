07Inside a funded-account evaluation

 A per-trade edge of +0.14R is modest. What makes it valuable is its shape: many small
  bounded losses, steady drift, and a fat right tail from the 3-to-1 target. That shape is
  close to ideal for a prop evaluation, which is a race to +$3,000 before a $2,000 trailing
  drawdown, not a race for maximum profit.

 We replayed the held-out daily results 4,000 times through a Topstep-style 50K
  evaluation, with the consistency rule enforced and one self-imposed policy: stop trading
  any day that reaches −4R.


 The evaluation as a barrier problem





  Read the left panel as a fan of possible futures. Almost every path clears the target; only
  the unluckiest sequences of losing days brush the fail line. The right panel is the headline:
  at $80 of risk per R, the strategy clears a Topstep-style evaluation 92% of the time. The
  rest of this chapter takes that apart: how size trades against speed, why the daily stop
  matters so much, and what a pass is actually worth.
