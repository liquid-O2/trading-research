ETHOS ORDER FLOW / RESEARCH                                                     THE CONCEPT


What the aggressive orders look like

Before any modelling, this is the raw material, read live off the DOM the way it actually
happens. The rectangles below are zones built by clusters of large aggressive orders; the
circles are individual large prints. The red and green boxes are the read in real time: a
level gets tested, gives the aggressor no result, and either holds again or finally gives way.

A live read: the level gets tested twice before buyers take it





A real NQ session. The level (red) is built early and tested twice, once on the first touch and again on a sharp sell-off back
into it, and both times the sellers get nothing: price holds, not breaks. Right after the second test, buyers build a small base
(green) just above the level and take control. That is the read this whole paper tries to quantify: not the entry trigger, but
whether the defenders are still there when price comes back.
