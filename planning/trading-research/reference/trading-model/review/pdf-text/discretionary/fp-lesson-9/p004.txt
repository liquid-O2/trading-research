The four step plan


The four step plan, straight from the desk.

      Step 1, mark the key levels
        Support and resistance zones where traders are likely to get trapped: old highs and lows, ledges, value edges.


      Step 2, watch the approach
       As price nears the level, get the order flow up: delta against the candle is the read you are waiting for.


      Step 3, spot the disagreement
         Bullish absorption: the candle is bullish but delta is negative, sellers are aggressive yet price still rises.
        Bearish absorption: the candle is bearish but delta is positive, buyers are aggressive yet price still falls.


      Step 4, interpret the trap
       The aggressive side is stuck on the wrong side while institutions quietly build the opposite position. Trade
        with the institutions, against the trapped.





The disagreement printed: aggressive flow one way, price the other. Someone big is taking the other side.
