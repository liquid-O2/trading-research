  Everything up to this point is lower timeframe. On its own it is not enough, and he is direct about

  The daily volume profile read for this trade runs in order. Current auction was balancing. Price

  came out of balance. The area being used as support is a minor volume node sitting at the

  extreme of that balance. Minor volume nodes are worth marking because they tend to give

  clean rejections and they tend to start trends.


  Then the part that decides it. Below that node there is no volume. No volume means no

  participation, so there is nothing underneath to hold price if it goes there. Volume tries to build

  lower and fails. That is the asymmetry: above the node there is structure, below it there is

  nothing.


  On the five minute the same story has a bigger frame around it. A yearly composite sits as

  support, which is 365 days of volume, and there is a bulk of balance with VWAP's median

  through it, both of which say price is looking for value rather than trending away from it.
