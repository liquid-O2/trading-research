12 Why prefiring costs you



  the roadblocks, and what CVD was actually saying


  He goes back to the earlier entry and shows what was in front of it, which is more convincing

  than the principle on its own.


  At the premature long there was sell aggression, a sell imbalance, and repeated sellers stacked

  above. Price was pushing lower with aggression behind it. CVD was extremely bearish and

   sitting below its own median, which is the summary version of the same thing: across that

  whole dealing range down, sellers were in control.


  So the entry was not just early in time. It was pointing into everything that was working at that

  moment. He tells his students to avoid these specifically, and the reason he gives is a win rate

  one rather than a theory one. The entry can be good and the win rate can still be poor, and on a

  prop account variance is the thing that removes you, not being wrong about direction.
