04 Reading the aggression into the extreme



  an aggressive push signals intent, a slow drift signals none


  The first read: how does price actually arrive at the extreme. An aggressive move, buyers

  pushing in hard and fast, signals that side wants higher prices and is likely to defend the

  balance once it gets there. A slow, grinding move into the same extreme signals the opposite:

  no real interest in holding prices up there, which hands the opposition an opening to step in,

  take control, and push price back through the whole range.


  This is the first filter, before any level or retest gets involved. The same extreme, reached two

  different ways, is two different situations. Reached aggressively, it's a level to expect defended.

  Reached slowly, it's a level to expect broken.
