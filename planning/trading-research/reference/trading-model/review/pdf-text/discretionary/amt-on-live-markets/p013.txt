13 The checklist



  every rule in this document, printable


  READING BALANCE AND VALUE


        I know whether price is currently in balance or imbalanced.


      I'm treating fair value as a range (VAH to VAL), not a single price.



  AT THE EXTREME


      I'm asking "did the auction accept or reject here," not "should I buy or sell here."


     A deep dip into a level isn't automatically acceptance. I've confirmed it actually held and traded there.



  HIGH AND LOW VOLUME NODES


        I know whether the level in play is a high volume node (expect a stall) or a low volume node (expect a slice
       through).



  THE 80/20 READ


      I'm checking POC behavior rather than defaulting to the 80% case.


        I can point to repeated failure to hold above or below POC before assuming who's in control.
