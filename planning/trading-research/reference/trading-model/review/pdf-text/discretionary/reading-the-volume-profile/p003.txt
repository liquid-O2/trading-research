03 What the volume profile actually measures



 why the bell curve is shaped the way it is


  The volume profile is an indicator that measures volume within price, not volume over time the

  way a normal volume bar does. Plot it against a session where price played back and forth

  inside a range, and the shape that results is a bell curve, because the majority of the traded

  volume sits where price actually spent the most time.


  That one relationship, more time at a price meaning more volume at that price, is what every

  other concept in this document is built from. It's used to determine areas of fair value,

  acceptance, rejection, support and resistance, high volume nodes, low volume nodes, and POC.
