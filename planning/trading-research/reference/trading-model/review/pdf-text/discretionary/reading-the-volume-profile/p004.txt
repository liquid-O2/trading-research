04 The three components: VAH, POC, VAL



  value area high and low, and the point of control between them


  Value area high is the highest price within the value area, the band where roughly 68 percent of

  the session's volume traded. Value area low is the same idea at the bottom of that band, the

  lowest price still inside that 68 percent. Between the two sits POC, the single price where the

  most volume of anywhere in the session traded.


  These three levels are the raw material for everything that follows. Marked correctly, they tell

  you where the market has already agreed to do business, which is a very different, much more

  concrete question than where you think price might go next.
