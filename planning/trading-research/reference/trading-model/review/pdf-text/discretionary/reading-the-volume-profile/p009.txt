09 The trending profile



 when to do nothing, and the mistake that blows accounts


  A trending profile is what happens when one side maintains control for the entire session,

  continuously driving price in one direction while the other side never gains enough footing to

  establish a balanced area of fair value. The result is a directional auction with little acceptance

  anywhere along the way, and on the profile itself, a shape with no real bell curve, just a long,

  narrow spread of volume.


  His instruction for this shape is the whole point of including it: when you see one, get off.

  There's nothing to trade. He names the specific mistake beginners make against it directly:

  seeing a strong trend and repeatedly trying to long a small reaction inside it, again and again,

  taken out each time, until the account is gone. A trending profile isn't a setup, it's a signal to

  stop looking for one.
