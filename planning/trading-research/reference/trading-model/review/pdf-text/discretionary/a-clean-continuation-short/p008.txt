08 The refill zone, repeated



 why the first entry stopped out, and why the second one held


  The first attempt at this short got stopped out. The read going in was that buyers who had

  previously controlled the push higher would offer a passive refill zone for sellers, a place to

  drive price back down from. Buyers came back in instead, and the trade got stopped, which he's

  plain about: that's fine, it happens.


  What made the re-entry worth taking was that the same level kept behaving the same way on

  repeat. Sellers had control, price went lower. Price came back up to the level, sellers had

  control again. It went lower again. Sellers had had control there before, more than once, which

   is exactly what a refill zone is: a level where the same side keeps winning the argument. Buyers

  had controlled the push up, but the level itself kept refilling back to sellers, which is what the

  re-entry was built on.
