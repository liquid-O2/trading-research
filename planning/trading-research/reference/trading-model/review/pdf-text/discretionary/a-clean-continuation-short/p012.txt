12 Why these were all the same trade



  filtering continuation entries against one higher timeframe bias


   All three setups in this document are continuation trades, and that's not a coincidence, it's the

   filter. The higher timeframe thesis was short from the open, so every entry taken had to agree

  with that bias before it was considered at all. A clean squeeze in the opposite direction would

  have been ignored, not because the read was wrong on its own terms, but because it disagreed

  with the higher timeframe context already established.


  That's the actual throughline of this whole session: one higher timeframe thesis, three separate

  lower timeframe confirmations that all pointed the same direction, at different points in the day.

  None of the three needed a new idea. Each one needed the same idea to show up again.
