11 The third setup: a squeeze with no failure



  the cleanest version of the same idea, no retry needed


  A third setup from the session is worth separating from the second: this one is actually a

  squeeze, not an origin of the move, because it never failed the way the second trade's first

  attempt did. Sellers showed willingness to price lower and the move happened fast, with real

  aggression, no retest, no false start.


  Once buyers hit that move and got absorbed, the entry is available with a tight stop right there,

  opposition visibly failing on both sides of the read at once. This is the same underlying model

  taught in the mentorship for catching continuation entries, whether that's framed as a squeeze,

  an origin of the move, or an earlier entry inside the same idea.
