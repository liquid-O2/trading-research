04 The 5 minute thesis



  a minor volume node, and negative Delta stacking against the level


  On the 5 minute, price had displaced lower and value had shifted down with it. Once price found

  balance, a minor volume node stood out as the level to use, a place used previously, multiple

  times, as resistance. In terms of Delta, negative Delta was stacking and stacking into that level,

  and cross-checked against the DOM and heat map, a clear pattern of sellers pinning the level.


  That combination, a real minor volume node plus visibly stacking negative Delta into it, was the

  read: be lenient on sellers here, expect buyers to be absorbed and exhausted, and treat this as

  a good area for price to come back into balance from. He also names this level a failed auction

  at the top, the wicks that never actually closed and held above it.
