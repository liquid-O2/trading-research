05 The AMT structure



  an established balance, POC as fair value, and failed auctions at the top


  With an established balance in place and POC treated as the area of fair value, the framework is

  simple to state: if price comes back to the top of that balance, the default expectation is shorts,

  unless price closes above it with real, extreme buying pressure. If that pressure shows up, the

  read flips to a retest and longs back toward the session's VWAP instead.


  The areas marked here are failed auctions, in his words, more than once: price wicking above

  the level without ever truly closing and holding there. One of those wicks, worked on a lower

  timeframe like the 40 range or 1 minute, would have been good for roughly three to four R on its

  own, before price came back down.
