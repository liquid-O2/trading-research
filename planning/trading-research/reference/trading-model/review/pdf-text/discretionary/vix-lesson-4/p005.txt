Risk by regime





The regime also decides how you manage risk. Same setups, different sizing rules.





   VIX above 20             Widen stops to 3 or 4 points or scale out quicker. The noise band is bigger, a tight
                               stop is just a donation.





   VIX 15 to 18              The best pocket for pressing structured risk-reward, the 2 point stop, 10 point
                                   target days live here.





   VIX below 14                Shrink targets to scalps, run smaller stops under 3 points, or stay out entirely.





   VIX below 13              Expected ES range under 30 points. Usually not worth fishing. Knowing there is no
                            edge today IS the edge.





   RANGE COMPLETION

   Track realized range against the implied range through the day. Once the implied range is mostly done, expect
   mean reversion and chop, the fuel is spent.
