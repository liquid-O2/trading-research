The daily range read





The most practical use: VIX lets you estimate how many points ES is likely to travel today. That sets realistic targets,
sensible stops, and sometimes tells you not to trade at all.





   VIX around 12.5          ES roughly a 30 point day. Scalps only, 3 to 5 points. Fishing for 10 point runners
                           on a day like this is how the account bleeds.





   VIX around 16            ES roughly a 50 point day. The sweet spot for structured R setups, opening drive
                                 plays with 10 point targets make sense here.





   VIX around 22            ES roughly a 95 point day. Violent swings. Do not force tiny stops, ride momentum,
                                take partials earlier and trail aggressively.
