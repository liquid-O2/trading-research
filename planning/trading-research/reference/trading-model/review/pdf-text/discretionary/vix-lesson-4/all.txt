The fear gauge




The VIX is the market's fear gauge. It measures implied volatility on S&P 500 options, essentially what traders
expect the market to move over the next 30 days.





   HIGH VIX                                    LOW VIX

   More fear, bigger expected ranges. Explosive, trend           Calm tape, tight ranges, grindy price action. Chop and
    friendly moves. The days you hold runners and stop          mean reversion, less opportunity for big R setups.
    fading strength.





The VIX. One number that frames what kind of session you are about to trade.





   THE RULE BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

   VIX is not a trade signal, it is a context tool. It tells you what kind of day to expect so you run the right layer of
   your edge, and it never tells you to click.

The daily range read





The most practical use: VIX lets you estimate how many points ES is likely to travel today. That sets realistic targets,
sensible stops, and sometimes tells you not to trade at all.





   VIX around 12.5          ES roughly a 30 point day. Scalps only, 3 to 5 points. Fishing for 10 point runners
                           on a day like this is how the account bleeds.





   VIX around 16            ES roughly a 50 point day. The sweet spot for structured R setups, opening drive
                                 plays with 10 point targets make sense here.





   VIX around 22            ES roughly a 95 point day. Violent swings. Do not force tiny stops, ride momentum,
                                take partials earlier and trail aggressively.

Risk by regime





The regime also decides how you manage risk. Same setups, different sizing rules.





   VIX above 20             Widen stops to 3 or 4 points or scale out quicker. The noise band is bigger, a tight
                               stop is just a donation.





   VIX 15 to 18              The best pocket for pressing structured risk-reward, the 2 point stop, 10 point
                                   target days live here.





   VIX below 14                Shrink targets to scalps, run smaller stops under 3 points, or stay out entirely.





   VIX below 13              Expected ES range under 30 points. Usually not worth fishing. Knowing there is no
                            edge today IS the edge.





   RANGE COMPLETION

   Track realized range against the implied range through the day. Once the implied range is mostly done, expect
   mean reversion and chop, the fuel is spent.

The intraday reads





VIX is not just an overnight forecast. The intraday direction of the number matters as much as its level.





   ES up, VIX down            Healthy trend, confirmation. Continuation is the play.





   ES up, VIX up               Divergence. Caution, the rally may fade, hedging demand is building underneath it.





   ES down, VIX up           Normal risk-off. Momentum likely continues, do not rush to catch the knife.





   ES flat, VIX flat            Expect chop. Scalp the rotations or stand down.





   THE SIMPLE VERSION

    Rising VIX intraday, expect ES to push away from value, expansion. Falling VIX intraday, expect ES to rotate and
   respect levels, balance.

Event days and the vol crush





Around scheduled events the VIX has a rhythm of its own, and knowing it keeps you out of the two classic traps.





   BEFORE THE EVENT

    Into CPI, FOMC and major earnings the VIX tends to rise, the market is nervous. High pre-event VIX means do not over-
    fade moves, expect volatility, and ignore the implied range, catalyst days can blow straight past it.





   AFTER THE EVENT

   Once the event passes, VIX often drops hard, the vol crush. The ES range shrinks and the tape goes back to rotation.
    Post-crush is when you return to fading rotations.





   THE TWO TRAPS

   Fading a pre-event tape like it is a normal chop day, and chasing breakouts after the crush like it is still an event
    day. Same mistake, opposite directions: trading yesterday's regime.

The VIX curve and VVIX





One layer deeper: the VIX is not a single number, it is a curve, and the shape of that curve tells you how much fear is
priced right now versus later.





   CONTANGO, THE NORMAL STATE                      BACKWARDATION, THE STRESS STATE

   Longer dated volatility trades above spot VIX. The              Spot VIX spikes above the longer dates. Fear is
   market is calm, fear is a future maybe, not a now. This           immediate. This is crash and panic behaviour, huge
     is the backdrop for grindy trend days and clean                  ranges, gaps, and levels that mean nothing until it
    rotations.                                                        resolves.





   VVIX, THE VIX OF THE VIX   Measures how fast volatility itself is expected to move. A rising VVIX warns that a
                                  quiet VIX is about to get loud, an early tell before the range even expands.





   THE PRACTICAL READ       Contango, trade normally with your levels. Backwardation, cut size hard, widen
                                  everything, and respect that the auction is broken until the curve normalises.





  WHY BOTHER WITH THE CURVE

   A VIX of 18 in calm contango is a totally different day to a VIX of 18 ripping into backwardation. The level alone
    lies, the shape tells the truth.

VIX plus the auction





This is where the lesson connects to everything before it. AMT logic tells you where value is. VIX tells you whether
the auction will rotate inside that value or explore outside it.




      Balance on the profile, VIX dropping
         Play the rotations. Fade the edges of value back toward the POC, price is more likely to rotate inside the
         value area and respect VAH and VAL.





      Balance on the profile, VIX rising
        Prepare for the range break. Expansion is loading, do not lean on the walls the way you would on a falling-
        VIX day.





       Size the breakout by the VIX
      When VIX is dropping hard, size smaller on breakout attempts, they fail into rotation more often. Real
        breaks need real volatility behind them.





   THE ONE LINE

   The profile is the map, the VIX is the weather. You need both before you drive.
