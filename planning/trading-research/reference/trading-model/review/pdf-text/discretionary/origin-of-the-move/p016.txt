08The playbook

  People hear "you need alpha" in this space constantly, and it stops them before they
  start. Here is the correction: inside a prop structure you do not need alpha. Real
  standalone alpha takes years and a maths PhD to manufacture. A prop evaluation is a
  barrier problem: reach the target before the trailing drawdown reaches you. What that
  rewards is a positive (even neutral) expectancy edge with the right risk geometry. That,
  this feature set has. So the playbook below is not "how to beat the market." It is how to
  convert a thin, real edge into passed evaluations and payouts.
