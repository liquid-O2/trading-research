15 The checklist



  every rule in this document, printable


  READING THE AUCTION


        I can name a misconception I used to trade on (a fixed win rate, static imbalance, large size as automatic
       absorption), and I've stopped trading it that way.


        I know whether size at my level is replenishing or vanishing before I treat it as support or resistance.



  ABSORPTION AND AGGRESSION


        I can point to all three conditions for absorption (high aggression, low response efficiency, the opposite
       side holding and refilling), not just one of them.


      I'm not fading a genuine discovery move, and I'm not chasing a failed aggressive push with no evidence of

      replenishment behind it.



  READING EXTREMES


     My low volume nodes, ledges and shelves all have a second transition behind them, not just a taper I'm

        calling an extreme.


        I know whether price is in balance or in discovery before I decide which side of a level to trade.



  PAIRING TIMEFRAMES


        I've identified the higher timeframe auction and the current cycle before I ever look at the dom or footprint

        for an entry trigger.
