   Participation is three verbs



       provide, withdraw, consume, and why your screen has to agree with the
       record


        Participation = provide + withdraw + consume. Everything you can observe about an

        auction reduces to those three actions repeating at every price level, over and over, all

        session.


        jetbundle draws a line between what discretion sees and what the math uses. What

        discretion sees: depth of market, tape, footprint. What the math uses: submits, cancels,

        executions. Same auction, described in two different languages, and financially sound

        discretion only starts once those two languages agree. If the tape suggests buying but

       the book shows massive cancels behind it, your read of the tape is a hallucination, not a

        trade.





         Same auction, two languages. Screen language is what you see and feel making a

             discretionary call. Record language is submits, cancels and executions, the raw

           mechanics underneath it. When they disagree, trust the record.
