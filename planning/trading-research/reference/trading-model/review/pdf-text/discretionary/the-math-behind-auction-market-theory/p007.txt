   Aggression and response efficiency



        effort without move, and the discipline of not fading discovery


       Aggressive buying does not mean the next tick is up. Aggression on its own is neither

         bullish nor bearish, it only means something once you measure it against the efficiency

        of the price displacement it actually produces. The same aggressive force, applied in

       two different participation environments, produces two financially distinct outcomes.


      When price displaces efficiently in response to aggression, that's discovery, and the rule

          is simple: don't fade discovery. When the same aggression goes nowhere, that's a

       candidate for absorption, and the rule there is just as simple: don't chase a failed

        aggressive push without evidence that the other side is actually replenishing.
