  THE BLUEPRINT26
 Pacing, then digits

  Two: Pacing. Effort against reward. A huge divergence of buyers over sellers while price
  stagnates, rotating in the same few ticks, is massive effort with no reward, and effort that
   isn't being paid is a warning, whatever the imbalance says.

  Three: Digits. At the open you can't compute bid-ask maths fast enough, so read digit
  size. Sellers refreshing from two and three-digit sizes down to singles are thinning:
  weakness. Buyers stepping from two and three digits to four and five are building effort.
 When the pacing then upticks, effort finally getting its reward, that's the entry.

  Location, then pacing, then digits, always in that order, always after the thesis. The
  reward systems underneath are in the DOM Advanced video. One warning: only your own
 DOM recordings of your own entries are worth studying. Other people's tape teaches
  other people's trades.
