 What collection produces

  Write it plainly. "Absorption." "Targeting value area high." Why you entered, what
  happened, how many sellers lost, the volume of the candle. Screenshots in. Money result
  last; by then it barely matters, because the process is what you're grading. What does
  collection buy? Hard numbers like this one from my DOM work: absorption fails 27% of
  the time unless there's a 3-tick aggression toward the absorbed side. A tradeable filter
  that only exists because the trades behind it were counted.

  Two closing points. Tools don't matter: Notion, a journaling site, Discord; collect properly
  and you can point a folder of screenshots at Claude and have it tell you what you're doing
  right and wrong, even test your feature sets. And I mean this: "You need to stop
  watching content... This is not school. You need to go on the actual charts live and
  record the videos yourself." 33:09 Your own recordings will teach you more than any
  video, including mine.
