    THE BASELINE POPULATION

    The French regulator tracked 14,799 active retail CFD and forex clients over four years: about 84% lost in
     any single year, rising to 89.4% cumulated over the full four, with an average result of minus 10,887 euros
     per client. More screen time without a process change made the outcome worse. The same shape as the
     eval spiral. AMF,retailCFDandforexstudyinFrance,13Oct2014
