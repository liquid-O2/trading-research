 The discretionary box





  The discretionary box, drawn while the red list still sits beside it: the market's chaos is tradeable only through the features
  you've defined inside the boundary. Everything outside gets an X, and the X means you stand down.



    NAMED IN 1979

    Kahneman and Tversky wrote that a person who has not made peace with his losses is likely to accept
     gambles that would be unacceptable to him otherwise. That is the break-even trade, named 47 years ago:
      after a loss the reference point stays anchored, so you accept risk you'd never take flat. KahnemanandTversky,

      "ProspectTheory",Econometrica,1979
