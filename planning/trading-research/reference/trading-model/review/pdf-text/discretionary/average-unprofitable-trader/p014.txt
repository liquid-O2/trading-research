    THE COST OF ACTIVITY

    Among 66,465 US brokerage households from 1991 to 1996, the most active traders earned 11.4% a
     year while the market returned 17.9%. Activity itself was the cost. Hunting a fourth confluence, like over-
      trading, spends real money on something never shown to pay. BarberandOdean,"TradingIsHazardoustoYour
