    THE UPDATING FAILURE

     Using every trade on the Taiwan Stock Exchange from 1992 to 2006, Barber, Lee, Liu and Odean found the
     vast majority of day traders unprofitable, many persisting despite extensive losses, and over 75% quitting
     within two years. Their core finding: losing traders fail to update on their own results. A journal exists to fix
     exactly that. Barber,Lee,LiuandOdean,"DoDayTradersRationallyLearnAboutTheirAbility?",2017
