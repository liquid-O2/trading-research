The pre-market routine



Discipline is not willpower, it is a routine you do not have to think about. Here is the pre-market ritual that makes the
boring things automatic.


     Map the auction before the bell
         Yesterday's value, the naked POCs, the VIX read, the day type call. Ten minutes. Walk in with a plan, not a
        hope.




       Write your A+ setup down
       One sentence. If the trade in front of you does not match it, it is not a trade. The written rule is what beats
        the impulse.




       Set a hard loss limit for the day
      A number of R, or a dollar figure. Hit it and you are done, no negotiation. This one rule saves accounts that
         everything else cannot.




       Trade the plan, journal the deviation
        Every time you break a rule, log it. The pattern in your breaks is the exact thing costing you money.




       Close the laptop after the session
        Detach. The market runs tomorrow. Grinding a screen after your limit is how discipline erodes into
         revenge.





   THE POINT OF THE RITUAL

   You cannot feel your way to consistency, you can only route around your feelings with a process. The routine is
   the process. Run it on the good days and the bad days identically, and the emotion stops driving.
