Process and routine





Performance is decided before the open, not during the session.





       Preparation defines performance
        Premarket analysis, journaling and a clear routine are non-negotiable. The trader with a plan is trading, the
       one without is reacting.





       Journal and tag every trade
         Timing, clarity, emotion, entry and exit quality. An unreviewed trade is wasted data, and data is the only
        honest coach you will ever have.





      Sim and review when struggling
        Switch to simulation, drill the setups, review the recordings and find the exact error. Struggling live with
          real money teaches nothing except fear.
