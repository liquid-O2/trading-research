Layering, flipping and the filter





Two more ladder games sit between the honest iceberg and the outright spoof. Naming them keeps you from being
played.





   LAYERING                                              FLIPPING

   A spoofer posts a ladder of fake orders at several                Size that jumps from the bid to the ask and back, faster
    prices, not just one wall, to paint a wall of pressure. It           than any real order manager would work. It is designed
    looks like deep conviction, it is theatre. Tell: the whole            to fake a change of control. Tell: no prints trade, only
    stack pulls together the moment price approaches.             the display flickers.





The single reliable filter under all of it is the refresh rate against the prints. Real hidden size (an iceberg) refreshes as
volume trades through it, you see the prints. Fake size (spoof, layer, flip) refreshes or vanishes with no prints at all.
Prints are truth, the display is a story.





   THE ONE HABIT

   Before you trust any wall, ask one question: is volume actually trading into it? If the size keeps changing but the
   tape is silent, it is a lie. Trade the prints, never the picture.
