14 Before the next session



  What this session actually checks for


  BEFORE THE OPEN


      The higher timeframe dealing range is marked, with the minor volume nodes that have already produced a
       reaction.


      The weekly delta print is checked for who got trapped and who is covering.


      The gamma regime is named, even when it sits near the flip and the answer is genuinely close.



  ON THE ENTRY


     A pre-file entry is sized small on purpose, as buffer, never as a conviction trade.


     A level is only traded once it has been tested and defended a second time, with refreshing size behind it.


     A low starting R:R is not a reason to skip the trade if the level behind it is strong.



  MANAGING THE TRADE


      The stop moves to the next protected high only after price closes below the prior one with real aggression.


      Covering by the losing side is read as confirmation, not confused with dealer hedging flow.


     A later trade is only sized up once an earlier one is no longer at risk.
