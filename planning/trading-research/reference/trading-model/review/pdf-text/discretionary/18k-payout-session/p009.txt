09 Trade 3: the close



  R:R 0.69 becomes a session





  Later in the same trail: stop now at +$1,240, resting target raised to $2,645. The order ticket, not the
  account total, which is the recap on page 13.


  By the time this trade closed, the running total for the session read "up 1.6, 1.6k. I took two

  losses and then we have this one." A trade that started at less than one to one on paper had, by

   itself, turned two losing trades into a green day. That reversal is the entire argument for trailing

  convexity over a fixed target: the reward a setup starts with is a description of the entry, not a

  ceiling on the trade.


  None of the mechanics on this trade were exotic. Every stop move was a reaction to a level the

  market had just defended a second time, read the same way trade one and trade two were read,

  except this time the market agreed. The difference between a losing trade and this one was

  never the model. It was whether the level held.
