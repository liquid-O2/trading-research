03 A full session, no cuts



  what this video actually is


  A New York AM open, traded live across a Tradecopia replication to six funded accounts plus a

  separate Alpha account, seven in total. Same price, same size, same time, on every one of them.

  Nothing here is a highlight reel: two of the four trades shown lost, and they are in this document

   for the same reason the two winners are.


  The read underneath every trade is order flow only, no indicators beyond CVD and speed of

  tape. Location tells you where price sits in the higher timeframe auction. Absorption tells you

  who is defending it. Refresh and pacing tell you whether that defense is thickening or thinning.

  None of it is prediction. It is a read of what is actually happening at the price, and a reaction to

    it.


  This recording opens before the trading, on the payout request for these exact seven accounts.

  Apex caps a single request at $2,000 per account, so the six funded accounts clear at $2,000

  each, $12,000 combined. The Alpha account clears separately at $6,000. $12,000 plus $6,000

   is the $18,000 on the cover, requested against the same seven accounts that go on to trade this

  session, not a different one shown for effect.


  The $2,300 this session made is not part of that $18,000. The payout was earned over the

  trading days before this one; the session below is what these accounts did on the day the

  payout also happened to be requested.

    Four trades from this session are broken down below: two losses taken
    on purpose, and two wins that only happened because the losses were
     small enough to not matter.
