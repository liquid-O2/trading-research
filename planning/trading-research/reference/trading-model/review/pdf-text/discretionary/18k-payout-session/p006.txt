06 Trade 2: still too early



  The same mistake, once more, on purpose





  A second short order live on the book, -$40 risked, stopped moments later. Buyers absorbed the sell
  aggression that was supposed to confirm the level.


  The second trade is the one that gets cut from most recap videos, because it repeats the first

  one's mistake rather than fixing it. Sellers pushed in again, looked aggressive on the tape, and

  got stopped out by buyers who simply would not give the level up. "I might have just got

  checked out." Small size again, -$40, a scratch rather than a real loss, but a loss all the same.


  Two trades in and the session is down two, both on the short side, both before the market had

  actually shown its hand. That is not hesitation and it is not stubbornness. It is what testing a

  level properly looks like when the level does not cooperate: you pay the small, known price

  twice, and you do not change the thesis because of it. The thesis was never about these two

  trades. It was about what buyers and sellers did next.
