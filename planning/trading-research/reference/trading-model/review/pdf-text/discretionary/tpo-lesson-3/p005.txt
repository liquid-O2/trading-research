Single prints


A single print is a single row of letters from one time period, with no overlap from any other period. Price moved
through so fast that only one 30 minute window ever touched it.





Two single print examples on the profile: thin strips where only one letter ever printed.

A single print is a sharp one sided move that leaves imbalance
behind it. The market tends to come back and fill it, like a magnet.
