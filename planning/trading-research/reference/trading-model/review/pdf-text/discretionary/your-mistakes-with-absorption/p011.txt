11 The Delta spike at both extremes



  value area high and value area low, same tell, opposite direction


  The same mechanic runs in both directions on the same chart. At value area high, a high Delta

  spike of buyers marks the opposition losing control, sellers absorbing that spike passively, and

  price rejecting lower. At value area low, the mirror image: a high Delta spike of buyers this time

  regaining control, sellers being absorbed at the bottom, price rejecting back up into balance.


  What makes this the fastest of the four checks is that it doesn't require waiting for price to fully

  confirm. The Delta spike is visible at the moment control changes hands, which is earlier in the

  sequence than the price reaction that eventually follows it.



  THE SAME DELTA SPIKE MECHANIC AT VAH AND AT VAL





  Both labeled the same way in his own notes: a high Delta spike of buyers, read as the opposition losing
   control, being absorbed passively by sellers. One rejects a high, the other rejects a low.
