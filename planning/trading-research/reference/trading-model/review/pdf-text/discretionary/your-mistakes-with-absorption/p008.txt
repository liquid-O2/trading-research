08 Mistake 3: not understanding the
 definition



  absorption as a sponge, and the effort versus reward test underneath it


  Before the chart, the plain definition: absorption is the process of one thing taking in, soaking

  up, or receiving another substance. His own image for it is a sponge, and it's worth taking

   literally rather than as a throwaway metaphor, because the mechanics map directly onto the

  chart.


  Price pushes up aggressively, a ton of effort from buyers, pumping. Then it instantly hits a wall.

  That wall is passive sellers, arriving not to chase price but to sit there and absorb it, soaking up

  the aggression the way a sponge soaks up liquid. You'll often see a bubble of aggressive buying

  print right at that wall, and then price goes lower anyway.


  The test that separates a real read from a guess: are the buyers being rewarded for that effort.

   If price goes up on effort and gets nothing for it, that's the tell. Then a second signal has to

  show up, seller aggression, actual reward on the other side, before the reversal is anything more

  than a hope.
