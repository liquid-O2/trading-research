05Why you re-enter

  The re-entry feels like a fresh read. It almost never is. Two findings from behavioral
  research describe the machine underneath it, and once you see them, you'll recognize
  both in your own trade log.

  So the honest description of a re-entry spiral is this: the first trade was a read, the second
  was a hope, and everything after that was the break-even effect picking trades for you.
  The fix is not willpower. The fix is a checklist the urge has to pass through, and that
  checklist is the rest of this document.

  One more thing worth saying plainly: none of this makes you defective. These biases
  showed up in professional floor traders too, and the professionals who survived weren't
  the ones without the urge, they were the ones with rules around it. That study is on the
  risk page near the end.
