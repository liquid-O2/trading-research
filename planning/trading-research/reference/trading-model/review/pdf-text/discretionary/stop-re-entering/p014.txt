14The checklist, printable

  Everything in this document, in nine boxes. Screenshot this page. Before an entry, and
  before every re-entry, it should be able to tick every box, from zero, as if the last trade
  never happened.



     THE THREE-STEP READ

           Location. I know where price sits in the higher-timeframe auction, and who should be in control
           here.

        Reward vs result. My side's aggression is getting rewarded, upticks or downticks that stick, not
            just producing effort against a wall.

           Delta. Delta agrees with my side; the opposite side is thickening while the losing side thins out.

      AT THE ABSORPTION

         Replenishment. The level has refreshed at least three ticks. One or two is the fake-out zone.

          Exhaustion. The aggressor's prints are shrinking, doubles or triples down to a single, not
          growing.

             Lift-off. I am entering on the aggression that follows exhaustion, not on the passive defense
              itself.

          Timing. My entry sits within a tick or two of that confirmation, not six to eight ticks late.

      RISK, EVERY TIME

           Daily stop. I am above minus four R for the day. If I am not, there is no trade, re-entry or
           otherwise.

          Re-entry rule. If this is a re-entry, it passes every box above from zero, not on the strength of
        how close it already got.



  Nine boxes. If any one is unticked, the answer is no trade yet, not a smaller trade. Waiting is free.
