12The correct entry, dissected

  Clip one: the entry that waited for stage four





  The long is on, taken only after the sellers' prints collapsed to single digits and buyers answered in triples. The stop sits
  under the absorption, and the reward system is already paying.

  What made this one correct, step by step. Sellers came in fast, and buyers barely printed
  at first, so there was no reason to catch the knife. Then the read flipped in sequence:
  sellers' double-digit prints started thinning to singles, which is weakness you can see,
  while buyers answered with triple digits, which is conviction you can count. Buying
  pressure held on the CVD. Then the aggression came: two upticks, which is my minimum,
  two to four, for calling it a reward system. Enter on the aggression, not on the
  passiveness. Passive absorption is the setup; aggression is the trigger.

  Notice what the entry did not require: prediction, courage, or a feeling about the low.
  Every input was observable, which is exactly why it's repeatable and why there was
  nothing to re-enter. The first entry was the confirmed one.
