08Where the re-entries actually happen

 The 40-range walkthrough from the video





  NQ, 40-range bars, daily volume profile with delta and speed of tape below and CVD at the bottom. Pink bubbles are sell
   aggression, white are buy. The boxed areas mark where aggression clustered at the highs; the circles mark the prints that
  mattered.

  Walk the sequence the way most people trade it. Buyers show up with result on the way
  up, so they enter with a small stop and get stopped. Sell aggression prints, so they flip
  short right into the pullback and get stopped again. More buy aggression at the wick, they
  re-enter, and the wick keeps absorbing. Three entries, three stops, and not one of them
  asked whether anyone was being rewarded. Buyers had result on the way in, then bought
  heavily at the highs. Were they rewarded? No. That's absorption, and it's also your
  answer.

  The entry this framework takes is the one where sellers regain control, buyers get
  absorbed, and the CVD confirms by rolling over its median. One entry, taken once,
  because the reward finally agreed with the read.
