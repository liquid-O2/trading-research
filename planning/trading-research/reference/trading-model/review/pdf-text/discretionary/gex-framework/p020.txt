Common mistakes


The traders who lose money with GEX almost always make one of these. Avoid them and you are ahead of most of
the crowd moving into options flow.

       Trading GEX levels blindly
         This is the big one. So many orderflow traders move into options flow and start trading the pinning and
          call/put levels like they are gospel. They are not, they are heavily assumption based. Use them as context,
        then confirm with the DOM and the footprint.


       Ignoring the regime
       Run your trend model on a long gamma chop day, or scalp fades on a short gamma trend day, and even a
        good setup bleeds you. The regime decides which layer of your edge is even allowed to trade.


       Treating pinning as a guarantee
       A pin is a tendency, not a promise. Time of day matters, the open is not the midday is not the close, and one
        event can break the whole thing. Never size like a pin is certain.


      Not updating after an impulse
      Gamma is not static intraday. After a big expansion the read can flip from short to long gamma, so re-read it
         before the next trade or you are trading the old environment.


       Using long-dated options
       You are an intraday trader, so read 0DTE. Contracts expiring in weeks or months are not what is controlling
         today's session, and reading them just gives you the wrong picture.



    Every one of these is the same error: treating GEX as a signal instead of context. Keep it as context, confirm with the
    tape, and you sidestep all five.
