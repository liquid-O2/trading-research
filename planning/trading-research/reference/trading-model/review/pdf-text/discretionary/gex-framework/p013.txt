The key levels


Within a regime, a few strikes do most of the work. Treat them as context, never as guarantees.





The GEX levels on price: call walls above (resistance), put walls below (support), the gamma flip in the middle, and max
pain at the bottom.

CALL WALL        Largest call gamma above. Resistance as dealers sell into it.


PUT WALL         Largest put gamma below. Support as dealers buy into it.


MAX PAIN          Strike where option value is lowest. Price often drifts toward it into expiry.



    In long gamma the walls are hard boundaries, so fading toward them works. In short gamma the same walls break
    easily, so trade the breakout through them.
