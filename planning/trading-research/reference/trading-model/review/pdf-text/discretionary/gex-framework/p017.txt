Vanna and charm


Gamma is the headline, but two slower forces explain the drift you see with no news. You do not need to calculate
them, just know they exist and which way they push.



   VANNA

    Delta's sensitivity to implied volatility. When IV falls, dealers must buy to re-hedge. That is the engine behind the steady
   grind higher after a VIX drop, the vol-compression rally.




   CHARM

    Delta's decay as time passes. Into expiry it quietly pulls price toward the biggest open-interest strikes. It is a big part of
   why quiet days melt up toward max pain and the call wall.


Put it together: a calm tape with IV drifting down into a large expiration gives dealers two reasons to keep buying.
That is why low-volatility regimes grind higher and drift toward the high open-interest strikes.
