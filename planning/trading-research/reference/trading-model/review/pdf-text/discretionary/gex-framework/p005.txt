Dealer hedging, in plain English


Market makers are the dealers. They move the market with billions, and they do not want directional risk. They want
to stay delta-neutral. So they take the opposite side of almost every options trade and hedge it instantly.


  A trader buys a call                                                                      dealer sells a call

  A trader buys a put                                                                       dealer sells a put

  A trader sells a call                                                                      dealer buys a call

  A trader sells a put                                                                       dealer buys a put


So dealers inherit the opposite risk of the trader, but they do not want it. They hedge it away instantly because they
have so much capital to protect. This hedging is mechanical, automatic and constant, run by algorithms, not human
discretion.


    Dealer hedging is a force, and it is not optional. It leaves a footprint in the futures every single session. The rest of GEX
     is just measuring how big that force is and which way it pushes.
