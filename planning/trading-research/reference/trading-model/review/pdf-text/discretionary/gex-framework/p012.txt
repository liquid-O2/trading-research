Pinning and gamma squeezes


Two behaviors fall straight out of the regime, and naming them is half the battle. One quietly kills trends, the other
creates the violent ones.

   Many sessions are simply range days because hedging pressure punishes extension. When the dominant flow is selling

    Pinning looks like rotations that keep failing at a strike. A squeeze looks like that same strike breaking on aggression
   and then accelerating away. Same level, opposite behavior, and the regime is what tells you which one to expect.
