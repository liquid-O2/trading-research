Where gamma lives: strikes


Gamma is not spread evenly. It clusters at strikes, and the closer a strike is to price, the more sensitive it is. That is
where hedging pressure concentrates.



   ATM                             OTM                             ITM

    Strike near price. Highest                Cheaper. Can create large              Behaves more like the stock.
   gamma, most reactive to            gamma shifts if price                  Lower gamma, more delta-heavy.
   movement.                            approaches.





Gamma stacked by strike. The biggest concentrations are where hedging pressure clusters, and where price tends to react.


  Gamma concentrates near the money, and hedging intensity rises as time-to-expiry compresses. That is why 0DTE
   near the big strikes is where the strongest pinning and squeezes happen.
