The gamma flip


One price matters more than any other: the level where net dealer gamma crosses from positive to negative. It is
called the zero gamma level, or the gamma flip, and it is the most useful line on the whole GEX map.





Net GEX by strike. Red bars are negative gamma (amplifying), green bars are positive (stabilising). The gamma flip is
where they cross; the call wall and max pain are marked too.

Above the flip, dealers are net long gamma, so the regime is stabilising: expect range and mean reversion. Below the
flip, dealers are net short gamma, so it is amplifying: expect trends and expansion. Crossing the flip intraday is a
regime change, and one of the highest-value events you can watch for.
