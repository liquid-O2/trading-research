Long gamma vs short gamma


GEX measures the dollar value of futures dealers must buy or sell to stay delta-neutral for a given move in price. The
sign of that number is everything.


  LONG GAMMA                             SHORT GAMMA
    Positive GEX, stabilising                                        Negative GEX, amplifying

        Volatility gets suppressed                                                Volatility expands

       Price pins near big strikes                                     Breakouts run, levels break

     Ranges and mean reversion                                   Trends and momentum
     RANGE      TREND

   When dealers are short gamma, a move forces them to trade in the same direction as price: they buy higher and sell
    lower. That feedback loop, lower prices triggering more dealer selling, is why the sharpest trend days happen in
    negative gamma.
