Regimes, the real mistake


A market regime is a persistent structural environment in which price behaves. It is not just volatility being high or
low. It is the statistical character of orderflow, liquidity and participation over a stretch of time. You have probably
trained your eye to one regime without realising it.

Most traders build a strategy that works in ONE regime,
then trade it in ALL of them.

When the regime shifts, they say my edge stopped working. It did not. The environment changed. The fix is not a new
strategy, it is knowing which regime you are in and only running the layer of your edge built for it. That is why a
serious strategy has layers: setups for trend days, and different setups for chop.
