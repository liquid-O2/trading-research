## Supplied material reviewed but intrinsically incomplete

The [source review ledger](SOURCE_REVIEW_LEDGER.md) records exact file/page/line coverage and each local limitation. Full review of the supplied artifact is complete even when a source itself crops a sentence, omits a video or makes an unsupported claim. The following content cannot be reconstructed honestly:

- Jumbo's supplied tweet archive includes 66 retrieved posts across 48 pages, not the author's complete work. Five referenced images and videos are absent; page 29 has partly ambiguous table digits. Manuals/summary discrepancies are recorded against originals.
- Historical conversations sometimes contain attachment descriptions or tool-call placeholders without original pixels/results; the raw log's Part A omits earlier assistant replies. The added DRF transcript is fully accounted for, but old excluded archive claims are not adopted or independently verified.
- GEX framework source images on pages 7/13/15 are cropped; page 16 is degraded with largely unreadable quantitative axes/labels. The standalone gamma screenshot has partially obscured lower table content and no published formula/gray-line legend or complete initialization times.
- TPO lesson page 5 ends with a clipped sentence. Average Unprofitable Trader page 18 has a masked right sentence and page 26 clipped bottom marks. Recovered original images/text resolved other crops where possible; no missing words or numbers were invented.
- Numerous discretionary PDFs omit original videos, exact footprint/profile/size settings, full fills/costs/account ledgers, or private KG1/trailing/refill formulas. Pending orders, SIM frames, edited brackets and selected payout panels cannot establish realized trade outcomes or repeatable edge. Their observable sequences and contradictions remain in the findings/Response registry.
- Pine members `hourly_stats_levels.txt` (PIN080) and `magic_hours.txt` (PIN081) end in incomplete code in the supplied files. All supplied lines were read; missing tails were not invented or executed. Other Pine numerical/clock/lookahead/reset/display-coupling defects are documented as benchmark limitations and causal-variant tests.
- Additional primary research was focused on methods actually used in the plan. The original Garman–Klass full text was not successfully retrieved; the original citation/abstract-level support is distinguished from full-text reads. Skylit review accounts for all 96 indexed pages: 41 full narrative/text reads and 55 focused generated-API semantic reviews, plus all 87 retrieved images. It does not claim every repeated generated schema example/boilerplate was read verbatim.

These limitations do not license filling gaps with confident narratives. Unresolved definitions become explicit competing variants, dependency-gated components or justified exclusions. The source matrix retains the original claim, correction and validation route.
