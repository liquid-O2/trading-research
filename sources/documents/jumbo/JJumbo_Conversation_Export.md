### Assistant (full)

Short answer: you can infer **absorption and reload at the touch** from MBP-1. You cannot infer the full DOM story of pull/stack **behind** the market. The model should **not** treat pull+stack as a required “now reverse” switch the way a discretionary DOM trader does.

#### What they were actually teaching

1. **Absorption** — aggressive size hits a price and the price does not go
2. **Stacking** — extra limit size appears on the defending side
3. **Pulling** — size on the attacking side vanishes without trading

The manual rule is: absorption alone is cheap; take the reversal when absorption is followed by defend-stack + opposite-pull. That is a timing trick for someone who has no statistical prior on *which* level should matter.

Your stack already has that prior: clock box, leftover extreme, VP node, regime. That is why flow-only AUC sat near 0.54.

#### What MBP-1 can and cannot see

**At the touch you can measure:** absorbed at this price; they reloaded; they pulled the touch; effort vs result; control flipped.

**You cannot measure without depth:** stack building 3–8 ticks under the low while the offer is still being eaten; ask pulling ahead of the high before first touch; a wall that is not yet BBO.

#### Does the model need it?

**Not as a gate.** Use it as a **soft score on an already-legal level.**

If you require pull+stack the way you were taught, you will miss clean clock reversals, get chopped in expand regimes, and fit a 2024 DOM look that dies when refresh rules change.

Define absorption for the model as **result at a pre-chosen place**:

```
candidate level from gate (clock / leftover / node)
+ first tag in the window
+ price fails to traverse by X ticks within T
+ (optional) BBO reload or aggressor delta fades
+ regime is mean-revert, not jump/expand
```

The failure to continue *is* the absorption.

#### Practical MBP-1 features at first tag

- `absorbed`
- `reload`
- `pull_at_touch`
- `delta_fade`
- `rewarded_side`

Put those in the grader next to memory + location + regime. Look at **incremental** AUC / PF. If pull/reload add nothing after absorbed + location, drop them.

If after that test you still lose on “the offer never pulled two ticks up,” *then* one year of MBP-10 is justified.

Until that test fails, do not block reversals on a tell you were taught because the human had no clock. The clock *is* the confirmation that the DOM was standing in for.

### Assistant (full)

That result is the whole point. The $3k/day NQ scalp model and your “every extreme + order flow” model are not the same job. Mixing them is how people stay unprofitable with better features.

#### Why his L2 scalp can work and yours did not

His model answers a **short, local** question: in the next few seconds to a couple of minutes, does this *current* book + tape resolve up or down?

At that horizon, pull/stack, BBO reload, cancel bursts, and depth imbalance *are* the event. Location is almost “wherever price is.”

Your generator answered a **different** question with the **wrong** features: of every swing high/low I can draw, which ones reverse for a 30–90 minute hold, using only tape/DOM?

That fails because:

- Extremes are cheap. Most are not session business.
- Order flow at a random extreme is close to a coin flip on a 30–90 minute label (the 0.54 AUC again).
- You asked OF to do **selection**. OF is bad at selection at your hold time. Clocks, leftover edges, profile nodes, and regime are the selection. OF is a **quality/timing** score on a short list.

You did not fail at order flow. You failed at **candidate definition**. He never scored “all extremes.”

#### Split models by job

| Expert | When it runs | Input | Label | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Regime** | Once pre-open | GK/YZ, overnight vs RTH vol, jumps, profile shape, calendar, ES/YM | AM state: revert / expand / news / dead | Which other experts are allowed |
| **Range gate** | Once pre-open | 6–8 named clocks + geometry | Which box paid fade vs single-break that session | 1–2 live boxes, or stand down |
| **Level grader** | At first tag of a live box/node | Memory, overlap, leftover, regime, *then* MBP-1 absorb/reload/delta-fade | Hold vs traverse + path MAE/MFE | `p_hold`, take / skip |
| **Manage** | After entry | Protected print, mid/open, −0.5 / 1.33 | Did trail rules beat fixed BE / re-entry | Rules, not a fourth net |
| **Scalp OF (optional)** | Only if you want *his* game | L2 / MBP-10, seconds–minutes | Next N seconds | Do not share capital or labels with the swing book |

The mixture is the **gate**, not a softmax over 40 features in one GBM.

#### How to use OF without repeating the dead model

OF only sees a row if the gate said this price is a candidate **today**.

```
if regime == dead: flat
if regime == expand: only single-break expert, ignore fade-OF
if regime == revert:
    boxes = top clock(s)
    on first tag of box/node:
        p = grader(location, memory, absorb, reload, delta_fade)
        trade only if p ≥ threshold calibrated on walk-forward
```

Diagnostic: train the grader twice.

1. Location + memory + regime only
2. Same + MBP-1 OF

If (2) does not beat (1) on purged walk-forward, the scalp features do not belong in this book. That is the expected outcome for 30–90 minute holds.

Treat the $3k/day guy as proof that **L2 + a short label can be a business**, not as proof that L2 will rescue a long-hold candidate generator. If you want that product, it is a second system.
